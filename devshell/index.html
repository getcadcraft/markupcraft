                    MarkupCraft settings

                    These settings are only for MarkupCraft, and you can change them only here, in the app.

                    Your name

                    Goes on every markup and reply you make, and into the PDFs you save. Reset leaves your name as it is.

                    New markups

                    For new pens, lines, arrows, shapes, clouds, callouts and text. Highlighters, notes, stamps and measurements keep their own colors. A color you pick for one tool in its panel wins over this.

                    Paste

                    At the pointer: with the pointer on the sheet, Ctrl+V puts the copy where it points. Beside the copy: pastes a little down and to the right of the original. The Paste button and Ctrl+Shift+V (paste in place) work the same either way.

                    Measurements

                    “Show lengths in” is what the sheet scale dialog offers first when you calibrate from two points; sheets you already calibrated keep their unit. The position on the sheet is the paper position shown at the bottom left.

                    Theme

                    The theme applies to every CadCraft app and the site.

                    Language

                    The language applies to every CadCraft app. The app reloads to change it.

                    Screen

                    Browsers allow full screen only after a click or key press, so it starts on your first click in the drawing.

                    Toolbar

                    Arrange: drag tools within their group, and drag a group by its name.

                    Panels

                    You can also drag a panel's inner edge. Double-click the edge to put it back.

                    Panels

                    Drag a panel by its name to dock it left, right or at the bottom, or drop it over the drawing to float it. A new window can go on your other screen. Reset layout puts every panel back where it started.

                    Add-ons

                      Add-ons add features to MarkupCraft. Switching one off takes effect at once; some need a reload to go completely.

                      Developer mode is on: add-ons that are not signed can load. Only use add-ons you trust.

                      Panel widths apply when the panels sit beside the drawing (wider windows).
