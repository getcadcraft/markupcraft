Every markup records who made it and when. Stamps print the name ("By Ana Ruiz, Oct 10, 2026"), replies show it, the markups list can filter by it, and other PDF viewers show it as the comment's author.

## Signed in: your account's name

If you are signed in to your CadCraft account, MarkupCraft uses your account's name and never asks. Markups, replies, stamps and the status bar show it. In {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:MarkupCraft}} › {{ui:Your name}} it reads "From your account (your name)".

- To use another name, choose {{ui:Use another name}} and type it. A name you type wins from then on.
- To go back to your account's name, choose {{ui:Use my account name}}.

Only the name is used, never your email address. If you change your name on the site, MarkupCraft shows it the next time it opens.

## The first time (signed out)

When you are not signed in, the first time you pick a drawing tool, a box called "Your name on markups" opens.

1. Type your name the way you want reviewers to see it (up to 80 characters).
2. Choose {{ui:Save}}.

Result: your name appears at the right end of the status bar, and every new markup carries it.

If you choose {{ui:Not now}}, markups are made with no author ("Not recorded" in {{ui:Properties}}) and stamps show only the date. MarkupCraft asks again the next time you open it.

## Change it

1. Click your name (or "Set your name") at the right end of the status bar.
2. Type the new name and choose {{ui:Save}}.

Or open {{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}), stay on the {{ui:MarkupCraft}} tab and type it under {{ui:Your name}}. See [MarkupCraft settings](topic:settings).

Only markups you make from now on get the new name. Markups already drawn keep the name they were made with; there is no way to change the author of an existing markup.

## Where the name is kept

Your name is stored **in this browser, on this computer** (the browser's local storage for this website). That means:

- Another browser on the same computer, or the same browser on another computer, asks again.
- A private or incognito window asks every time, and forgets when it closes.
- Clearing this site's data (cookies and site data) in the browser's settings forgets it.
- Nothing is sent anywhere. The name only leaves this computer inside the PDFs you save.

:::safari
Safari may clear a website's stored data if you have not visited it for a while. If MarkupCraft asks for your name again, that is why.
:::

If several people share one computer and one browser login, each should check the name in the status bar before starting, since it is shared.

See also [What stays in this browser](topic:browser-storage).
