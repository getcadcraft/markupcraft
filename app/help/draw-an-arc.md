The {{ui:Arc}} tool ({{key:O}}) draws a circular arc. Besides the usual three clicks, it offers ten ways to define the arc, for when you know the radius, the angle or the centre rather than a point on the curve.

## What you'll do

Pick the {{ui:Arc}} tool, choose an {{ui:Arc method}} and draw the arc with clicks or typed numbers.

## Steps

1. Choose {{ui:Arc}} in the {{ui:Draw}} tools, or press {{key:O}}.
2. In the box over the page, pick the method under {{ui:Arc method}}: {{ui:3-Point}}, {{ui:Start, Center, End}}, {{ui:Start, Center, Angle}}, {{ui:Start, Center, Length}}, {{ui:Start, End, Angle}}, {{ui:Start, End, Direction}}, {{ui:Start, End, Radius}}, {{ui:Center, Start, End}}, {{ui:Center, Start, Angle}} or {{ui:Center, Start, Length}}.
3. Follow the prompt in the status line, one step at a time. Each step asks you to click the start, a point the arc passes through, the centre or the end.
4. When the step asks for an angle, a chord length, a direction or a radius, move the pointer and click, or type the number and press {{key:Enter}}.
5. The arc is made when the last step is done. Select it and restyle it in {{ui:Properties}} like any other markup.

## Good to know

- {{key:Esc}} cancels the arc; {{key:Backspace}} undoes the last step.
- A minus sign turns an angle or a length clockwise. For a radius, a minus sign takes the long way round. A direction is in degrees: right is 0, up is 90.
- If the points cannot make an arc (three points in a line, two points in the same place, a radius too small to reach the other end) the status line says so; pick another point.
- See [Markup types](topic:markup-types) for the other drawing tools.
