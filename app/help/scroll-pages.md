MarkupCraft shows a PDF in two ways. Switch between them with {{ui:Scroll pages}} on the sheet bar, under the sheet.

## Scroll pages

The pages of the PDF are one under the other, like a document.

- The mouse wheel scrolls through them. {{key:Shift}} with the wheel scrolls sideways. {{key:Ctrl}} with the wheel zooms, and so does a pinch on a trackpad.
- The page in the middle of the screen is the open sheet: the sheet bar, {{ui:Properties}}, the sheets list and the markups list follow it as you scroll.
- Click or start drawing on any page you see, and you mark up that page. Markups on the other pages show as you scroll.
- {{key:PageDown}} and {{key:PageUp}} scroll to the next and previous page.
- In a workspace layout with tabs, each PDF gets one tab, and its pages scroll inside it.

## One page at a time

Only the open page is on screen. {{key:PageDown}}, {{key:PageUp}} and the sheets list go from page to page, and each page keeps its own zoom. In a workspace layout with tabs, each page you open gets a tab.

## Good to know

- MarkupCraft remembers your choice in this browser.
- With several PDFs open, Scroll pages shows the pages of one PDF at a time: the one the open sheet belongs to. Pick a sheet of another PDF (or its tab) to scroll through that one.
- Comparing two sheets always shows one page at a time; Scroll pages comes back when you stop comparing.
- Turning the view (the turn buttons on the sheet bar) turns every page in the column.
