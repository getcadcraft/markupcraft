I-MarkupCraft yokubuyekeza imidwebo: uvula amasethi amapulani e-PDF, uwamaka ngamafu, izichazi, umbhalo, izitembu nezilinganiso, ulandele isimo sekhomenti ngayinye, bese ulondoloza ikhophi ye-PDF ezobonakala kunoma yisiphi isibukeli se-PDF.

Isebenza ku-webhu sakho sesiphequluli. Ama-PDF akho avulwa kukhompyutha yakho futhi **awalayishwa ngaleso sikhathi**. Ukulondoloza kulanda ikhophi entsha; ifayela lakho lokuqala alishintshwa.

:::note
I-MarkupCraft isathuthukiswa. Usizo lichaza ibhilidi oyisebenzisayo. Izinto ezingakenziwa okwamanje zidwetshwe ku-[Okungakakhiwa okwamanje](topic:not-built-yet).
:::

## Okudingekayo

- I-Chrome, i-Edge, i-Firefox noma i-Safari yakamuva ku-Mac, Windows noma Linux, noma i-Safari noma i-Chrome ku-iPad. Bona [Iziphequluli nekhompyutha](common:browsers).
- Ama-PDF amodwa noma amaningi. Isethi yonke yamapulani ingaba i-PDF eyodwa noma eminingi.
- Imawusi enevili yokuskrola iyona ekhululekile kakhulu. I-trackpad iyasebenza, njalo nesikrini sokuthinta (bona [Ukubuyekeza ku-iPad noma kwisikrini sokuthinta](topic:review-on-a-tablet)).

## Ukubuyekeza kokuqala ngezinyathelo eziyishumi

1. Vula i-MarkupCraft kusesiphequluli sakho. Maphakathi nesikrini kuthi "Vula isethi yamapulani".
2. Khetha {{ui:Open PDFs}} (noma {{ui:Open}} ngakwesobunxele senhlanganisela yamathuluzi, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}), khetha i-PDF eyodwa noma eminingi, bese uqinisekisa.
   Ungaphinda uhudulele ama-PDF ku-{{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=umphathi wamafayela wakho}} bese uwawisela endaweni yamashidi.
   Okokuqala, ibha yesimo ngaphansi ithi "Kulayishwa isifundi se-PDF…". Bese ikhasi ngalinye liba yishidi ohlwini {{ui:Sheets}} ngakwesobunxele, nishidi lokuqala ligcwalise maphakathi.
3. Chofoza ishidi ohlwini ukuze ulibonise. Phendula ivili lembewu phezu kweshidi ukuze ukhulise lapho insika ikhomba khona.
4. Khetha {{ui:Cloud}} eqenjini {{ui:Revise}} lenhlanganisela yamathuluzi (noma cindezela {{key:C}}).
   Okokuqala lapho ukhetha ithuluzi lokudweba, i-MarkupCraft ikucela igama lakho. Libhale bese ukhetha {{ui:Save}}. Yonke imakwe iligcina. (Okwengeziwe ku-[Setha igama lakho lombhali](topic:set-your-name).)
5. Dweba ibhokisi ozungeze indawo ofuna ukuyiphawula. Ifu elibomvu lokubuyekeza liyavela futhi likhethiwe.
6. Khetha {{ui:Callout}} eqenjini {{ui:Annotate}} (noma cindezela {{key:K}}). Cindezela into oyikhombayo, hudula lapho umbhalo kufanele ufike, bese uyashiya.
7. Bhala ikhomenti yakho ebhokisini elivelayo. Chofoza noma yikuphi ngaphandle kwebhokisi (noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}}) ukuqeda.
8. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} kabili: kanye ukuze ususe ukukhethwa, kanye ukubuyela ku-{{ui:Select}}. Chofoza ifu. Ithebhu {{ui:Properties}} ngakwesokudla iboniswa ukuthi ubani wayenzile, nini, nesimo sayo {{ui:Status}}.
9. Khetha {{ui:Save}} kwibha yamathuluzi (noma cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}).
   Isiphequluli sakho silanda ikhophi ebizwa njengokuthi "A-Plans (marked up).pdf". Ibha yesimo ibonisa ukuthi izimakwe ezingaki zibhalwe.
10. Vula leyo kopi kunoma yisiphi isibukeli se-PDF ukuhlola: ifu nesichazi kukhona, njengezimvo ezijwayelekile ze-PDF negama lakho kuzo.

Ukuze uqhubeke kamuva, vula ikhophi "(marked up)" ku-MarkupCraft. Izimakwe zakho ziyabuya zihlelekayo, nezimo zazo, izimpendulo nezinombolo zokulungisa.

## Ku-inthanethi nangaphandle kwayo

:::online
Uxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi. I-MarkupCraft ilayisha isifundi se-PDF ngokuqala ukuvula i-PDF, nombhali we-PDF ngokuqala ukulondoloza. Ukuze usebenze ngaphandle kokuxhumeka kamuva (ekuvakasheni isiteshi, endizeni), khetha {{ui:Save for offline}} ebhalweni eliphezulu manje bese ulinda "Ilungele ukusebenza ngaphandle". Ikhophi ifaka isifundi nombhali, ngakho ukuvula nokulondoloza ama-PDF kusebenza ngaphandle. Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle](common:offline).
:::

:::offline
Awuxhunyiwe. Uma ibha ephezulu ithi "Ilungele ukusebenza ngaphandle", i-MarkupCraft yagcinwa ukuze isetshenziswe ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kulesi siphequluli futhi konke kuyasebenza: ukuvula, ukumaka, ukulinganisa, ukulondoloza. Uma kungenjalo, sebenzisa okulayishiwe kalelithebhu futhi ungabuyisi ikhasi kuze ubuyele ku-inthanethi. Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle](common:offline).
:::

## Okugcinwa uhlelo nokungagcinwa

- **Izimakwe zakho zihlala kwi-PDF oyilondolozayo.** Akukho okugcinwa ku-server. Uhlelo alulondolozi i-PDF ngokwalo, kodwa ligcina ikhophi yokubuyisa kulesi siphequluli kuze ulondoloze. Londoloza ngaphambi kokuvala ithebhu; isiphequluli sibuza kuqala uma uzama ukuphuma nezimakwe ezingalondolozwanga (ngaphandle kwe-iPad, bona [Ukubuyekeza ku-iPad noma kwisikrini sokuthinta](topic:review-on-a-tablet)).
- **Igama lakho, izitayela zakho zamathuluzi nesikhwama sakho samathuluzi** zihlala kulesi siphequluli kule khompyutha. Isiphequluli esihlukile noma ikhompyutha ehlukile iqala kusha, futhi ukucisha idatha yesayithi yesiphequluli kuyasusa. Bona [Okugcinwa kulesi siphequluli](topic:browser-storage).
- Ukulondoloza ku-ifu nezikhathi zokubuyekeza ezabelwana phakathi kwabantu akukatholakali okwamanje.
