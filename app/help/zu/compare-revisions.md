Qhathanisa kubeka amashidi amabili phezu komunye nomunye. Imigqa etholakala eshidini elidala kuphela ibonakala ngomugqa obomvu, imigqa etholakala eshidini elisha kuphela ngeluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, kanti imigqa etholakala kokubili ngegrey ethulile. Imibala ichazwa encazelweni engaphansi kweshidi: "Eshidini elidala kuphela", "Eshidini elisha kuphela", "Kokubili".

## Qhathanisa amashidi amabili

1. Vula zombili izinkinga. Zingaba amakhasi e-PDF efanayo noma ama-PDF ahlukene; zivule ndawonye njengesethi (bona [Sebenza ngamafayela amaningi ngesikhathi esisodwa](topic:several-files)).
2. Khetha ithebhu {{ui:Qhathanisa}} ngakwesokudla.
3. Khetha {{ui:Ishidi elidala}} ne-{{ui:Ishidi elisha}}. Ishidi elisha liqala njengelona oseliku. Uma kuvulwe amafayela amaningi, igama lefayela leshidi ngalinye likhonjiswa ngemuva kwegama lalo.
4. Khetha {{ui:Qhathanisa}}.

Umphumela: i-MarkupCraft ikhombisa ishidi elisha ixutshwe nelidala, incazelo iyavela, nethebhu ithi "Iqhathanisa <old> (elidala) no <new> (elisha). Izimakiwe eshidini elisha zihlala zihleleka."

## Ukufiphala phakathi kwendala nensha

Donsa isilayida {{ui:Okudala nokusha}}. Ngakwesobunxele kuze kube sekugcineni kubonisa ishidi elidala kuphela, ngakwesokudla kuze kube sekugcineni elisha kuphela, phakathi kubonisa kokubili. Ukudonsa phambili nasemuva kuvame ukuba indlela esheshayo yokubona ushintsho oluncane.

## Hlanganisa amashidi

Uma izinkinga ezimbili zadwetshwa ngomkhawulo, ubukhulu noma ukujikeleza okuhlukile, konke kuzobukeka kushintshile. Lungisa ngalokhu:

**Ngamaphoyinti amabili (kuhle kakhulu):**

1. Khetha {{ui:Linganisa ngamaphoyinti amabili}}. Ukubuka kubonisa ishidi elidala kuphela.
2. Chofoza isici esibukhali ongasithola kumashidi womabili, njengokuhlangana kwegridi yekholomu. Ithebhu ibala izinyathelo ("1 of 4").
3. Chofoza isici sesibili, kude nesokuqala, njengekhoni elingaphambili legridi.
4. Ukubuka kushintshela eshidini elisha. Chofoza isici sokuqala futhi.
5. Chofoza isici sesibili futhi.

Umphumela: "Ihlanganisiwe: ishidi elidala lilinganiswe ngo-100.00%, lijikelezwe ngo-0.00°. Hlanganisa kancane ukuze ulungise kahle." Ishidi elidala liyanyakaziswa, lilinganiswe futhi lijikelezwe ukuze lilingane. {{key:Esc}} ikhansela ngenkathi ukhetha. Sondeza kusici ngasinye ngaphambi kokuchofoza ukuze uthole ukulingana okunembile.

**Ngokuhlanganisa kancane:** sebenzisa izinkinobho ezine zomcibisholo ngaphansi kwe-"Hlanganisa" ukuze uhambise ishidi elidala. Khetha isinyathelo (0.25, 1, 5 noma 20 pt; 72 pt kuyinshi eyodwa ephepheni). Ithebhu ikhomba ukuthi ihambe kangakanani.

{{ui:Setha kabusha}} isusa ukuhlanganiswa nokuhlanganisa kancane.

## Imigqa eshintshiwe kuphela

Ngaphansi kwezinkinobho zokuhlanganisa, khetha {{ui:Imigqa eshintshiwe}} esikhundleni se-{{ui:Isithombe esembozwe phezu}}. Ngemva kwalokho i-MarkupCraft iqhathanisa imigqa yamashidi womabili, hhayi izithombe zawo: ishidi elisha libonakala njengoba likhona, nemigqa etholakala eshidini elidala kuphela ibomvu, nemigqa etholakala eshidini elisha kuphela iluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka. Umugqa oyifushane kuphela ubonisa ingxenye ewelayo. Qala ngokuhlanganisa amashidi (bona ngenhla): ukuqhathanisa kusebenzisa ukuhlanganiswa, ngakho ishidi elihanjiswe kancane liyakhombisa ukuthi azikho izinguquko. Umugqa wesimo ukhombisa ukuthi kushintshe kangakanani.

Lokhu kusebenza kuma-PDF adwetshwe kusuka ku-CAD, lapho umdwebo wakhiwe ngemigqa. Amashidi askeniwe awanayo imigqa yokuqhathanisa: sebenzisa i-overlay yesithombe kuwo. Umbhalo osethwe ngefonti (ongadwetshwanga njengezibambo) awuqhathaniswa.

## Makela izinguquko

Uma uqhathanisa, usemshiyeni eshidini elisha futhi wonke amathuluzi ayasebenza. Dweba amafu azungeze izinguquko njengoba uzithola; zingeshidi elisha futhi zihlala lapho uyeka ukuqhathanisa.

{{ui:Embethe izinguquko ngamafu}} ikwenzela wena: iqoqa imigqa eshintshiwe ehlezi iseduze omunye nomunye futhi idwebe ifu lokubuyekeza lizungeze ngamunye weqembu eshidini elisha, ngo-"Eshintshiwe kusukela ku-<old sheet>" njengamazwana alo. Kanye {{key:Mod+Z}} iyawasusa wonke; susa angadingeki.

## Qhathanisa isethi yonke

Uma inkinga entsha ifika njenge-PDF yayo, vula ama-PDF womabili bese uvula {{ui:Qhathanisa amasethi amabili}} ku-ithebhu {{ui:Qhathanisa}}. Khetha ifayela elidala nelisha, bese ukhetha {{ui:Qhathanisa amasethi}}. Amashidi aphawulwa ngenombolo yawo (njengoba kuchazwe ku-[ukugoqa amashidi](topic:slip-sheet-a-revised-set)), aqhathaniswe ngemigqa yawo, futhi abhalwe kuhlu lowashintsha kakhulu uqala. Khetha ishidi ohlwini ukuqhathanisa lowo mabili nge-{{ui:Imigqa eshintshiwe}}.

{{ui:Embetha izinguquko zonke ngamafu}} izungezela wonke amaqembu emigqa eshintshiwe kuwo wonke amashidi ashintshiwe, ngesinyathelo esisodwa ongasihlehlisa. Ukuqhathanisa kwesethi akuhlanganisi amashidi kuqala, ngakho ishidi elihanjiswe ekhasini liyabonakala njengeshintshiwe: qhathanisa lowo mabili wodwa usebenzisa {{ui:Linganisa ngamaphoyinti amabili}}.

## Yeka ukuqhathanisa

Khetha {{ui:Yeka ukuqhathanisa}}, noma uye kwelinye ishidi. Ukuhlanganiswa kukhunjulwa kulo mabili afanayo kuze kube yilapho uvala isethi.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ukuqhathanisa kusebenza kokudwetshiwe, ipikseli ngepikseli. Alazi ukuthi umugqa ushintshile; ukhombisa indawo endala ngombala owodwa nendawo entsha ngowesibili.
- Umbhalo ogcwalisiwe kabusha, usuku lwebhulokhi lesihloko oluguqukile noma isitayela sokudweba esihlukile nakho kuvela njengezinguquko.
- Ishidi eskenwe liqhathaniswa nelidwetshiwe lisindwa: ukuskena kuyadilika futhi kuhambise imigqa.
- Ukuqhathanisa umbhalo obhalwe ngefonti kanye nohlu lwalokho okushintshile akuke kwakhiwa okwamanje.
