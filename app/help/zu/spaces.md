Isikhala yindawo equkethe igama esheshini: igumbi, ingxenye, noma isigaba somsebenzi. Yonke okumakiwe ngaphakathi kuyo iyayo, ngakho ungabhala ohlwini, uhlunge futhi uthumele okumakiwe egumbini elilodwa.

## Dala isikhala

1. Vula {{ui:Izikhala}}, phezulu kuphaneli {{ui:Amashidi}}, bese ukhetha {{ui:Isikhala esisha}}.
2. Chofoza amakhona alo mkhakha, bese uchofoza kabili noma ucindezele {{key:Enter}}.
3. Bhala igama lawo, njengo `Ikhishi` noma `Izinga 2 empumalanga`, bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza isikhala}}.

Isikhala sibonakala njengendawo eluhlaza okhanyayo enomugqa ophukile negama layo. Ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi okumakiwe angaki kuso.

## Okusesikhaleni

Okumakiwe kungaphakathi esikhaleni esibamba phakathi kwawo. Lapho izikhala zingenelana (igumbi ngaphakathi kwengxenye), esincane liyanqoba. Hambisa okumakiwe bese kuyashintsha isikhala. Khetha isikhala ukushintsha umugqa waso ngamanqamu aso, noma igama laso kuphaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}}, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Igama}}.

- {{ui:Izici}} ibonisa isikhala somakhi.
- Uhlu lwe-{{ui:Okumakiwe}} luhlunga ngu-{{ui:Isikhala}}, kuhlanganise {{ui:Akukho sikhala}}.
- Ukuthumela kwe-CSV ne-XLSX kuthola ikholomu {{ui:Isikhala}} uma isethi inezikhala.
- Iphaneli {{ui:Izikhala}} ibhala zonke izikhala nokuthi zinomakiwe angaki kuzo; chofoza esinye ukuze uyifike.

## Ku-PDF elondoloziwe

Isikhala yikhomenti yepholigoni enegama layo njengombhalo wayo, edwetshwe ngomugqa ophukile, ukuze nabanye ababoni be-PDF bakubone futhi. Ivulwa futhi ku-MarkupCraft njengesikhala.
