Ixhumanisi leshidi likuthatha usuka kumaka uye kwelinye ishidi lesethi: usuka kubhamuza lesiqephu uye esiqeshini, usuka kunothi uye eshidini okuliphathelayo.

## Xhuma amaka eshidini

1. Khetha amaka.
2. Ku-{{ui:Izici}}, khetha ishidi ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Xhuma kushidi}}.

Umcibisholo omncane uvela ngasekugcineni kwesokudla kwamaka. Ukuze uwulandele, bamba {{key:Mod}} uchofoze leli maka, noma khetha ukuwukhetha bese ukhetha {{ui:Hamba ku…}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}. {{key:Alt+Left}} ibuyisela eshidini owavela kulo.

Ixhumanisi libiza ishidi ngenombolo yalo, ngakho lisebenza ngisho nakuvula isethi kabusha noma nakufaka ukushicilelwa okusha: livula ishidi elisha kakhulu elinalena nombolo. Uma leyo shidi lingavulwanga, ibha yesimo iyasho lokho.

## Xhuma zonke izimakhalekhukhwini zeshidi ngesikhathi esisodwa

Khetha {{ui:Amakhasi}} › {{ui:Amaxhumanisi ngebhetshi…}}, noma {{ui:Xhuma izimakhalekhukhwini zeshidi…}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Thola umbhalo}} phezulu kwepaneli lika-{{ui:Amashidi}}.

1. **Hlola izinombolo zeshidi.** I-MarkupCraft ibala wonke amashidi avulekile ngenombolo negama elifundwe kwibhulokhi yesihloko yeshidi (noma ilebula lekhasi), nokuthi avela ephi. Lungisa angalungile, bese ubhala inombolo lapho engayitholi khona (ibhulokhi yesihloko lapho inombolo ihlaziywe ngemigqa akukho mbhalo okufundeka kuyo). Bese ukhetha {{ui:Thola izimakhalekhukhwini}}. Izilungiso zakho zingesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, futhi ziba amalebula amakhasi kwi-PDF elondoliwe.
2. **Hlola uhlu.** I-MarkupCraft ifunda umbhalo wawo wonke amashidi ifune izimakhalekhukhwini ezifana no-"3/A-501", "5 / A501", "DETAIL 2 ON A-501", ibhamuza elinelungu elithi "3" phezu kwe-"A-501", noma i-"A-501" elula kunothi, futhi ifunda amagama emibono yeshidi ngayinye (inombolo yesiqephu nenombolo yeshidi ngaphansi kwayo, noma inombolo enkulu negama ngasohlangothini). Uhlu lunezingxenye ezintathu:
   - {{ui:Izoxhunyiwe}}: isimakhalekhukhwini ngasinye, ishidi elisiya kulo, nombono walo eshidini. Susa ukukhethwa kwanoma yiliphi ongalidingi. Uma umbono ungatholakali (isimakhalekhukhwini sombono 7 eshidini elinemibono 1 kuya kwe-6), khetha umbono ohlwini oluseceleni, noma shiya {{ui:Ishidi lonke}}.
   - {{ui:Akuxhunyiwe}}: izinombolo zamashidi ezingekho eshidini elivulekile elilodwa, nokuthi izimakhalekhukhwini zingaki ezizikhomba. Khetha ishidi elisho ngalinye (isibonelo ishidi elaqanjwa kabusha), futhi wonke amasimakhalekhukhwini ayoxhumela kulo. Noma shiya lingaxhunyiwe.
   - Izimakhalekhukhwini ezingaka sezixhunyiwe futhi sezisiya endaweni efanele. Ziyashiywa njengoba zinjalo.
3. Khetha {{ui:Dala izixhumanisi}}.

Isimakhalekhukhwini ngasinye sithola ibhokisi elincane eliluhlaza elingasile elingusixhumanisi weshidi. {{key:Mod}}+ukuchofoza kulo kuvula ishidi lisondezelwe kumbono. Ukucindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} kukodwa kususa konke.

Izinhlamvu ezibonakala njengezinombolo zeshidi ezifana no-R-19 noma W12X26 zishiywa ngaphandle: isimakhalekhukhwini kumelwe sifane nezinombolo zeshidi zesethi. Inombolo yeshidi lalo, ebhulokhini yesihloko nasemagameni emibono yalo, ayikaze ibe yisimakhalekhukhwini. Amashidi askeniwe awanamagama okufunda: sebenzisa {{ui:Funda umbhalo (OCR)…}} kuqala, noma xhuma izimakhalekhukhwini zawo ngayinye.

## Yenza kabusha uma isethi ishintsha

Amaxhumanisi ngebhetshi angasetshenziswa njalo lapho kudingeka:

- **Kungezwe ishidi**, njengesengezo esine-A-601: vula ifayela lalo bese usebenzisa futhi Amaxhumanisi ngebhetshi. Izimakhalekhukhwini ezibhekisela ku-A-601 ezingaxhunyiwe ngaphambili manje ziyaxhuma.
- **Ishidi laqanjwa kabusha**: lungisa inombolo yalo kwisinyathelo 1. Amaxhumanisi enziwe ngamaxhumanisi ngebhetshi angasafumani ishidi lawo ahlwini lwe-{{ui:Akuxhunyiwe}}; khetha ishidi kanye bese wonke ayilandela. Amaxhumanisi ahambele kombono omunye ayavuselelwa.
- Amaxhumanisi owawenze ngesandla ku-{{ui:Izici}} awakaze ashintshwe.

## Ku-PDF elondoliwe

Ixhumanisi eshidini kulefayela elifanayo liyabhalwa njengexhumanisi le-PDF, ngakho ezinye izibukeli zihamba lapho ngokuchofoza. Ixhumanisi lapho kukhona umbono livula lowo mbono kwesinye isibukeli (ukugcwaliswa kwe-PDF "fit rectangle"); elingenalo umbono livula ishidi lonke. Amaxhumanisi aya eshidini kwesinye ifayela sesethi asebenza kuphela ku-MarkupCraft.
