I-MarkupCraft igcwalisa iwindi lesiphequluli. Kusuka phezulu kuya ezansi nakwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla:

## Ibha ephezulu kakhulu

Ibha encane ephezu kwakho konke ingeyomndeni we-CadCraft, hhayi ye-MarkupCraft ngokwayo. Inayo:

- igama lohlelo nenguqulo oyisebenzisayo,
- {{ui:About MarkupCraft}}, evula ikhasi lohlelo kuwebhusayithi,
- {{ui:Help}}, evula lolu sizo ephaneli eseceleni (noma cindezela {{key:F1}}),
- {{ui:Save for offline}}, egcina i-MarkupCraft, isibukeli se-PDF nombhali, nalolu sizo kulesi siphequluli ukuze livule ngaphandle kokuxhumeka; bese ithi "Ilungele ukusebenza ngaphandle" (bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle](common:offline)),
- inkinobho yemodi yesikrini ({{ui:Full screen}}), futhi
- {{ui:Settings}} (noma {{key:Mod+,}}), enethebhu ethi {{ui:MarkupCraft}} yezilungiselelo zalolu hlelo ngalo, no-{{ui:General}} wezilungiselelo ezabelwana zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft (bona [Izilungiselelo ze-MarkupCraft](topic:settings)), futhi
- {{ui:Paper}} no-{{ui:Model}}, izitimu ezikhanyayo nezimnyama. Ku-{{ui:Model}} amashidi adwetshwa ngokukhanya phezu kokumnyama; izimakwe zakho zigcina imibala yazo.

Inkinobho yemodi yesikrini, noma {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}}, idlulisa ngemodi ezintathu: ejwayelekile, isikrini esigcwele, nesikrini esigcwele namabha afihliwe. Uma amabha efihliwe, hudula iphoyinta ngomphetho ophezulu wesikrini (noma uthinte lapho) bese ebuyela phezulu. Lesi shotikhi sisebenzisa inkinobho ye-Control kuzo zonke ikhompyutha, kuhlanganise ne-Mac.

:::note
Esikrinini esigcwele, {{key:Esc}} nayo yenza isiphequluli siphume esikrinini esigcwele. Uma ubusho ukukhansela umdwebo kuphela, cindezela futhi {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} ukuze ubuyele esikrinini esigcwele.
:::

## Ibha yamathuluzi

Ngaphansi kwebha ephezulu, amathuluzi ahlelwe futhi abizwa ngamagama:

| Iqembu | Izinkinobho |
|---|---|
| Ifayela | {{ui:Open}}, {{ui:Save}}, {{ui:Close}} |
| Khetha | {{ui:Select}}, {{ui:Pan}} |
| Dweba | {{ui:Pen}}, {{ui:Highlight}}, {{ui:Line}}, {{ui:Arrow}}, {{ui:Rectangle}}, {{ui:Ellipse}}, {{ui:Polyline}}, {{ui:Polygon}} |
| Hlaziya | {{ui:Cloud}}, {{ui:Free cloud}} |
| Ukuchaza | {{ui:Callout}}, {{ui:Text}}, {{ui:Note}}, {{ui:Stamp}} |
| Ukulinganisa | {{ui:Calibrate}}, {{ui:Length}}, {{ui:Area}}, {{ui:Count}} |
| Hlela | {{ui:Undo}}, {{ui:Redo}}, {{ui:Copy}}, {{ui:Paste}}, kanye {{ui:More}}: {{ui:Cut}}, {{ui:Copy}}, {{ui:Paste}}, {{ui:Paste in place}}, {{ui:Duplicate}}, {{ui:Delete}}, {{ui:Select all}}, {{ui:Select none}} |

Ithuluzi osilisebenzisayo libonakala licindezelwe. Mamatheka isikhombisi phezu kwenkinobho ukuze ubone ukhiye wayo (isibonelo "Unxande (R)"); imenyu ye-{{ui:More}} ibala umyalo ngamunye ngokhiye wawo ngasecaleni. Izinkinobho ezingenzi lutho manje zimnyama: {{ui:Copy}} uze ukhethe imakwe, {{ui:Paste}} uze kukopishwe okuthile. Uphawu oluncane ngemva kwe-{{ui:Save}} kusho ukuthi ezinye izimakwe azilondolozwanga. Uhlu oluphelele lwezinkinobho zikhiye lusekhasini [Ikhibhodi nemawusi](topic:keyboard).

Ithuluzi lihlala livulekile ngemva kokudweba, ukuze udwebe amafu amaningi ngokulandelana. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} (kabili uma imakwe entsha isakhethiwe) noma {{key:V}} ukubuyela ku-{{ui:Select}}.

## Uhlu lwamashidi (ngakwesobunxele)

{{ui:Sheets}} lubala wonke amakhasi wawo wonke ama-PDF avuliwe. Umugqa ngamunye ubonisa isithombe esincane seshidi, igama lalo, inombolo yeshidi noma ifayela nekhasi, nenani lezimakwe. Inombolo ngemva kwesihloko iyinani lamashidi.

- Chofoza umugqa ukuze ubonise leyo shidi.
- Thayipha ku-{{ui:Search sheets}} ukuhlunga ngegama leshidi, ngenombolo yeshidi noma ngegama lefayela. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuvula okokuqala okuhambisanayo.

Amagama amashidi avela ku-PDF uma enawo. Bona [Vula isethi yamapulani uhambe phakathi kwamashidi](topic:open-a-plan-set).

## Indawo yeshidi (maphakathi)

Ishidi osisebenzayo. Ngaphambi kokuthi kuvulwe lutho ibonisa "Vula isethi yamapulani" nenkinobho ethi {{ui:Open PDFs}}. Phonsa ama-PDF noma yiphi indawo lapha ukuwavula.

Ngaphansi kweshidi, ibha encane inalezi:

- imicibisholo yeshidi elandelayo nelangaphambili, no "3 of 12: A-101 — Floor plan" (mamatheka phezu kwayo ukubona ifayela nekhasi),
- khulula (zoom out), ibhokisi lokuzoom (thayipha iphesenti bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}; 100% kusayizi wangempela esikrinini esijwayelekile), sondeza (zoom in),
- {{ui:Fit page}} no {{ui:Fit width}}.

Ishidi ngalinye likhumbula isondeza layo kuze kuvulwe isethi.

Uma uqhathanisa amashidi amabili, ikhiye livela lapha: "Only on the old sheet", "Only on the new sheet" kanye "On both". Bona [Qhathanisa izinguqulo ezimbili](topic:compare-revisions).

## Amaphaneli (ngakwesokudla)

Amathebhu amahlanu:

| Ithebhu | Ikhombisa |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Properties}} | Imakwe ekhethiwe: umbhali, izinsuku, izinani ezilinganisiwe, isimo, isihloko, umbhalo, ikhomenti, isitayela, izimpendulo zayo nento yayo yokulungisa. Uma ithuluzi lokudweba livuliwe kodwa lutho alukhethiwe, izilungiselelo izimakwe ezintsha ezizosetshenziswa. Uma lutho alukhethiwe kanti {{ui:Select}} livuliwe, kuboniswa ishidi: inombolo, isihloko, lapho igama lafundelwa khona, usayizi wephepha, isikali, kanye no-{{ui:Rename sheet}}. |
| {{ui:Markups}} | Zonke izimakwe kusethi evulekile, namafiltha, kanye nokuthumela kwe-{{ui:CSV}} ne-{{ui:BCF}}. Bona [Uhlu lwezimakwe](topic:markups-list-and-export). |
| {{ui:Punch list}} | Izinto zokulungisa ezinenombolo, nomuntu obekelwe, usuku lokugcina nesimo. Bona [Yenza uhlu lokulungisa](topic:punch-list). |
| {{ui:Tools}} | Isikhwama samathuluzi: amathuluzi alondoloziwe, anezitayela. Bona [Isikhwama samathuluzi](topic:tool-chest). |
| {{ui:Compare}} | Beka amashidi amabili phezu komunye. Bona [Qhathanisa izinguqulo ezimbili](topic:compare-revisions). |

## Ibha yesimo (ezansi)

- Ngakwesobunxele, lapho iphoyinta ikhomba khona ishidi, ngamaintshi (noma ama-millimeter, kuma-[Izilungiselelo](topic:settings)) ephepheni.
- Maphakathi, imilayezo: okulindelwe yithuluzi elilandelayo, okulondoloziwe, nesizathu sokuthi okuthile kunqatshelwe. **Yifunde lapho okuthile kungenzeki.**
- Inkinobho yesikali seshidi ("Akulinganiswanga", noma isikali). Cofa ukuze uyisethe. Bona [Linganisa ishidi bese ulinganisa](topic:calibrate-and-measure).
- Igama lakho ("Setha igama lakho" kuze unikeze elithile). Cofa ukuze ulishintshe.

## Esikrinini esincane

Ngaphansi kwamapikseli angaba ngu-1180 ububanzi, amaphaneli ancipha nezithombe zamashidi zincipha. Esikrinini sefoni izingxenye zimi zodwa: ibha yamathuluzi (yiskrolele ngakohlangothi), ishidi, ibha yesimo, amaphaneli, bese uhlu lwamashidi ngaphansi. Ithebulethi ibanzi igcina isakhiwo esigcwele.
