Ubude nezindawo kudinga ukwazi isikali seshidi. I-MarkupCraft igcina isikali **ngeshidi ngalinye**: linganisa ishidi ngalinye oqopha kulo. Ukubala akudingi isikali.

Ibha yesimo ikhombisa isikali seshidi lamanje, noma "Akulinganiswanga".

## Khetha isikali kuhlu

Sebenzisa lokhu uma i-PDF isesayizi eliphelele (ishidi lika-24 × 36 elo iphrintwa ku-24 × 36) nomdwebo ubonisa isikali sawo.

1. Chofoza inkinobho yesikali ebhalweni lesimo ("Akulinganiswanga"). Ibhokisi "Isikali sishidi" liyavula.
2. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Isikali}}, khetha isikali: sokwakha (1/32" = 1'-0" kuya ku-3" = 1'-0"), sobunjiniyela (1" = 10' kuya ku-1" = 100') noma semethrikhi (1:1 kuya ku-1:1000).
3. Chofoza {{ui:Setha isikali}}.

Umphumela: ibha yesimo ikhombisa isikali, isibonelo `1/4" = 1'-0"`. Izikali zokwakha zikhombisa ubude ngamafiti namainshi, izikali zobunjiniyela ngamafidi ngedesimali, izikali zemethrikhi kuze kube ku-1:50 ngamamilimitha, kanti ukusuka ku-1:100 ngamamitha.

:::warning
Ukusethwa kwangaphambili kulungile kuphela uma i-PDF ingakancishiswa. Isethi esingxenye-ngxenye, noma ishidi elaba "ilingana nekhasi" lapho lenziwa, lilinganisa kabi ngokusethwa kwangaphambili. Linganisa ngobukhulu obaziwayo kunalokho, futhi hlola ubukhulu obubodwa noma kunjalo.
:::

## Linganisa ngobukhulu obaziwayo

Sebenzisa lokhu kumasethi angxenye-ngxenye, emashidini askenwe, emininingwaneni ezikalini ezingajwayelekile, noma ukuhlola isethwa kwangaphambili.

1. Thola ubukhulu obude othembekile, noma ibha yesikali esibonakalayo.
2. Khetha {{ui:Linganisa}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Linganisa}} (noma khetha {{ui:Khetha amaphoyinti amabili}} ebhokisini "Isikali sishidi").
3. Chofoza ekugcineni kwesinye sobukhulu, bese ekugcineni kwesinye. (Noma hudula kusuka kwesinye isiphetho kuya kwesinye.) Sondeza kuqala ukuze ube nokunemba; {{key:Shift}} ugcina umugqa ngezinyathelo ze-45°.
4. Ibhokisi likutshela ukuthi amaphoyinti akhe kangakanani ephepheni bese libuza "Angakanani empeleni?". Thayipha {{ui:Ibanga langempela}}, isibonelo `24'-0"`, `12' 6 1/2"`, `7.2 m` noma `900 mm`.
5. Khetha indlela ubude obubonakala ngayo ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Bonisa ubude nge-}}: {{ui:Amafiti namainshi}}, {{ui:Amafidi ngedesimali}}, {{ui:Amayintshi}}, {{ui:Mitha}} noma {{ui:Millimitha}}.
6. Chofoza {{ui:Setha isikali}}.

Umphumela: ibha yesimo ikhombisa "Ilinganisiwe: 24'-0" ngaphezu kwe-1.00 in" (ubude bangempela nobude ephepheni), futhi ithuluzi owayezolinganisa ngalo liyabuya. Zonke izindlela zokubhala ubude zisemaqondweni [Amayunithi nobude](topic:units-and-scales).

Ibanga langempela lingaba ukubalwa okuncane futhi: `12'6"+3/4"`, `(24'-0")/3` noma `2*450mm`. Sebenzisa `+ - * /` namabhulaketi; inani elingenayunithi libalwa ngeyunithi ephansi kwe-{{ui:Bonisa ubude nge-}} (amafiti kwe-{{ui:Amafiti namainshi}}). {{ui:Setha isikali}} isebenzisa umphumela.

Uma i-MarkupCraft ingakwazi ukufunda lokho obhale, ibhokisi liyakutshela: 'Lokho akuyona ubude i-MarkupCraft eyaziwayo. Zama 24'-0", 12' 6 1/2", 7.2 m noma 900 mm.' Ukubalwa okungakwazi ukusebenza kuchaza ukuthi kungani nokuthi kuphi, isibonelo "Ibhulaketi alivaliwe (uhlamvu 5)."

## Linganisa ubude

1. Khetha {{ui:Ubude}} ({{key:M}}).
2. Chofoza okokuqala, bese okokugcina. Noma hudula kusuka okokuqala kuya okokugcina. {{key:Shift}} ugcina ngezinyathelo ze-45°.

Umphumela: umugqa wobukhulu onamathikhi nobude obubhalwe kuwo. Ibha yesimo iphinda ikhombise ("Ubude: 12'-6 1/2" (1/4" = 1'-0")."), futhi {{ui:Izici}} ikhombisa ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ubude}}. Amafiti namainshi ajikelezwa kwi-1/16" eseduze kakhulu.

Uma ishidi lingakabi nesikali, ukukhetha {{ui:Ubude}} kushintsha kube {{ui:Linganisa}} futhi ibha yesimo iyachaza: "Linganisa lo shidi kuqala: khetha amaphoyinti amabili ayo ekude ngebanga elaziwayo, noma khetha isikali sawo. Bese uyalinganisa." Emva kokusetha isikali, {{ui:Ubude}} buyabuya.

## Dweba ngesikali

Eshidini elilinganisiwe ungathayipha ubude bangempela uma udweba {{ui:Umugqa}}, {{ui:Utolo}}, {{ui:Ubude}}, {{ui:Iphola-layini}}, {{ui:Ipholigoni}}, {{ui:Ifu elikhululekile}}, {{ui:Indawo}} noma isikhala:

1. Chofoza iphoyinti lokuqala.
2. Phawula indlela isegimenti elilandelayo elihamba ngayo bese uthayipha ubude bayo, njengo `16` (kuyunithi yeshidi), `12'6"` noma `3.5 m`. Ibhokisi liyavula eduze komkhombi njengoba uthayipha.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}}. Iphoyinti liyahlala lokho kude, endleleni yomkhombi.

Ukuze usethe indlela ngokunembile, engeza `<` ne-engeli ngamadigri: `8 < 90` iya ku-8 amafiti ngqo phezulu, `4<0` ngakwesokudla, `10 < 225` ngaphansi nangakwesobunxele, njengoba ishidi libonakala (namakhasi aphendukile nawo). Qhubeka uthayipha amabanga amakhona alandelayo, bese uqeda isakhiwo njengoba ujwayelekile. {{key:Esc}} ivala ibhokisi ngaphandle kokubeka iphoyinti.

## Linganisa i-engeli

1. Khetha {{ui:Ikona}} ({{key:X}}).
2. Chofoza ekugcineni kwesinye isandla, bese ekhoneni, bese ekugcineni kwesinye isandla.

Umphumela: izandla ezimbili, ijika elincane ekhoneni kanye ne-engeli ngamadigri, ngamaxenye eshumi (0° kuya ku-180°). I-engeli ayidingi isikali. {{ui:Izici}} ikhombisa ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ikona}}, futhi uhlu, i-CSV ne-BCF kuyiphethe.

{{ui:Ijika}} ({{key:O}}) eshidini elilinganisiwe liphinde likhombise nobude baso ngaphezulu komjikelezo ku-{{ui:Izici}}.

## Linganisa indawo nomjikelezo wayo

1. Khetha {{ui:Indawo}} ({{key:Q}}).
2. Chofoza wonke amakhona egumbi noma indawo.
3. Qeda ngokuchofoza kabili, {{key:Enter}} noma ngokuchofoza ngesokudla (uthinte kabili kuthinta). {{key:Backspace}} isusa ikhona lokugcina.

Umphumela: umphetho oshadiwe onendawo kanye "P" nomjikelezo phakathi, isibonelo "128.00 sq ft" kanye "P 48'-0"". Izikali zamafiti zinikeza izikwele zamafiti, amainshi izikwele zamainshi, amamitha izikwele zamamitha, amamilimitha izikwele zamamilimitha. Indawo idinga okungenani amakhona amathathu.

### Izikhala ezisikiwe

Ukuze ushiye ikholomu, i-shaft noma i-courtyard, khetha indawo bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza ukusika}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}. Chofoza amakhona esikiwe ngaphakathi endaweni bese uqeda njengendawo. Isikiwe sihlala sivulekile (singashadiwe), indawo yaso iyakhishwa, nemiphetho yaso iyengezwa emjikelezweni: igumbi le-16' × 8' elinesikiwe se-4' × 4' lifunda "112.00 sq ft" kanye "P 64'-0"". Engeza izinhlobo eziningi ozidingayo; azikwazi ukuhlangana omunye nomunye noma ukuwelula umphetho wendawo. {{ui:Susa izikhala ezisikiwe}} zisusa zonke, futhi {{key:Mod+Z}} ihlehlisa eyodwa.

## Bala izinto

1. Khetha {{ui:Bala}} ({{key:U}}).
2. Ku-{{ui:Izici}}, thayipha {{ui:Igama leqembu}}, isibonelo "Imikhwele yomgangatho". (Ihlala ngesikhathi esizayo.)
3. Chofoza yonke into eshidini.

Umphumela: ukuchofoza ngakunye kwengeza ichashazi elinenombolo, ithegi lokuqala linethegi elinjengo "Imikhwele yomgangatho: 7", futhi ibha yesimo igcina inani eliqhubekayo.

- **Qala elinye iqembu:** cindezela {{key:Esc}} kanye (isula ukukhetha), shintsha {{ui:Igama leqembu}}, bese uchofoza futhi.
- **Engeza eqenjini elikhona:** khetha iqembu kuqala, bese ukhetha {{ui:Bala}} uchofoze.
- **Susa isimaka:** ngeqembu elikhethiwe, bamba {{key:Shift}} uchofoze ichashazi. Ukususa ichashazi lokugcina kususa iqembu. Esikrinini sokuthinta ngaphandle kwekhibhodi, sebenzisa {{key:Mod+Z}} noma {{ui:Hlehlisa}} esikhundleni.
- **Qamba kabusha iqembu:** likhethe bese uhlela {{ui:Igama leqembu}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}.

Iqembu lokubala lingeleshidi elilodwa. Izibalo ezishidini eziningi zibonakala njengamaqembu ahlukene ohlwini lwezimakiwe.

## Shintsha noma susa isikali

Chofoza inkinobho yesikali ebhalweni lesimo. Ibhokisi likhombisa isikali samanje; khetha esinye, khetha {{ui:Khetha amaphoyinti amabili}}, noma khetha {{ui:Susa isikali}}.

Ubude nezindawo osezidwebile sezilinganiswa **kabusha** ngesikali esisha ngokushesha. {{ui:Hlehlisa}} ibuyisela isikali esidala.

## Lapho izinombolo ziya khona

- {{ui:Izici}} ikhombisa amanani okulinganisa akhethiwe.
- Ithebhu {{ui:Okumakiwe}} ihlu wonke amanani, nesi-CSV sayo sinekholomu ethi "Measurement". Bona [Uhlu lwezimakiwe](topic:markups-list-and-export).
- I-PDF elondoloziwe igcina isikali ekhasini futhi ibhale ubude nezindawo njengamazwana okulinganisa, ukuze ezinye izinhlelo ze-PDF ezisiqondayo zikwazi ukuwabonisa. Bona [Okulondolozwe ku-PDF](topic:what-the-pdf-keeps).

Uma ufuna ukubalwa okugcwele kwezinto, izakhiwo namanani, sebenzisa i-TakeoffCraft: i-MarkupCraft ilinganisa ukubuyekeza, hhayi ukulinganisela izindleko.
