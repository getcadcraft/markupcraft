Lokhu yiyo okushintshile ku-MarkupCraft 0.9, uma kuqhathaniswa no-0.8.1. Umqondo oyinhloko ukuthi amanothi akho okusebenza angafiki ekhasini eklayenti eliyithola, kanti sonke isethi yemidwebo ingaxhunyelwa ngokudlula kanye.

## Okusha

### Amanothi, ibhodi lokusebenza namanothi asese

- **Ibhodi lokusebenza.** Indawo ezungeze ishidi ngalinye iyidesika: noma yini edwetshwe, ebhalwe noma eqanjwe eceleni kweshidi iyinothi futhi ayisoze iphrintwe. Khetha {{ui:Bonisa ibhodi lokusebenza}} ukuze uzumeze ukuze uthole indawo, noma {{ui:Thatha isithombe sibe ebhodini lokusebenza}}, hudula ibhokisi, bese uKhetha {{ui:Ebhodini lokusebenza}} ukubeka isithombe sengxenye yomdwebo eceleni kweshidi. Isithombe siyisithombe futhi asishintshi uma ishidi lishintsha. Bona [Amanothi, ibhodi lokusebenza namanothi asese](topic:notes-and-pasteboard).
- **Bonisa nokufihla amanothi.** Noma yiliphi ukumaka lingaba inothi: setha {{ui:Izinto ezintsha zokumaka ziba}} ethebhini le-{{ui:Amanothi}}, noma {{ui:Lesi simaki siba}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}. {{key:Shift+H}} noma iswichi ye-{{ui:Amanothi}} ngaphansi kweshidi ifihla futhi ibonise onke amanothi ngesikhathi esisodwa. Amanothi ayekwa ngaphandle kokuphrinta, amakhophi acwebile, izinhlu nokuthunyelwa, futhi abanye abafundi be-PDF abawabonisi. Ifayela lakho le-PDF elilondoloziwe liwagcina ukuze uwabone ngesikhathi esilandelayo uwuvula ku-MarkupCraft.
- **Amanothi asese.** Khetha {{ui:Setha iphasiwedi}}, bese wenza ukumaka kube {{ui:Inothi elingasese}}. Ibethelwe ku-faylela ngephasiwedi eyizinhlamvu ezingu-10 noma ngaphezulu, futhi akukho ukubuyiselwa: akekho, nathi okwalona, ongalivula ngaphandle kwephasiwedi. {{ui:Vala}} ufihla amanothi asese; {{ui:Hlala uvuliwe kule divayisi}} avalwe ngokuzenzakalelayo.
- **Londoloza ikhophi ehlanzekile.** {{ui:Londoloza ikhophi ehlanzekile}} isusa wonke amanothi, amanothi asese nakho konke okusebhodini lokusebenza. Sebenzisa lokho kwifayela eliya eklayenteni.

### Xhuma isethi yonke

- **Amaxhumanisi ebhetshi.** Khetha {{ui:Amakhasi}} › {{ui:Amaxhumanisi ngebhetshi…}}, noma {{ui:Xhuma izimakhalekhukhwini zeshidi…}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Thola umbhalo}} kuphaneli ye-{{ui:Amashidi}}. Isinyathelo 1 sidweba inombolo negama leshidi ngalinye njengoba lifundwa kwibhulokhi yesihloko yalo; lungisa amanye uma angalungile. Isinyathelo 2 sidweba izimakhalekhukhwini ezitholakele, njengo-`3/A-501` noma `DETAIL 2 ON A-501`. I-{{ui:Izoxhuma}} ibonisa ishidi nombono ngakunye, i-{{ui:Akuxhunyiwe}} ibonisa izinombolo ezingekho ndaweni evulekile, futhi i-{{ui:Dala izixhumanisi}} zizenza zonke esinyathelweni sokuhlehlisa esisodwa. Yenza futhi emva kwesengezo noma ukuphinda ubale; amaxhumanisi owenze ngesandla awakaze ashintshwe. Ixhumanisi elinombono lilondolozwa njenge-PDF link, ukuze nabanye abafundi bavule nemininingwane. Bona [Amaxhumanisi eshidi](topic:sheet-links).

### Umdwebo

- **Arc, izindlela eziyishumi.** Ithuluzi le-{{ui:Arc}} ({{key:O}}) manje linebhokisi le-{{ui:Indlela yomnsalo}} phezu kwekhasi, linezindlela eziyishumi zokudweba umnsalo, futhi i-engeli, ubude, i-radius nokuqondisa kungachofozwa noma kubhalwe. Bona [Dweba umnsalo ngezindlela eziyishumi](topic:draw-an-arc).

### Ukusebenza ndawonye

- **Landela umbuyekezi.** Ku-seshini ye-Live khetha {{ui:Landela}} eduze kwegama lomuntu, bese ukubuka kwakho kuhamba nokwakhe; ukudonsa noma ukusondeza ngokwakho kuyakuyeka lokho. Umbambi angakhetha {{ui:Cela wonke umuntu andilandele}}. Uhlu lwabantu lubonisa umbala womuntu ngamunye, igama, ishidi aseliphethe nesikhathi sokuhlela kwakhe kokugcina. Bona [Landela omunye umbuyekezi ku-seshini ye-Live](topic:follow-in-live).

## Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- Amathiphu ayi-Bootstrap (ama-tooltip), ithiphu yokuqala ingavalwa, imigqa yamathebhu iyaskrola uma ichichima, nezihloko zamathebhu ebhrawuzi zifunda `<document> — MarkupCraft | CadCraft`.
- Uphaneli onezigaba eziningi uvula eyodwa aphinde avale ezinye (ukhiye oseduze kwekhefa ugcina eyodwa ivulekile); iswichi yezilungiselelo iyayivala lokhu.
- Uhlelo ngalunye luqala nohlu oluzenzakalelayo lwezinto ze-Library.
- Amayunithi wesisindo nowokushisa atholakala ezilungiselelweni zamayunithi.

## Okulungisiwe

- **Amaki asusiwe awasenakufundeka ngaphakathi kwe-PDF elondoloziwe.** Ukulondoloza ekhale kwakususa isichazi esisusiwe noma esibhalwe kabusha ekhasini lapho, kodwa kushiye into, nombhalo wayo, kwifayela. Manje ukulondoloza kususa lezo zinto. Amafayela alondolozwe yizakhiwo zangaphambilini aseqakathi ezikhona uze awalondoloze futhi.

## Imikhawulo eqinisekisiwe

- Amaxhumanisi ebhetshi ahlolelwe izethi zethu zokuhlola. Izethi zangempela zidweba amabhamuza ngezindlela eziningi, ngakho kwezeklayenti ezinye izinkombandlela zingase zingatholakali. Amashidi ascanwe adinga i-{{ui:Funda umbhalo (OCR)…}} kuqala.
- Amanothi, amanothi asese nebhodi lokusebenza kuhlolwe kusiphequluli esingenayo i-GUI nasemafayelweni ethu, hhayi ngesethi enkulu yangempela ye-vector nakwiseshini yangempela ye-Live yabantu ababili. Ukushintshwa kwephasiwedi akukakwakhiwa. Izakhiwo ezidlule ku-0.9 azibonisi amanothi.
- Ikilasi lombono liyuhlu lwabantu; alikaze lihlolwe nothisha nabafundi bangempela.
- Izilimi ezingesona isiNgisi ziyizinguqulo zomshini kuze kufundwe isipikha somdabu.
- Abafakeli be-Windows abasayinwanga ngekhodi (Okwengeziwe, bese Qhuba noma kunjalo); isithombe sediski se-macOS asisamukelwanga (chofoza ngesokudla, bese Vula).

## Ngaphambilini: 0.8 no-0.8.1

Lokhu yiyo okushintshile ku-MarkupCraft 0.8 no-0.8.1, uma kuqhathaniswa no-0.7. Into enkulu emisha ukuthi ukumaka kungaba umsebenzi owenziwa umuntu, noma umsizi wakho, futhi uzivala uqobo uma umdwebo ushintsha ngempela.

### Okusha

**Ukumaka okwenza**

- **Yenza ukumaka kube umsebenzi.** Khetha izimaki ezingaba ezisodwa noma eziningi, vula ithebhu ye-{{ui:Imisebenzi}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Yenza okukhethiwe kube umsebenzi}}. Umsebenzi unesihloko, umuntu noma umsizi wakho oxhunyiwe owuzoyenza, usuku lokuphela olungaphoqelekile nesimo, futhi ugcina indawo yawo eshidini nokuxoxisana kwawo. Bona [Yenza ukumaka kube umsebenzi](topic:markups-that-act).
- **Imisebenzi evala ngokuzenzakalelayo.** Umsebenzi ungabheka umdwebo ongaphakathi kwesimaki sawo. Vula ukubuyekezwa okusha kweshidi bese ukhetha {{ui:Hlola manje}}: uma imigqa esendaweni leyo ishintshe ngaphezu kokuncane, umsebenzi umakwa njengo-Done, isimaki sawo sibe yi-Completed, nenothi ekuxoxisaneni ichaza ukuthi yini eshintshile. Usuku olusha lokufaka, noma imigqa eyanyakazela ngaphansi kwamaphoyinti angu-1.5, akubalwa. Umsebenzi ungabheka nento yemodeli; umsizi wakho ubika into nge-`tasks_check`, futhi umbiko wamanje ohlukile, noma into esusiwe, ivala umsebenzi. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa siwuvula futhi.
- **Thola umsebenzi.** Hlunga uhlu lwe-{{ui:Imisebenzi}} ngesimo nangokuthi ngubani, kuhlanganise ne-{{ui:Ephuzile}}; khetha {{ui:Obekwe kuye}}, bese {{ui:Umsizi}} ukubona okulindele umsizi wakho. Umsebenzi odlule usuku lokuphela ubonakala njengesexwayiso kuphaneli ye-Problems. I-{{ui:CSV}} ithumela uhlu. Imisebenzi igcinwa ku-PDF ndawonye nokumaka futhi ihamba nge-Live njengezinye izakhiwo zokumaka.

**Okushintshile, njengohlu olulula**

- **Hlola uhlu lwezinguquko.** Vula ithebhu ye-{{ui:Qhathanisa}}, bese {{ui:Qhathanisa isethi yonke}}, khetha ifayela langaphambili nelamuva bese ukhetha {{ui:Bhala uhlu lwezinguquko}}. Uhlu luqala ngenani, bese luhlanganisa ishidi ngalinye oleliguqukile, eliguquke kakhulu liqala: lapho eshidini, bukhulu kangakanani (ngamayunithi angempela kanye namaintshi ephepha eshidini elilinganisiwe), ukuthi imigqa yengezwe noma yasuswa yini, izimaki ezingaki ezikhona lapho, nokuthi yimaphi amagama avela noma anyamalala. I-{{ui:Kopisha uhlu}} ibeka lokho ebhodini lokukopisha, bese i-{{ui:CSV}} ibhala umugqa owodwa ngokushintsha ngakunye. Bona [Hlola okushintshile phakathi kwezinguqulo](topic:what-changed-in-words).

**Amanothi ezwi nezinqumo**

- **Amanothi ezwi.** Ethebhini le-{{ui:Amanothi}} khetha {{ui:Rekhoda inothi lezwi}}, bese {{ui:Yeka ukurekhoda}}; izirekhodo ziyeka zodwa emizuzwini emibili. Inothi libekwa embhoshongweni weshidi, uyihudula lapho elifanele, ubhale isihloko kuthi ku-{{ui:Izici}}, futhi i-{{ui:Dlala}} ohlwini iyidlala. Irekhodi igcinwa ngaphakathi ku-PDF.
- **Izinqumo.** I-{{ui:Phina isinqumo}} ibhala isigaba noma amabili eshidini. Uma sithatha indawo yesinqumo sangaphambili, khetha lowo ku-{{ui:Sithatha indawo ye}}: owakudala uhlala, umakwa njenge-Replaced, futhi ukhomba entsha. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa sisusa lokho. Bona [Phina amanothi ezwi nezinqumo eshidini](topic:voice-notes-and-decisions).

**Ukuya ekubuyekezweni kwe-redline**

- **Thumela futhi uphendule.** Ethebhini le-{{ui:Imisebenzi}} vula i-{{ui:Redline ukuya ekubuyekezweni}}, bhala ukubuyekezwa okuqhathaniswa nakho okumakiwe bese ukhetha {{ui:Thumela ifayela le-redline}}. Uthola ifayela le-`.redline.json` linento eyodwa yesimaki esivulekile ngasinye: ishidi, indawo (njengamaxabiso eshidi, ukuze ukuphrinta kabusha ngosayizi ohlukile kusalingana), amagama, umbhali, ukuxoxisana kwaso nomsebenzi waso. Lowo odweba izinguquko uphendula ngokuthi Accepted, Rejected noma Done ngefayela le-`.redline-answers.json`; i-{{ui:Layisha ifayela lezimpendulo…}} inika isimaki ngasinye loho kanye nenothi komdwebi, njengesinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa. Bona [Thumela ukumaka komdwebi njenge-redline](topic:redline-to-revision).

**Abasizi be-AI**

- **Amathuluzi amaningi.** Umsizi oxhunywe nge-CadCraft Studio angahlola imisebenzi (`tasks_list`), enze noma ashintshe enye (`task_set`), ayisuse (`task_clear`), ahlole enye ngokubuyekezwa okusha noma ngento (`tasks_check`), futhi afunde uhlu olulula lwezinguquko phakathi kwamasethi amabili emidwebo avulekile (`revisions_changes`). Uthola kuphela amathuluzi owagqamisa ngaphansi kweswichi ye-Assistants, futhi ushintsho ngalunye lungesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Bona [Sebenzisa i-MarkupCraft nomsizi we-AI](topic:use-with-ai-assistants).

### Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- **Izilimi.** I-CadCraft Studio manje inezilimi ezingu-54. Eziyishumi zisha: isiLithuania, isiLatvia, isiBosnia, isiMarathi, isiTelugu, isiAlbania, isiAfrikaans, isiFilipino, isiSwahili nesiUzbek. Zonke ezingesona isiNgisi ziyizinguqulo zomshini kuze kufundwe isipikha somdabu.
- **Amadivayisi okufaka.** I-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Jikelele}} › {{ui:Amadivayisi okufaka}} ibeka okwenziwa yi-trackpad, izinkinkinki zesandla sakho sembewu, ipeni, ukuthinta ngeminwe, imouse ye-3D, ikhonsoli yegeyimu noma ikhonsoli ye-MIDI, kuzo zonke izinhlelo noma kuhlelo lwamanje kuphela. Uze ushintshe okuthile, zonke izidivayisi zisebenza njengakuqala. Bona [Sebenzisa i-trackpad, imouse ye-3D noma ikhonsoli](common:input-devices).
- **Imigqa yebha yamathuluzi.** I-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Imigqa yebha yamathuluzi}} inezinketho ezithi {{ui:Phindisela emugqeni olandelayo}} (okuzenzakalelayo), {{ui:Imigqa emi-2}} no-{{ui:Umugqa owodwa}}, ukuze umdwebo uthole indawo eningi. Bona [Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels).
- **Igridi yempahla.** Izinkambu zesitayela sikamaki zisebenzisa igridi ejwayelekile, enezigaba ezingavuleka nezingqimba zegama nenani.
- **Uphaneli wezinkinga.** Izexwayiso ezivela ohlelweni, njengomsebenzi ophuzile, ziqoqelwa kuphaneli eyodwa ye-Problems.
- **I-Live nezengezo.** Iseshini ze-Live zichazwe ku-[Sebenza ndawonye ngokuqondile](common:work-together-live); uhlu lwezengezo olulandela i-akhawunti yakho lusencwadini ethi [Izengezo nohlu lwe-akhawunti yakho](common:addons-basics).
- **Xhuma i-AI yakho.** I-CadCraft Studio yisiphakeli somsizi wakho siqu: xhuma kanye kuzo zonke izinhlelo eziyisishiyagalombili. Izinhlelo azinayo i-AI yazo.

### Okulungisiwe ku-0.8.1

- **I-Studio ingashintsha ulimi ngenkathi isebenza.** Ngaphambili, uhlelo lwedeskithophu nezinguqulo ezipakishiwe bekuhlala ngesiNgisi. Lokhu akukahlolwa kuzo zonke izinkundla: sitshele uma ulimi lungashintshi.
- Ukufakwa kwe-Studio 0.8 kunikezwa i-0.8.1 ngo-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Izibuyekezo}}.

### Akukakwenziwa kule nguqulo

- **Ayisayinwanga ngekhodi.** Abafakeli be-Windows nefayela le-.dmg le-macOS abakaqukethi isignisha. Ku-Windows, uma i-SmartScreen imisa umfakeli, khetha {{ui:Okwengeziwe}}, bese {{ui:Qhuba noma kunjalo}}. Ku-Mac, chofoza ngesokudla i-CadCraft Studio bese ukhetha {{ui:Vula}}. Izinguqulo zesitolo zimisiwe.
- **Ukuxhuma i-AI yakho akuqedile ngezinye izindawo.** Ukuxhuma ngokuchofoza kanye ngaphakathi kwe-Studio, isixhumi esiphethwe sabasizi besiphequluli nokhiye wakho we-API ngaphakathi kwe-Studio akuthenjiswa kule nguqulo. Iloop yemisebenzi ihlolwe, kodwa ayikahlolwa nomsizi wangempela.
- **Ukuhlola kwemisebenzi kudinga usayizi wekhasi ofanayo.** Ishidi elaphrintwa kabusha ngosayizi ohlukile aliyikuhambisana, ngakho imisebenzi yalo ayivali. Amashidi ahlanganiswa ngenombolo yeshidi, noma ngendawo yekhasi.
- **Amanothi ezwi ayizirekhodo kuphela.** Akukho umbhalo wokukhuluma, nomsizi ubona ubude benothi, umbhali nesihloko, hhayi umsindo. Ukurekhoda kuhlolwe ngomakrofoni wamanga kusiphequluli esingenayo i-GUI; omakrofoni wangempela kufoni, ithebulethi noma ilaptop usaselokhu uzohlolwa.
- **Izikali zemishini kanye nezizathu ku-PDF** zigcinwa kungene eliyimfihlo lefayela. I-MarkupCraft endala iyilahla lapho ilondoloza ifayela futhi, ngakho vula futhi ulondoloze amafayela anjalo nge-0.8 noma ngaphezulu.
- **Uhlangothi lomdwebi lwama-redline.** I-CadCraft efunda ifayela le-redline iliphendule akukakwakhiwa.
- **Ezinye izilimi.** Ibha yohlelo ye-Tamil iyadlula ngo-528 px.

Konke okunye kusencwadini ethi [Yini engakakwakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet). Ukukhishwa okwandulela lokhu, bona [Yini entsha ku-0.7](topic:whats-new-0-7).
