## Hlanza izibambo zepeni

Dweba ngo-{{ui:Ipeni}}, bese uvula imenyu {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} eqenjini lokuHlela bese ukhetha {{ui:Hlanza izibambo zepeni}}. Isibambo sepeni ngasinye esikhethiwe siba yilokho ebeshiwe ukuba yikho: umugqa oqondile ube ngumugqa (wenziwe wathi ngqo noma wema ngqo uma wawusondele kakhulu kulokho), okuzungezayo kube yi-ellipse, ingxenye yendilinga ibe ijika, umugqa onamakhona amane avaliwe ube unxande, kanti konke okunye kube yi-polyline noma i-polygon. Imiphetho ekhululekile yezibambo ezidwetshwe ndawonye ihuzwa omunye nomunye. Uma kungakhethwa sibambo sepeni, zonke izibambo zepeni eshidini elivulekile ziyahlanzwa. Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa: Ctrl+Z ibuyisela ifreehand. Ukwakheka kuqanjwa kukhompyutha yakho ngemithetho emisiwe; akukho okuthunyelwa noma kuphi.

## Letha amazwana okubuyekeza

Iphakheji lemvume ye-BimCraft linegama elithi `01-review-comments.csv`: amazwana umbuyekezi wepulani angase awenze, ngalinye linomthetho, isigatshana, okudingekayo, okutholakele nokufanele kwenziwe. Ku-ithebhu {{ui:Imisebenzi}}, vula {{ui:Amazwana okubuyekeza imvume}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Letha amazwana (CSV)}}. Ikhomenti ngalinye liba inothi elisimweni elithi "Vuliwe" ekholomini esohlangothini lwesobunxele loshidi olivulekile; hudula ngalinye endaweni ehlobene nawo. Ayabalwa ngokuhlolwa okukhona, hhayi ngumhloli wepulani.

## Letha amabhokisi enodi ye-HAZOP

Okwengeziwe kwe-P&ID ye-CadCraft kudweba amanodi ocwaningo lwe-HAZOP futhi kungawagcina njenge-{{ui:Amabhokisi enodi ye-HAZOP}} (ifayela le-JSON). Vula ishidi le-P&ID elishicilelwe lapha, uye ku-ithebhu {{ui:Imisebenzi}}, vula {{ui:Amabhokisi enodi ye-HAZOP}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Letha amabhokisi enodi (JSON)}}. Inodi ngayinye iba unxande onesimo esithi "Vuliwe" eshidini, onenombolo yalo, igama, izinsiza nemigqa embhalweni, bese ithimba libhala okutholakele kwalo njengezimpendulo kuwo. Ishidi lithathwa njengoba lishicilelwe ngobukhulu obugcwele kusukela ekhoneni lesobunxele langezansi kwemidwebo; uma lishicilelwe ngesinye isikali, thayipha leyo nombolo ku-{{ui:Iphrintiwe ngesikali}} kuqala (0.5 ye-1:2). Inodi ewela ngaphandle kwekhasi ayidwetshwa, nephaneli iyatshela ukuthi zingaki. Izimpawu ziyisisekelo sokufunda: zikhombisa lapho amanodi ekhona, azigwegwezi ucwaningo.

## Letha inqubekelaphambili yesiza

I-CivilCraft igcina umugqa ngamunye wokundiza kwedrone noma wokuskena kwesiza. Ku-ithebhu {{ui:Imisebenzi}}, vula {{ui:Inqubekelaphambili yesiza}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Letha inqubekelaphambili (JSON)}}. Ukuhlola ngakunye kuba inothi eshidini elivulekile elichaza ukuthi kwenzeke kangakanani, yini esele ukusikwa noma ukugcwaliswa, yini eyembiwe ngokweqile noma egcwaliswe ngokweqile, nokuthi yini emi ngaphandle kwepulani. Ukuhlola okunento engaphandle kwepulani kuhlala "Vuliwe"; okuhlanzekile kuba "Samukelwe".
