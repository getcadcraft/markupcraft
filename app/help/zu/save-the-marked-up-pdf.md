## Londoloza

1. Khetha {{ui:Londoloza}} kumbhalo wamathuluzi, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}.
2. Uma kuvulwe amafayela amaningi, khetha {{ui:Lelifayela}} (ifayela leshidi osebenza kulo) noma {{ui:Wonke amafayela ashintshiwe}}. Ikhophi ngalinye lilandelwa ngokwahlukana.
3. Linda ukuthi "Ukulondoloza <file>…" kuphele. Ukulondoloza kokuqala esikhathini esisodwa kulayisha futhi umbhali we-PDF.

Umphumela: isiphequluli sakho silanda "<name> (marked up).pdf", futhi ibha yesimo iyasho: 'Ilondoloziwe A-Plans (marked up).pdf (amamaka angu-24, 18.2 MB). Ifayela lokuqala alishintshiwe.' Ichashazi elisemva kwe-{{ui:Londoloza}}liyanyamalala.

**Ifayela lakho lokuqala le-PDF alikaze lishintshwe.** I-MarkupCraft ibhala ifayela elisha. Lapho lifika kuncike ezilungiselelweni zokulanda zesiphequluli sakho (bheka [Amafayela i-MarkupCraft evula futhi ilondoloze](topic:files#where-downloads-go)).

## Qhubeka nokusebenza kuwo kamuva

Vula ikhophi le-"(marked up)" ngesikhathi esilandelayo, hhayi okokuqala. Konke kuyabuya kuhlelekile: amamaka, izimo, izimpendulo, izinto zesijeziso, isikali sangakanye kweshidi nanoma yimaphi amagama amashidi owawabhalile. Ukulondoloza kabusha kulanda ifayela elinegama elifanayo elithi "(marked up)"; isiphequluli sakho singangeza inombolo njengo "(1)" uma idawunilodi endala isasefolda. Ushintshe noma uhlanze amakhophi amadala wena.

I-MarkupCraft ayilondolozi i-PDF yodwa. Ngenkathi usebenza igcina ikhophi lokubuyisa kulesi siphequluli, ngakho ukuphahla noma ukuvala ithebhu akulahlekisi amamaka akho: ngesikhathi esilandelayo uvula i-MarkupCraft izobuza ukuthi "Buyisela umsebenzi wakho?". Bheka [Buyisela umsebenzi wakho](common:recover). Nokho londoloza ngaphambi kokuvala; uma uzama ukuphuma namamaka angalondolozwanga, isiphequluli sibuza "Shiya isayithi?" kuqala (i-iPad Safari ayenzi lokho).

## Okubonwa abanye abafundi be-PDF

Wonke amamaka abhalwa njengamazwana ajwayelekile e-PDF (annotation) anomdwebo wawo, ngakho noma yiliphi umbukeli we-PDF uyawubonisa, uwuphrinte, futhi uwubhale ohlwini lwakhe lwamazwana:

- **Ukubukeka kufana** nokuku-MarkupCraft: amafu anemiphetho egobile, izinhloko zomcibisholo, izitembu, amachashazi okubala, amabheji enombolo yezinto zesijeziso, namalebula okulinganisa.
- **Imininingwane ijwayelekile:** umbhali, usuku, isihloko, umbhalo wezwana, nemibala.
- **Amafu** ngamazwana e-rectangle noma e-polygon anomngcele ofana namafu; **amabhokisi okubhala** nezimakhalekhukhwini ngamazwana amabhokisi okubhala; **amanothi** ngamanothi anamathela; imigqa yepeni nokukhanyisa ngu-ink; **izitembu** ngamazwana ezitembu.
- **Ubude nezindawo** ngamazwana okulinganisa, futhi ishidi ngalinye elilinganisiwe lithwala isikali salo, ngakho izinhlelo ezaziwa ngokulinganisa zingabonisa amanani.
- **Izimpendulo** ziyizimpendulo zezwana, futhi **izimo** ziyizimo zokubuyekeza (Ivulekile ibonakala njengengenasimo).
- **Izinombolo zeshidi** oziloba zibe ngamalebula amakhasi abanye abafundi abawabonisayo.

Izinto ezimbalwa kuphela i-MarkupCraft iyazifunda emuva: umuntu obekelwe into yesijeziso nedethi yokuphela kwayo, isimo sento, nezitayela ezithile. Ezinye izinhlelo ziyazigcina uma zingazibhalanga kabusha amazwana.

Kwenye inhlelo, amazwana angahanjiswa noma ahlelwe njengamanye. Uma umuntu enza lokho futhi ebuyisela ifayela, i-MarkupCraft isebenzisa indawo entsha yezwana lapho ivula ifayela.

Imininingwane egcwele: [Okulondolozwa ku-PDF](topic:what-the-pdf-keeps).

## Izinhlamvu ezikhiphayo njengo "?"

Umbhalo wamamaka ubhalwa ngefonti ejwayelekile i-Helvetica, equkethwe yiyo yonke i-PDF viewer. Umboze izinhlamvu zesiNgisi nezaseNtshonalanga Yurophu (é, ñ, ü, ø, nokunye okufanayo), kodwa hhayi ezinye izinhlelo zezinhlamvu, izimpawu ezingaphandle kwalelo sethi, noma ama-emoji. Lezo zinhlamvu zibonakala kahle ku-MarkupCraft kodwa ekhophini elilondoliwe ziphrinta njenge "?", futhi umlayezo wokulondoloza uzibala: "Izinhlamvu ezingu-3 ngaphandle kwesethi yaseNtshonalanga Yurophu ziphrinta njenge "?" ekhophini." Bhala amagama anjalo ngokugcwele, noma sebenzisa okulinganayo okulula (isibonelo "dia." esikhundleni sophawu lwebanga).

## Amafayela angalondolozeki

- **Ama-PDF avikelekile:** "Le PDF ivikelekile (ifihliwe), ngakho ikhophi elinamamaka alikwazi ukubhalwa. Cela ikhophi elingavikelekile." Usangakwazi ukuyibuyekeza esikrinini, uhlinzeke i-CSV ne-BCF.
- Uma ukulondoloza kwehlulekile ngenye isizathu, ibhokisi elithi "Akukwazanga ukulondoloza <file>" libonisa isizathu. Amamaka akho usasethebhu; akukho okulahlekayo uze uyivale.

## Ukucindezela nokuphrinta

Akukho ukucindezela (ukushisa amamaka ekhasini) futhi akukho umyalo wokuphrinta okwamanje. Ukuphrinta, vula ikhophi elilondoliwe kunoma yimuphi umbukeli we-PDF uphrinte ngokubonisa amazwana.
