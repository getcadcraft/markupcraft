Amathuluzi e-MarkupCraft afana noma uwafinyelela kanjani. **Isitayela semenyu** sinquuma kuphela lapho ahlala khona esikrinini, amagama asetshenziswa khona, nokuthi yiziphi izinkinobho ezizikhethayo. Khetha esinye endaweni ethi {{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:MarkupCraft}} › {{ui:Menus, keys and mouse}}; ushintsho luyasebenza ngokushesha, futhi lesi siphequluli siyasikhumbula.

## Izitayela ezintathu

| Isitayela | Okutholayo |
|---|---|
| {{ui:MarkupCraft}} | Ibha yamathuluzi yayo ye-MarkupCraft (File, Select, Draw, Revise, Annotate, Measure, Edit) nokhiye bayo bezinhlamvu ezilodwa: {{key:V}} khetha, {{key:H}} shelelisa, {{key:P}} ipeni, {{key:I}} iqaqambisi, {{key:L}} umugqa, {{key:A}} utolo, {{key:R}} unxande, {{key:E}} iyelipsi, {{key:C}} ifu, {{key:K}} isichazi, {{key:T}} umbhalo, {{key:N}} inothi, {{key:S}} isitembu, {{key:M}} ubude, {{key:Q}} indawo, {{key:U}} bala. Yisona esiqalayo. |
| {{ui:PDF review}} | Ibha yemenyu ({{ui:File}}, {{ui:Edit}}, {{ui:View}}, {{ui:Document}}, {{ui:Markup}}, {{ui:Measure}}, {{ui:Review}}, {{ui:Window}}) phezu komugqa wamathuluzi okubuyekeza, nokhiye behlelwe ngendlela ezibeka ngayo izinhlelo zokubuyekeza i-PDF: {{key:V}} khetha, {{key:Shift+V}} shelelisa, {{key:H}} iqaqambisi, {{key:Q}} isichazi, {{key:N}} ipholigoni, {{key:Shift+N}} iphola-layini, {{key:Shift+T}} inothi, {{key:Shift+A}} ijika, nezilinganiso ku-{{key:Alt+Shift}}: {{key:Alt+Shift+K}} linganisa, {{key:Alt+Shift+L}} ubude, {{key:Alt+Shift+G}} ikona, {{key:Alt+Shift+A}} indawo, {{key:Alt+Shift+C}} bala. {{key:Alt+L}} ivula uhlu lokumaka. Inkinobho ephakathi ishelelisa; ukuchofoza ngakwesokudla akwenzi lokho. |
| {{ui:Ribbon}} | Amathebhu anegama elihambisana nomsebenzi: {{ui:Mark up}} (khetha, dweba, amafu, izichazi, umbhalo, amanothi, izitembu), {{ui:Measure}}, {{ui:Edit}}, {{ui:Review}} (uhlu lokumaka nokuthumela kwalo, izindaba ze-BCF, uhlu lokulungisa, amarekhodi asensimini, ibhokisi lamathuluzi, ukuqhathanisa), {{ui:Pages}} no-{{ui:View}}, kanye no-{{ui:File}} nezinkinobho ezisheshayo zokuvula, ukulondoloza, ukuhlehlisa nokuphinda. Amathuluzi agcina okhiye bezinhlamvu ezilodwa be-MarkupCraft. Inkinobho yephaneli engakavulwa ivula leyo phaneli kuqala. |

Zonke izitayela ziyagcina izinqamuleli ze-{{key:Mod}}: {{key:Mod+Z}}, {{key:Mod+Y}}, {{key:Mod+S}}, {{key:Mod+O}}, {{key:Mod+P}}, {{key:Mod+C}}, {{key:Mod+X}}, {{key:Mod+V}}, {{key:Mod+D}}, {{key:Mod+A}}, {{key:Mod+F}}, {{key:Mod+0}} {{ui:Fit page}} kanye no-{{key:Mod+9}} {{ui:Fit width}}; {{key:PageUp}} ne-{{key:PageDown}} zishintsha ishidi.

:::tip
Ukhiye ongowe-layout ye-MarkupCraft kodwa ongowesitayela okhethe ngaso akwenzi lutho, nebha yesimo ikhombisa lapho ithuluzi lakhe seliye khona (Esitayeleni {{ui:PDF review}}, {{ui:Callout}} ingu-Q). {{key:Delete}}, {{key:Esc}}, {{key:Enter}}, {{key:Tab}}, {{key:Space}} nezinkinobho zikhala zigcina incazelo yazo. Uma usabhala ebhokisini lombhalo, esichazini noma enothini, wonke okhiye bembhalo; izinombolo ozibhalayo ngenkathi udweba zisavula ibhokisi lobude.
:::

## Yenza ngokwakho

1. Khetha isitayela esiseduze kakhulu nalokho ofuna, bese uchofoza {{ui:Customize…}}.
2. Endaweni ethi {{ui:Keys and menus}}, thola ithuluzi (ibhokisi lokusesha liyasiza). Chofoza {{ui:Add a key}} bese ucindezela ukhiye, noma okhiye ababili ngokulandelana ukuze uthole ukhiye wezinhlamvu ezimbili. Chofoza ukhiye ukuze uwususe.
3. Nebha yemenyu, {{ui:Where it sits}} ihambisa ithuluzi kwenye imenyu, noma libe {{ui:Keys only}}.
4. Endaweni ethi {{ui:Mouse}}, setha iwili nezinkinobho ezishelelisa ishidi.
5. Nikeza igama, bese uchofoza {{ui:Save}}.

Ukulondoloza isitayela esakhelwe ngaphakathi kwenza ikhophi yakho ("Ukubuyekezwa kwami kwe-PDF"); isakhelwe ngaphakathi asishintshi. Ukhiye onikezwe amathuluzi amabili ubonakala obomvu futhi kumele ulungiswe ngaphambi kokuthi ulondoloze.

## Imouse

- **Iwili lemouse**: {{ui:Scroll up zooms in}} (okokuqala) noma {{ui:Scroll up zooms out}}, nokuthi lishesha kangakanani. Ukucindezela kwe-trackpad kusondeza njalo ngendlela eminwe yakho enyakaza ngayo.
- **Shelelisa ishidi ngokusebenzisa**: inkinobho ephakathi, inkinobho yesokudla, noma zombili. {{key:Space}} nokudonsa, nethuluzi {{ui:Pan}}, kushelelisa njalo; inkinobho yesokunxele ngaphandle kwalokho idweba ikhethe, ngakho ukuyikhetha lapha akushintshi lutho.
- MarkupCraft ayinambono ye-3D, ngakho izinkinobho ze-3D azenzi lutho lapha.

## Yithathe nawe

{{ui:Export}} ilondoloza isitayela esisetshenziswayo njengefayela elincane le-`.ccinput.json`; {{ui:Import…}} lingeza elinye, elivela kwenye ikhompyutha noma komlingani. Ifayela elingenisiwe lingakwazi ukubhekisela kumathuluzi e-MarkupCraft kuphela: konke okunye kukho kuyasuswa futhi kubalwe ebhokisini lesimo. {{ui:Delete this style}} isusa esakho.

:::note
Lezi zilungiselelo zihlala kulesi siphequluli. {{ui:Use this style in every app}} ngaphansi kwamakhadi esitayela yenza ukuthi zonke izinhlelo zivule esitayeleni sazo esihambisanayo: {{ui:PDF review}} ihamba nezitayela zemenyu yezinye izinhlelo, {{ui:Ribbon}} neribhoni zazo, nebha yamathuluzi ye-MarkupCraft nezazo. Uhlelo olungenaso isitayela esihambisanayo lugcina ezalo.
:::

:::tip
Izakhiwo zendawo yokusebenza ezisendaweni ethi {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Layout}} ({{ui:Ribbon and project browser}}, {{ui:Toolbox and info box}}) zakha iribhoni yazo ngamathebhu esitayela se-{{ui:Ribbon}}, nebhokisi lamathuluzi lazo ngebha yamathuluzi ye-MarkupCraft, ngakho lonke ithuluzi lisebenza ngendlela efanayo kuzo.
:::
