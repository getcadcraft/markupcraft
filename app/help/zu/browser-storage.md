I-MarkupCraft ayinayo i-akhawunti futhi ayinayo isitoreji seseva. Izinto ezimbalwa ezincane zigcinwa esitoreji sesisheshi sakho kulolu webhusayithi, konke okunye kuhlala emafayeleni akho.

## Lokho kusho ukuthini

- **Ngesisheshi, ngekhompyutha.** Chrome ne-Firefox kulo kompyutha ngamunye zinesitoreji sazo. Ilaptop yakho nedeskithophu yehhovisi ngayinye inesitoreji sayo. Akukho okuvumelanisa phakathi kwazo, ngisho noma ungena ngemvume kwisisheshi.
- **Amawindi azimele nawe-incognito** aqala engenalutho futhi akhohlwa konke lapho evalwa.
- **Ukusula idatha yesayithi kuyisula.** Ukususa ama-cookies nedatha yesayithi yalolu webhusayithi, noma yazo zonke izisayithi, kuzilungiselelo zesisheshi kususa igama lakho, izilungiselelo nechibi. Amanye amathuluzi okuhlanza nezinqubomgomo zenkampani ezinye zenza lokhu ngokuzenzakalelayo.
- **Akukho okulayishwayo.** Lezi zinani azishiyi ikhompyutha, ngaphandle kwegama lakho ngaphakathi kwama-PDF oyilondolozayo. Izikopi zokubuyisela nazo zihlala kulesi sisheshi.

:::safari
Safari ingasusa idatha egcinwe yiwebhusayithi emva kwamasonto athile ngaphandle kokuvakasha. Vula i-MarkupCraft njalo ngesikhathi, futhi gcina ichibi lamathuluzi oliphumayo.
:::

## Okungagcinwa esisheshini

- **Izimakiwe zakho, ngokuphelele.** Zifanele kwi-PDF "(marked up)" oyilondolozayo. Ikhophi yokubuyisela ingumsizi wokuphepha uma kwenzeka ukuphahlazeka noma ithebhu evaliwe, hhayi indawo yokugcina umsebenzi: iyasuswa uma ulondoloza, futhi ukusula idatha yesayithi kuyisusa. Bheka [Buyisela umsebenzi wakho](common:recover).
- **Amafayela akho avulekile.** Njalo lapho uvula i-MarkupCraft, vula ama-PDF futhi.
- **Ishidi obekuso, isikhulisi, izihlungi nomlando wokuhlehlisa.** Lokhu kuhlala kuze kube yilapho uvala isethi noma ithebhu.

## Gcina ikopi bese uhambisa izilungiselelo zakho

- **Ichibi lamathuluzi:** {{ui:Amathuluzi}}, bese {{ui:Thumela}}. Kwesinye isisheshi noma ikhompyutha, {{ui:Amathuluzi}}, bese {{ui:Ngenisa}}.
- **Igama:** libhale futhi kwesinye isisheshi (chofoza inkinobho yegama kubha yesimo).
- **Izilungiselelo zamathuluzi:** zisethe futhi, noma londoloza ngamunye umakiwe wesitayela njengethuluzi ukuze uhambe nechibi.

## Qala kusha

Ukuze usethe kabusha konke, sula idatha yalolu webhusayithi ezilungiselelweni zesisheshi sakho. Igama lakho, izilungiselelo nechibi kuyonyamalala; amathuluzi amasithupha ajwayelekile ayobuya.

## Ikhophi engaxhunyiwe

{{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} ebhalweni eliphezulu igcina amafayela e-app ngokwawo (kanye nomfundi nomlobi we-PDF, cishe ka-6 MB) nakusisheshi, ngaphandle kwalezi zilungiselelo. Ukusula idatha yesayithi kususa nalelo kopi; chofoza "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi" bese ukhetha "Susa ikhophi engaxhunyiwe" ukuze ususe kuphela lowo. Bheka [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

Ukulondoloza efwini nokuvumelanisa phakathi kwamakhompyutha akutholakali okwamanje.
