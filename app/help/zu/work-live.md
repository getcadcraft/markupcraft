## Okuzofunda

Okwenzeka emakiwen­i ngenkathi abantu abaningi bebuyekeza isethi eyodwa yamapulani ngesikhathi esifanayo. I-CadCraft Studio iqala futhi ijoyine isikhathi se-Live TeamWork (Yabelana ngokuphilayo, Joyina); leli khasi likhuluma ngesethi ngokwayo.

## Wonke umuntu umaka ngesikhathi esisodwa

Ama-PDF womphathi afika kubo bonke abajoyinayo, ngakho nonke nibuka amashidi afanayo. Dweba, hambisa, shintsha isitayela bese ususa okumakiwe njengoba kujwayelekile. Ushintsho lwakho lubonakala ngokushesha; ushintsho lwabanye luvela ngemuva kwesikhashana lapho lwenziwe.

- **Abantu ababili beshintsha okumakiwe okukodwa:** izinto ezihlukile (enye umbala, enye ubukhulu womugqa) zombili zigciniwe. Into efanayo iya kushintsho lwakamuva.
- **Izimpendulo:** izimpendulo zabo bonke zihlala entonjeni, ngokulandelana zabhalwa ngakho. Izimo ziyahamba ngendlela efanayo.
- **Izikali namagama amashidi** abekwe noma ngubani asebenza kubo bonke.
- **Izinombolo ze-punch azikaze ziphindeke:** uma abantu ababili bengeza into ye-punch ngesikhathi esifanayo, enye yazo iyahlehliswa futhi ingayengeza futhi.

Okusalela wena kuphela: izendlalelo ozifihlayo noma ozikhiyayo, amakholomu akho, ukubukwa, ithuluzi lakho negama lakho njengombhali.

## Bona ukuthi ubani uphi

Umbuyekezi ngamunye unombala wakhe. Ebhodini obheka kulo ubona izinkomba zabanye ngamagama abo, nebhokisi elinamachashaza ngombala wabo elizungeze okumakiwe abakukhethile. Uhlu lwababuyekezi lukhona ngakwesokudla esiphezulu kweshidi: lutshela ukuthi iyiphi ishidi ngalinye likuyo, futhi ukuchofoza igama kukuyisa kuleyo shidi.

Ichashaza ngombala wombuyekezi lihlezi ecaleni kwegama lombhali, ngakho ubona ngokushesha ukuthi ubani wabhala yini. Isihlungi sombhali sithola okumakiwe kombuyekezi oyedwa. Londoloza i-PDF futhi okumakiwe ngakunye kuthwala umbhali wakho, ngakho isethi ebuyekeziwe ibonisa ukuthi ubani wabhala yini.

Izinkomba nemibala yesikhathi kuphela: azilondolozwa ku-PDF.

## Lapho ushintsho luhlehliswa

Ikhophi yomphathi ihlola wonke ushintsho. Elingangeni liyahlehliswa esikrinini sakho, futhi ibha yesimo ichaza ukuthi kungani, isibonelo lapho umuntu esuse lowo kumakiwe ngaphambi kwakho.

## Ukuhlehlisa kuhlehlisa umsebenzi wakho

{{key:Mod+Z}} ihlehlisa ushintsho lwakho lokugcina futhi ishiye abanye bonke. Uma umuntu eshintshe into efanayo kusukela lapho, akukho okuhlehliswayo futhi ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi ngubani.

## Joyina usuka kusiphequluli

Ilungu leqembu elingenayo i-Studio lingaskena isithombe esingaphansi kwekhodi yomphathi ngefoni, ithebulethi noma ikhompyutha esinethiwekhini elifanayo. I-MarkupCraft ivula kusiphequluli futhi igcwaliswe yisethi yamapulani yomphathi uma omphathi ekuvumela ungene. Isixhumanisi sokumema sivumela umuntu ukuthi ajoyine usuka kwenye inethiwekhi: umphathi udinga isihlalo se-Team Cloud Plan sale-app, abanye bonke bajoyina mahhala, futhi isixhumanisi sisebenza kuphela uma isikhathi somphathi sivulekile.

## Lapho isikhathi siphela

Umphathi kuphela ulondoloza ama-PDF amakiwe. Abanye bonke bagcina ikhophi engalondolozwanga: yilondoloze uma ufuna ukuyigcina.

Ama-PDF ovula ngemva kokujoyina ahlala esikrinini sakho: abanye abawathholi.
:::
