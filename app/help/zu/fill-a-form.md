Ama-PDF amaningi afika nefomu: izicelo zemvume, uhlu lokuhlola, amakhava okuthumela. I-MarkupCraft ibonisa izinkundla zefomu namanani azo, futhi ikuvumela ukuzigcwalisa. Amanani angena ezinkundleni ze-PDF ngokwazo, ngakho i-Acrobat nawo wonke amanye amafunda e-PDF ayawabonisa futhi ayagcina.

## Gcwalisa ifomu

1. Vula i-PDF. Uma inefomu, ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi inezinkundla ezingaki.
2. Khetha {{ui:Gcwalisa ifomu}} kumbhalo wamathuluzi (noma cindezela {{key:F}}). Yonke inkundla ekhasini ithola ibhokisi onganikwa kulo okubhala.
3. Chofoza inkundla bese uthayipha. Thikitha amabhokisi, khetha izinkinobho zomsakazo, khetha kuma-drop-down nakuma-uhlu. {{key:Tab}} iya enkundleni elandelayo.
4. Ukuze unyakazise ishidi ngenkathi ugcwalisa, hudula noma yikuphi ngaphandle kwezinkundla.
5. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} noma khetha elinye ithuluzi uma useqedile. Okuthayiphile kuhlala eshidini.

Ngo-{{ui:Khetha}}, ukuchofoza kabili inkundla kuvula {{ui:Gcwalisa ifomu}} bese kukubeka kuleyo nkundla.

## Londoloza ifomu eligcwalisiwe

Khetha {{ui:Londoloza}}. Ikhophi oyilandayo inamanani akho ezinkundleni zayo, namamaki akho eceleni kwawo. Ibha yesimo ibonisa ukuthi zingaki izinkundla ezigcwaliswe.

Ukuze unikeze ifomu elingeke lishintshwe muntu: khetha {{ui:Amakhasi}}, bese {{ui:Londoloza ikhophi egcwalisiwe…}}: amanani aba yingxenye yekhasi. Ukuphrinta kwenza okufanayo.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Inkundla ekhiywe yi-PDF (efundeka kuphela) ayishintshwa. Ibhokisi layo liba ngwevu.
- Umbhalo kuze kufike obude bomkhawulo wenkundla uyagcinwa; izinhlamvu ezengeziwe ayithayiphwa.
- Amanye amafomu afika engenayo imifanekiso eboniswayo yamabhokisi nezinkinobho, namanye amaprogram awabonisa engenalutho. I-MarkupCraft iyawadweba, futhi uma ulondoloza yengeza umfanekiso, ukuze abonakale noma yikuphi.
- Ukuhlehlisa ngaphakathi kwenkundla kuyeyayo: cindezela {{key:Ctrl+Z}} uma uthayipha kuyo. Ukuhlehlisa kokumaka akuhlehlisi amanani efomu.
- Uma isiphequluli sakho sivala, amanani owathayiphile abuyela kanye nomsebenzi wakho owungalondolozwanga.
- Amanani athayiphwe ngezinhlamvu ofonti ojwayelekile we-PDF ingeke iwabhale (isibonelo isiShayina) ayalondolozwa, futhi i-PDF icela umfundi ngamunye ukuthi awadwebe yena.
- Izinkundla zesignesha namaconsi wenkinobho awigcwaliswa lapha. Ukusayina, sebenzisa {{ui:Isitembu}} nesignesha yakho.
- Amanani athayiphwe efomini awabelwani ngesikhathi esiphilayo okwamanje.
