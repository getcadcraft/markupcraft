I-MarkupCraft isebenza ngokuthinta: ku-iPad (Safari noma Chrome), kulaptop enesikrini esithintwayo, noma ku-tablet ye-Windows noma ye-Android nesiphequluli sakamuva. Ngesimo se-landscape ithebhulethi ibonisa isakhiwo esigcwele.

## Izenzo zesandla

| Isenzo sesandla | Okwenzayo |
|---|---|
| Umunwe owodwa | Udweba ngethuluzi elikhona, noma ukhethe nge-{{ui:Khetha}} |
| Iminwe emibili, ihlukaniswe noma ihlanganiswe | Isondeza ngokuzungeza indawo phakathi kweminwe yakho |
| Iminwe emibili, ihanjiswe ndawonye | Ishelelisa; ungasondeza nokushelelisa ngokunyakaza kunye |
| Umunwe wesibili ngesikhathi udweba | Ukhansela okuqalwe ngumunwe wokuqala bese uqala ukusondeza esikhundleni salokho |
| Ukuthepha kabili, noma ukubamba | Iqeda i-{{ui:Iphola-layini}}, i-{{ui:Ipholigoni}}, {{ui:Ifu elikhululekile}} noma {{ui:Indawo}} |
| Bamba umbhalo, isichazi noma inothi, nge-{{ui:Khetha}} | Ivula umbhalo wayo ukuze uhlelwe |
| Umunwe owodwa nge-{{ui:Shelelisa}} evuliwe | Ushelelisa |

Esikrinini esithintwayo izinkinobho zebha yamathuluzi, imenyu, amathebhu nezinkinobho zengxoxo zinkulu, ngakho zilula ukuzithinta ngomunwe.

## Okwenzeka lapho uphakamisa

Ukuze umunwe wokuqala wokusondeza ungashiyi isibonakaliso, amanye amathuluzi asebenza lapho uphakamisa umunwe, **hhayi** lapho uthinta:

- {{ui:Bala}}: ichashazi ilingezwa lapho uphakamisa.
- {{ui:Inothi}}: inothi libekwa lapho uphakamisa.
- {{ui:Iphola-layini}}, {{ui:Ipholigoni}}, {{ui:Ifu elikhululekile}} no-{{ui:Indawo}}: iphoyinti ngalinye lingezwa lapho uphakamisa.
- {{ui:Umugqa}}, {{ui:Utolo}}, {{ui:Ubude}} no-{{ui:Linganisa}}: uma uthepha okokuqala bese uthepha okokugcina, okokugcina kubekwa lapho uphakamisa.

Uma umunwe wakho uhamba ngaphezu kancane ngaphambi kokuphakama, akubalwa njengokuthepha futhi akukho okwengezwayo. Thepha ngokucacile; ukuze ulungise, sebenzisa {{ui:Hlehlisa}}.

Amathuluzi ohudulayo ({{ui:Ipeni}}, {{ui:Qaqambisa}}, {{ui:Ifu}}, {{ui:Unxande}}, {{ui:Iyelipsi}}, {{ui:Isichazi}}, {{ui:Umbhalo}}, {{ui:Isitembu}}) aqala uma uthinta; umunwe wesibili uwakhansela.

## Ukukhetha ngomunwe

- Thepha imaki ukuze uyikhethe; hudula ukuze uyihambise; hudula isibambi ukuze ushintshe usayizi. Ngemva kokuthi umunwe uthinte ishidi, amamaki nezibambi athatha isilinganiso esikhulu, futhi izibambi zidwetshwa zikhulu.
- Hudula phezu kweshidi elingenalutho ukuze udwebe ifasitela lokukhetha (kusukela ngakwesobunxele kuya ngakwesokudla: okugcwele ngaphakathi; kusukela ngakwesokudla kuya ngakwesobunxele: okuthintayo).
- Ku-{{ui:Khetha}}, umunwe owodwa **akushelelisi**. Sebenzisa iminwe emibili, noma khetha {{ui:Shelelisa}}.

## Okudinga ikhibhodi

Ngaphandle kwekhibhodi akukho {{key:Shift}}, {{key:Esc}} noma {{key:Enter}}. Nansi okufanele wenze esikhundleni salokho:

| Isenzo sekhibhodi | Ngaphandle kwekhibhodi |
|---|---|
| {{key:Esc}} ukukhansela noma ukubuyela ku-Select | Thepha {{ui:Khetha}} ebhalweni lamathuluzi |
| {{key:Shift}} ngemigqa ye-45°, izikwele nezindilinga | Dweba ngokucophelela; lungisa ngezibambi |
| {{key:Shift}}-chofoza ukuze wengeze ekukhethweni | Sebenzisa ifasitela lokukhetha |
| {{key:Shift}}-chofoza ukuze ususe ichashazi lokubala | {{ui:Hlehlisa}} ngemva kokuthepha okungalungile |
| {{key:Mod+Enter}} ukuze uqede ukuthayipha | Thepha ngaphandle kwebhokisi lombhalo |
| {{key:Enter}} ukuze uqede i-polyline noma uhlele umbhalo wemaki | Bamba ukuthinta, noma {{ui:Hlela ehlokweni}} e-{{ui:Izici}} |
| {{key:Delete}} | {{ui:Susa}} ebhalweni lamathuluzi noma e-{{ui:Izici}} |
| Ukuqala iqembu elisha lokubala nge-{{key:Esc}} | Thepha ishidi elingenalutho nge-{{ui:Khetha}}, bese uthepha {{ui:Bala}} futhi |

Ikesi yekhibhodi noma ikhibhodi ye-Bluetooth ibuyisa zonke izinqamuleli; ku-iPad, inkinobho ye-⌘ isebenza ku-{{key:Mod+Z}} nakokunye.

Izinkinobho azibonisi amathiphu azo ekuthintweni. Ukhiye wethuluzi ngalinye ulotshwe ku-[Ikhibhodi nembewu](topic:keyboard).

## Ipeni lesitayela ne-Apple Pencil

Ipeni lesitayela (i-Apple Pencil, i-Surface Pen nokunye okufanayo) lisebenza njengembewu: udwebe futhi likhethe, futhi lenza ngokushesha lapho luthintwa phansi. Sebenzisa iminwe yakho ukusondeza nokushelelisa. Ingcindezi ayishintshi ububanzi bomugqa.

## Ukuvula nokulondoloza ku-iPad

- {{ui:Vula}} ivula i-Files picker: i-iCloud Drive, On My iPad, nanoma iyiphi i-storage app oyixhumanisile.
- {{ui:Londoloza}} icela ukulanda; ikhophi ifika ku-Files, ku-Downloads (noma kufolda eyethwe kumasethingi e-Safari).

## Gcina umsebenzi wakho uphephile ethebhulethini

:::warning
I-iPad Safari ayikuxwayisi ngaphambi kokushiya ikhasi elinomsebenzi ongalondolozwanga, futhi i-iPadOS ingalayisha kabusha ithebhu ngemuva ukuze ikhulule imemori. I-MarkupCraft igcina ikhophi yokubuyisa esiphequluleni futhi iyinikeza uma uvula kabusha (bona [Thola umsebenzi wakho](common:recover)), kodwa **londoloza ngemva kweshidi ngalinye noma ngemva kohambo ngalunye**: ithebhu eyimfihlo ayigcini ikhophi.
:::

- Igama lakho, izilungiselelo zamathuluzi nebhokisi lamathuluzi kugcinwa kulesi siphequluli kule iPad, njengakukhompyutha. Bona [Okugcinwa kulesi siphequluli](topic:browser-storage).
- **Ngaphambi kokuvakasha esiteshini ngaphandle kwesignali:** ngenkathi usuku-inthanethi, vula i-MarkupCraft ku-Safari (noma Chrome) ku-iPad bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} ebhalweni eliphezulu. Linda kuze kutshele ukuthi "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi" (ibala "Ilondoloza u-N kwangu-M"; umfundi nomlobi we-PDF bayi-6 MB cishe). Esiteshini, vula ikheli elifanayo noma ibhukumaki yalo: ukuvula, ukumaka nokulondoloza ama-PDF kusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhuma. Uma kuthi "Ukungaphandle kwe-inthanethi akutholakali", usesithebheni esiyimfihlo; sebenzisa ethebhini ejwayelekile. Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).
- I-Safari isusa ikhophi yangaphandle ongayisebenzisanga izinsuku ezithile. Ngaphambi kohambo, chofoza "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi" bese ukhetha "Londoloza futhi (thola inguqulo emusha kakhulu)".
- Amasethi amakhulu ashidi asebenzisa inkumbulo eningi. Kuthebhulethi, vula kuphela amashidi odinga kulokhu kuvakasha.

## Kufoni

Iyasebenza, kodwa isikrini sincane: izingxenye zishiyana (ibha yamathuluzi, ishidi, ibha yesimo, amapaneli, bese uhlu lwamashidi), futhi ibha yamathuluzi iskrola ngokuthi isuke ohlangothini. Sebenzisa ifoni ukubheka nokungeza inothi elisheshayo, nethebhulethi noma ikhompyutha ukubuyekezwa okungempela.
