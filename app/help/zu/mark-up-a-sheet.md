Zonke izithuluzi zisebenza ngendlela efanayo: zikhethe, dweba ephepheni, bese ukumaka okusha kukhethwa ukuze ukubone endaweni ethi {{ui:Properties}}. Ithuluzi lihlala livuliwe okulandelayo. Ibha yesimo ihlala isho ukuthi ithuluzi lilindele ukuthini okulandelayo.

:::touch
Esikrinini sokuthinta, sebenzisa umunwe owodwa ukudweba. Umunwe wesibili ukhansela lokho owaqala ngakho owokuqala futhi usondeza noma uhambise kunalokho, ngakho awusoze wenze isimaka ngephutha ngesikhathi useondeza. Okwengeziwe ku-[Ukubuyekeza ku-iPad noma kusikrini sokuthinta](topic:review-on-a-tablet).

## Amafu okuguqula

**Ibhokisi lefu:** khetha {{ui:Cloud}} ({{key:C}}) bese uhudula ibhokisi. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuze lenze lisikwele.

**Ifu elikhululekile** ozungeze isimo esingajwayelekile:

1. Khetha {{ui:Free cloud}} ({{key:Shift+C}}).
2. Chofoza kulo lonke ikhona lendawo.
3. Qeda ngokuchofoza kabili, {{key:Enter}} noma ngokuchofoza ngakwesokudla. Esikrinini sokuthinta, thinta kabili.

Ngenkathi uchofoza amaphuzu, {{key:Backspace}} isusa iphuzu lokugcina futhi {{key:Esc}} ikhansela yonke imumo. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuze uhlangothi olulandelayo lube ngezinyathelo ze-45°. Ifu elikhululekile lidinga okungenani amaphuzu amathathu.

Amajika ehla ahlala ephuma ngaphandle, nosayizi wawo ulandela ububanzi bomugqa: umugqa obukhulu unikeza amajika amakhulu.

## Izichazi

Isichazi yibhokisi lombhalo elinomholi oqondisa kokuthile.

1. Khetha {{ui:Callout}} ({{key:K}}).
2. Cindezela endaweni ofuna ukuyichaza, bese uhudula lapho umbhalo kufanele khona. Khipha.
   Uma uchofoza nje ngaphandle kokuhudula, ibhokisi libekwa phezulu nakwesokudla kwephuzu.
3. Bhala umbhalo. Ibhokisi likhula phansi njengoba imigqa ihlephuka.
4. Chofoza noma yiphi indawo ngaphandle kwebhokisi, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}}, ukuze uqede. {{key:Esc}} ilahla isichazi.

Uma uqeda ungabhalanga lutho, akukho sichazi esengeziwe ("Akukho okubhalwe, ngakho akukho sichazi esengeziwe.").

Lapho sekudwetshiwe, khetha ukuze uhambise ibhokisi, udonse ithiphu yomholi noma ukugoba kwawo (izibambelelo ezimbili zedayimane), noma ulungise usayizi webhokisi.

## Amabhokisi okubhala

1. Khetha {{ui:Text}} ({{key:T}}).
2. Chofoza lapho umbhalo uqala khona (uzothola ibhokisi lobubanzi obufanele), noma hudula ibhokisi losayizi ofunayo.
3. Bhala, bese uchofoza ngaphandle noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}}.

Umbhalo awunamngcele ngokuzenzakalelayo. Ukuze unikeze umngcele, phakamisa {{ui:Line width}} endaweni ethi {{ui:Properties}}. Ukuze unikeze ingemuva, chofoza ibhokisi {{ui:Fill}}.

Ukuze ushintshe amagama kamuva: uma ithuluzi {{ui:Select}} livuliwe, chofoza kabili umbhalo, noma uwukhethe bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma khetha {{ui:Edit on the sheet}} endaweni ethi {{ui:Properties}}. Ungawuhlela nakwibhokisi {{ui:Text}} endaweni ethi {{ui:Properties}}.

## Utolo nemigqa

1. Khetha {{ui:Arrow}} ({{key:A}}) noma {{ui:Line}} ({{key:L}}).
2. Hudula kusukela ekuqaleni kuya ekupheleni. Noma chofoza ekuqaleni, khipha, bese uchofoza ekupheleni.
3. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuze ugcine kungezinyathelo ze-45°.

Ikhanda lotolo lisendaweni yokuphela lapho uqeda khona.

## Amanxande, amayelipsi, amaphola-layini nopholigoni

- {{ui:Rectangle}} ({{key:R}}) kanye no-{{ui:Ellipse}} ({{key:E}}): hudula ibhokisi. {{key:Shift}} yenza isikwele noma isiyingi. Ukuchofoza ngaphandle kokuhudula akudwetshi lutho, futhi ibha yesimo itshela ukuhudula.
- {{ui:Polyline}} ({{key:Y}}) kanye no-{{ui:Polygon}} ({{key:G}}): chofoza amaphuzu; qeda ngokuchofoza kabili, {{key:Enter}} noma ngokuchofoza ngakwesokudla (thinta kabili esikrinini sokuthinta). {{key:Backspace}} isusa iphuzu lokugcina. Ipholigoni liyazivala lodwa futhi lidinga amaphuzu amathathu; iphola-layini idinga amabili.

## Ipeni nokuqaqambisa

- {{ui:Pen}} ({{key:P}}): hudula ukudweba ngesandla. Umshayo uthambisa kancane lapho uwukhipha.
- {{ui:Highlight}} ({{key:I}}): okufanayo, kodwa ngomshayo omkhulu, ophuzi ophucuzayo, owenza umbala womdwebo ngaphansi kwawo esikhundleni sokuwumboza.
- Bamba {{key:Shift}} ngenkathi udweba ukuze uthole umshayo owodwa oqondile kusuka lapho uqale khona.

Iqaqambisi liwumshayo wokudweba ngesandla. Alibambeleli embhalweni ku-PDF.

## Umbala, umugqa nokugcwalisa

Izindlela ezimbili:

- **Ngaphambi kokudweba:** khetha ithuluzi. Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, {{ui:Properties}} ibonisa izilungiselelo zethuluzi "<ithuluzi>": {{ui:Color}}, {{ui:Line width}} (ngamaphoyinti), {{ui:Fill}} kanye {{ui:Fill opacity}} ngezimo ezivaliwe, {{ui:Opacity}}, kanye {{ui:Text size}} ombhalweni. Konke ukumaka okusha kohlobo olulodwa kusebenzisa lezi, futhi i-MarkupCraft iyazikhumbula kulesi siphequluli.
- **Ngemva kokudweba:** khetha okumakiwe okukodwa noma okuningi bese ushintsha izilawuli ezifanayo ngaphansi kokuthi "Isitayela". Uma kukhethwe okuningi, ushintsho lusebenza kukho konke ngesinyathelo esisodwa.

Izinhlobo ezizenzakalelayo: okumakiwe okubomvu, iqaqambisi eliphuzi, izilinganiso eziluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, izibalo ezioranji, nezitembu ezinemibala ngokusho kwazo.

Izindlela ozigcinayo njalo kumele zibe kwi-tool chest: bona [Ibhokisi lamathuluzi](topic:tool-chest).

## Isihloko nokuphawula

Zonke izinto ezimakiwe nazo zinendawo ethi {{ui:Subject}} (isibonakaliso esifushane, njengo "Cloud" noma "RFI", ibonakala ohlwini lokumaka nakwezinye izibukeli ze-PDF) kanye {{ui:Comment}} (amanothi amade). Yigcwalise endaweni ethi {{ui:Properties}}. Ukuze ukhulume ngokumaka, sebenzisa izimpendulo: bona [Izimo nezimpendulo](topic:statuses-and-replies).
