Khetha {{ui:Khetha}} ({{key:V}}) kuqala. Ukucindezela {{key:Esc}} nakho kukufikisa lapho: ukucindezela kokuqala kususa ukukhethwa, okulandelayo kubuyisela ku-{{ui:Khetha}}.

## Khetha amamaka

:::mouse
- **Elilodwa:** chofoza kulo. Ibhokisi elinemigqa ephukile namahhandlu liyavela, futhi {{ui:Izici}} iwubonisa.
- **Engeza noma ususe elilodwa:** bamba {{key:Shift}} uchofoze.
- **Ngokuphelele ngaphakathi kewindi:** hudula kusukela kwesokunxele kuya kwesokudla endaweni engenalutho yeshidi. Kukhethwa kuphela amamaka angaphakathi ebhokisini ngokuphelele (umugqa oqinile).
- **Ukuthinta iwindi:** hudula kusukela kwesokudla kuya kwesokunxele. Konke okuthintwa yibhokisi kuyakhethwa (uhlaza, umugqa ophukile).
- **Konke kuleshidi:** {{key:Mod+A}}, noma {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} bese {{ui:Khetha konke}} eqenjini {{ui:Hlela}} lombhalo wamathuluzi.
- **Lutho:** chofoza indawo engenalutho, cindezela {{key:Esc}}, noma {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} bese {{ui:Khetha lutho}}.
:::

:::touch
- **Elilodwa:** thinta lona.
- **Amaningi:** hudula umunwe owodwa endaweni engenalutho yeshidi ukuze udwebe iwindi. Kusuka kwesokunxele kuya kwesokudla kukhetha okungaphakathi ngokuphelele; kusuka kwesokudla kuya kwesokunxele kukhetha okuthintayo.
- Ukuthinta kanye nokubamba {{key:Shift}} kusebenza kuphela uma kuxhunywe ikhibhodi. Ngaphandle kwayo, sebenzisa iwindi.
- Umunwe owodwa awuskroli ku-{{ui:Khetha}}; sebenzisa iminwe emibili, noma ithuluzi {{ui:Shelelisa}}.
:::

Amazwana afundwayo kuphela afika nefayela abonisa ibhokisi elimpunga elinemigqa ephukile futhi alikho amahhandlu. Angakhethwa aphendulwe kuwo, kodwa awakwazi ukushintshwa.

## Hambisa nokushintsha usayizi

- **Hambisa:** hudula amamaka akhethiwe. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuhambisa kuphela ngokuphindaphinda kwama-45°. Amamaka amaningi akhethiwe ahamba ndawonye.
- **Shintsha usayizi:** nelimaka elilodwa elikhethiwe, hudula isibambelelo. Izibambelelo ezisikwele zishintsha ibhokisi (bamba {{key:Shift}} ukugcina izilinganiso zalo); izibambelelo ezingqukuqele zihambisa iphoyinti elilodwa lomugqa, i-polyline, i-polygon, ifu noma ukulinganisa (bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuze kube ama-45°); izibambelelo ezinesimo sedayimani zihambisa ithiphu nokugoba kwesimakhalekhukhwini. Amapeni angamagqa ashintsha usayizi kusuka ebhokisini layo.
- **Hambisa kancane:** cindezela izinkinobho zomcibisholo ukuhambisa ukukhethwa ngepikseli eyodwa yesikrini; bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuze kube ishumi. Ukucindezela ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

## Shintsha okushiwo noma indlela ebukeka ngayo

- **Umbhalo, izimakhalekhukhwini namanothi:** cofoza kabili kumaka, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma khetha {{ui:Hlela ehlokweni}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}. Bhala, bese uchofoza ngaphandle noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}}. {{key:Esc}} ukhansela ukuhlela.
- **Izitembu:** khetha isitembu esihlukile ku-{{ui:Izici}} (ukuchofoza kabili kuvula {{ui:Izici}} wena).
- **Isitayela:** {{ui:Umbala}}, {{ui:Ububanzi bomugqa}}, {{ui:Ukugcwalisa}}, {{ui:Ukukhanya}} kanye {{ui:Usayizi wombhalo}} ngaphansi kwe-"Isitayela" ku-{{ui:Izici}}. Uma kukhethwe amaningi, {{ui:Izici}} ibonisa izinhlobo ozikhethile futhi iziguqule zonke ngesikhathi esisodwa.

## Kopisha, namathisela nenze ikhophi

Iqembu {{ui:Hlela}} lombhalo wamathuluzi linezinkinobho {{ui:Kopisha}} no-{{ui:Namathisela}}; imenyu yalo {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} inakho konke, ngakunye nokhiye wakho.

| Umyalo | Ukhiye | Yenzani |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Sika}} | {{key:Mod+X}} | Ikopisha amamaka akhethiwe bese iwasusa |
| {{ui:Kopisha}} | {{key:Mod+C}} | Ikopisha amamaka akhethiwe |
| {{ui:Namathisela}} | {{key:Mod+V}} | Ibeka ikhophi eshidini osebenza kulo (lapho, ngezansi) |
| {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}} | {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} | Ibeka ikhophi endaweni eyodwa ngqo lapho likhishwe khona: eshidini elifanayo, phezu kwesokuqala; kwelinye ishidi, endaweni efanayo ephepheni |
| {{ui:Enza ikhophi}} | {{key:Mod+D}} | Yenza ikhophi lokukhethwa elingaphansi kancane nangakwesokudla, ngaphandle kokuthinta okukopishwe ngaphambili |

**Lapho Namathisela ibeka khona ikhophi:**

- {{key:Mod+V}} nesikhombi esisekhasini ibeka ikhophi liphakathi kwendawo esikhomba kuyo. Hambisa isikhombi, cindezela {{key:Mod+V}} futhi, bese ubeka elinye.
- Inkinobho {{ui:Namathisela}} (lapho isikhombi sisethuluzini), noma {{key:Mod+V}} nesikhombi siphuma eshidini, ibeka ikhophi incane ngaphansi nangakwesokudla kwesokuqala. Ukunamathisela ngakunye kwekhophi elifanayo kuyanyathela kancane, ngakho amakhophi awasoze afihlane. Eshidini elihlukile ukunamathisela kokuqala kufika lapho kwakhona okwakukhona khona eshidini lalo. Uma leyo ndawo ingaphandle kokubukwa, ikhophi lifika maphakathi nokubukwa.
- Ukuze {{key:Mod+V}} ihlale inamathisela eduze kwesokuqala, khetha {{ui:Eduze nekhophi}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Namathisela}} ku-[izilungiselelo ze-MarkupCraft](topic:settings).

Amamaka anamathisiwe akhethwa ngakho ungawahudula ngokushesha. Ukusika, ukunamathisela noma ukwenza ikhophi ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa: {{key:Mod+Z}} ususa ukunamathisela konke.

Amakhophi anamathisiwe ngamamaka amasha: athola igama lakho, usuku lwanamuhla, isimo Ivulekile, akekho umbala wenombolo yento yesijeziso, nethredi elingenalutho. Uhlobo lwawo, isimo, isitayela, umbhalo nezimpendulo kuyahamba nawo. Amazwana afundwayo afika nefayela awakwazi ukukopishwa noma asikwe.

**Phakathi kwamathebhu:** okukopishiwe kugcinwa nalapha kulesi siphequluli, ngakho ithebhu ye-MarkupCraft efanayo kulesi siphequluli ingakunamathisela. Ukukopisha amamaka akusebenzisi i-clipboard yekhompyutha: awukwazi ukuwanamathisela kwenye inhlelo, futhi umbhalo okopishwe kwenye indawo awunamathiseli njengamaka.

**Imenyu yokuchofoza ngesokudla:** chofoza ngesokudla kumaka (noma ephepheni elingenalutho) ukuze uthole {{ui:Sika}}, {{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Namathisela lapha}}, {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}}, {{ui:Enza ikhophi}}, {{ui:Susa}}, {{ui:Londoloza ukukhethwa njengesitembu…}}, {{ui:Cindezela ibe yikhasi lapho silondolozwa}}, {{ui:Faka isithombe lapha…}} kanye {{ui:Khetha konke}}. Ukuchofoza ngesokudla kumaka ongakakhethwa kuqala kuwukhethe. Ukuhudula ngenkinobho yesokudla kusashelelisa. Esikrinini sokuthinta, ukubamba isikhathi eside kumaka kuvula le menyu (ebhalweni, kuqala ukuhlela umbhalo). Inkinobho yemenyu noma {{key:Shift+F10}} iyivula ekhethweni.

Okuqukethwe kwekhasi okukhethwe ngethuluzi {{ui:Okuqukethwe}} nakho kuyakopishwa kunamathiselwe: bheka [Shintsha imigqa, izithombe nombhalo bekhasi](topic:page-content).

Ukukopisha ingxenye yekhasi ngokwayo njengesithombe (unxande, i-ellipse noma isimo esikhululekile), noma njengesendlalelo esibonakala ngaso kwelinye ishidi noma uhlelo, bheka [Kopisha indawo yekhasi, noma isendlalelo](topic:copy-an-area).

## Susa

Cindezela {{key:Delete}} noma {{key:Backspace}}, khetha {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} bese {{ui:Susa}} kwibha yamathuluzi, noma {{ui:Susa}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}. Ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi kususwe bangaki. Amazwana afundwayo afika nefayela awasoze asuswe ("kugcinwe njengoba kunjalo kufayela").

## Hlehlisa nophinde wenze

- {{key:Mod+Z}} iyahlehlisa, {{key:Mod+Y}} noma {{key:Mod+Shift+Z}} iyaphinda yenze. Ibha yamathuluzi inezinkinobho {{ui:Hlehlisa}} no-{{ui:Phinda wenze}}.
- Ibha yesimo itshela okuhlehliswe ("Uhlehlisile ukuhambisa kwamaka.").
- Ukuhlehlisa kuhlanganisa noshintsho lwamamaka, izimo, izimpendulo, izinto zesijeziso, izilinganiso zamashidi namagama amashidi, kuze kufike ezinyathelweni ezingu-500.
- Umlando uyasulwa uma uvala isethi.

## Kopisha amamaka kwamanye amashidi

Ukuze ufake isitembu, inothi noma ifu elifanayo kumashidi amaningi, khetha (noma amamaka amaningi) bese ukhetha {{ui:Kopisha kumanye amashidi…}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}. Thika amashidi, noma {{ui:Wonke amashidi}}, bese ukhetha {{ui:Kopisha}}. Ishidi ngalinye lithola ikhophi endaweni efanayo ekhasini, lisayinwe ngegama lakho. Ukucindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} kukodwa kususa konke.
