## Ukuthayipha ubude

Uma ulinganisa kumaphoyinti amabili, uthayipha ubude bangempela. I-MarkupCraft ifunda konke lokhu:

| Uthayipha | Kusho |
|---|---|
| `24'-0"` | amafiti angu-24 |
| `12' 6 1/2"` noma `12'-6 1/2"` | amafiti angu-12 namayintshi ayi-6 ½ |
| `12' 6` | amafiti angu-12 namayintshi ayi-6 (uphawu lwamayintshi lushiyiwe) |
| `6"` noma `1/2"` | amayintshi |
| `12.5'` noma `12.5 ft` | amafiti angu-12 ½ |
| `3 ft 6 in` | amafiti ayi-3 namayintshi ayi-6 (kuphinde kwamukelwe i-feet, foot, inch, inches) |
| `3.5 m`, `90 cm`, `900 mm` | metric (kuphinde kwamukelwe meter, metre, millimeter, centimetre…) |
| `24` | inani elingenalo igama, ngeyunithi okhethwe ku-{{ui:Bonisa ubude nge-}} |

Izinhlamvu zokugoqa (’ ”) kanye namaphoyinti amabili (\'\') wamayintshi nazo ziyasebenza. Ubude obungu-zero nobungaphansi kwezero bayenqatshwa.

:::warning
Uphawu lwekhoma lufundwa njengephoyinti ledesimali: `1,5 m` kungu-1.5 mitha. Ungabhali izihlukanisi zezinkulungwane: `1,200 mm` kufundwa njengo-1.2 millimitha. Thayipha `1200 mm`.
:::

## Amasikali amiswe kusengaphambili

Amasikali amiswe kusengaphambili asho ukuthi i-PDF ingobukhulu obugcwele obuphrintiwe: amaphoyinti angu-72 e-PDF ngeyintshi, njengoba uhlelo lwe-CAD lukuphlotha.

| Iqembu | Amasikali | Ubude obubonisiwe nge |
|---|---|---|
| Ezokwakha | 1/32" = 1'-0", 1/16", 3/32", 1/8", 3/16", 1/4", 3/8", 1/2", 3/4", 1", 1 1/2", 3" = 1'-0" | Amafiti namainshi |
| Obunjiniyela | 1" = 10', 20', 30', 40', 50', 60', 100' | Amafidi ngedesimali |
| Metric | 1:1, 1:5, 1:10, 1:20, 1:25, 1:50 | Millimitha |
| Metric | 1:100, 1:200, 1:250, 1:500, 1:1000 | Mitha |

Ukuze usebenzise isikali esimiswe kusengaphambili kodwa ubone ubude kwenye iyunithi, linganisa kumaphoyinti amabili, bese ukhetha iyunithi lapho.

## Amasikali okonge ezinye izinhlelo elawalondoloza

Uma i-PDF isivele iphethe isikali sekhasi kusuka kwenye inhlelo (isikali sokulinganisa esigcinwe ekhasini), i-MarkupCraft iyasisebenzisa uma sisemafitini, emayintshini, emamitheni, emasentimitheni noma emamilimithini. Ilebula layo ibonakala njengoba ifayela lisho.

## Izikhundla zephepha

Ibha yesimo ibonisa lapho inkomba ikhona ephepheni, ngamayintshi ukusukela ekhoneni elingaphezulu lesobunxele leshidi, noma yisiphi isikali seshidi. Izinyathelo zokushintsha ze-Compare zisekelwe kumaphoyinti (amaphoyinti angu-72 ngeyintshi).

## Ukunemba

Isilinganiso sincike ekulinganiseni nasekuchofozeni kwakho. Linganisa ngobude obude obuwazi kakhulu, sondeza ukuze uchofoze izinhlangothi zawo, bese uhlola esinye isilinganiso. I-PDF eshintshwe isikali lapho idalwa (ukulingana nekhasi noma isethi enesigamu sobukhulu) isasebenza ngemva kokulinganisa kusuka esilinganisweni, kodwa hhayi nge-preset.
