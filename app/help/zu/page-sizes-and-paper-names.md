Ngaphambi kokuthi isethi iye kukliyenti, hlola ukuthi yonke ishidi ingusayizi okufanele ube wona. {{ui:MarkupCraft}} ifunda usayizi wekhasi ngalinye kusuka ku-PDF ngokwayo futhi iqamba iphepha.

## Lokho ozokwenza

Bheka usayizi weshidi elilodwa, bese uhlela sonke isethi ngosayizi.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula ama-PDF ({{ui:Vula}}), bese ucindezela {{key:Esc}} kuze kungakhethwa lutho futhi {{ui:Khetha}} ivuliwe.
2. Bheka {{ui:Izici}}. {{ui:Iphepha}} likhomba igama nendlela ephezulu, isibonelo "A4, ukuma". {{ui:Usayizi}} unikeza amamilimitha namainshi.
3. Umugqa ngamunye wohlu {{ui:Amashidi}} unomugqa wosayizi ngaphansi kwegama leshidi.
4. Khetha {{ui:Ngosayizi}} ekhanda le-{{ui:Amashidi}}. Uhlu lubutha amashidi ngaphansi kwesihloko esisodwa ngosayizi ngamunye, iqembu elikhulu kuqala. Usayizi onamashidi ambalwa kuphawulwa njengongavamile.
5. Khetha {{ui:Ngosayizi}} futhi ukuze ubuyele ohlelweni lwesethi lwangempela.

Umphumela: ikhasi elihlukile ngosayizi kwabanye liyalula ukulibona ngaphambi kokuthumela isethi.

## Indlela igama elikhethwa ngayo

- Usayizi uvela kubhokisi lekhasi ku-PDF nokujikeleza kusetshenziswe, ngakho i-A3 evundlile isaba yi-A3.
- Amagama ngu-uchungechunge lwe-A nolwe-B (ISO 216), uchungechunge lwe-C (ISO 269), i-ANSI kusuka ku-A kuya ku-E nengu-ARCH kusuka ku-A kuya ku-E.
- Ikhasi elingaphakathi kuka-2 mm kusayizi obizwa ngegama lithola leligama. Noma yikuphi okunye kuthi {{ui:Okwenziwe ngokwezifiso}}, futhi {{ui:Usayizi okuseduze kakhulu obizwa ngegama}} ukhomba usayizi osondele kakhulu nokuthi ukhashana kangakanani.
- Amainshi avela kusayizi wekhasi langempela, ngakho ikhasi le-A4 lingu-8.26 by 11.69 in.

## Kumafayela oyawagcinayo

Ibhukhu lesifinyezo ({{ui:Uhlu lokumaka}}, bese i-spreadsheet) linekholomu {{ui:Usayizi wephepha}} kwishidi {{ui:Ngeshidi}} nomsebenzi {{ui:Usayizi wamakhasi}}. Umbiko we-PDF unomugqa othi "Usayizi wamakhasi" onenani losayizi ngamunye.

:::note
Amanani amainshi e-ANSI ne-ARCH athathwe kumatafula ashicilelwe, futhi awakaqhathaniswa namazinga ashicilelwe. Khipha ikhasi "elenziwe ngokwezifiso" elisondele kwelinye lawo njengento yokuhlola kwakho.
:::
