Kuseshini ye-Live ungenza ukubuka kwakho kulandele okomunye umsebenzisi, njengothisha ehamba nekilasi eshidini. Lesi sihloko sikhuluma ngokulandela; ukuqala nokujoyina iseshini kwenziwa ku-CadCraft Studio, bona [Buyekeza isethi eyodwa yamapulani ndawonye ne-Live TeamWork](topic:work-live).

## Okuzoyenza

Landela umuntu oyedwa, yeka ukulandela, futhi (njengomphathi) cela wonke umuntu ukuthi akulandele.

## Izinyathelo

1. Kuseshini ye-Live, vula uhlu lwabantu. Lubonisa umbala negama lalowo ngamunye.
2. Khetha {{ui:Landela}} eceleni kwegama lomuntu ozomlandela. Umugqa wesimo ufunda {{ui:Ilandela}} negama lakhe, futhi ukubuka kwakho kunyakaza nokwakhe.
3. Ukuze uyeke, khetha {{ui:Yeka ukulandela}}, noma nje shelelisa noma usondeze ukubuka kwakho: ukulandela kuyeka ngokwako.
4. Njengomphathi, khetha {{ui:Cela wonke umuntu andilandele}} ukuze icele iqembu lonke ukuthi likulandele. Khetha {{ui:Yeka ukucela wonke umuntu ukuthi andilandele}} ukuze ikuphelise.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ngamunye ubonisa ishidi ayikuso nokuthi ukuhlelwa kwakhe kokugcina kwenzeka nini, ukuze uthisha abone ukuthi ubani oqhubekile.
- Uma umuntu olandelwayo esebukeni elingalandelwa, noma kweminye ishidi, umugqa wesimo utshela lapho ekhona.
- Ukulandela kushintsha ukubuka kwakho kuphela. Akukho okulondolozwa ku-fayela.
- Ukubuka kwekilasi akusazami nothisha nabafundi bangempela.
