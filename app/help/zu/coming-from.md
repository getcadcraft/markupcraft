Uma ubuyekeza imidwebo ku-Bluebeam Revu (noma uphawula kuma-PDF ohlelweni olunye lwedeskithophu lwe-PDF), iningi le-MarkupCraft lizokwazeka: amafu, izichazi, ichibi lamathuluzi, uhlu lwezimakiwe, izimo ezinezimpendulo, izilinganiso ezilinganisiwe, ama-overlay eshidi. Indlela okuphathwa ngayo amafayela umehluko omkhulu, ngakho-ke funda isigaba esilandelayo nakuba ungadlula kokunye.

## Imehluko emihlanu ebalulekile

1. **Ukulondoloza kwenza ifayela elisha.** I-MarkupCraft isebenza kusisheshi futhi ayikwazi ukubhala phezu kwe-PDF ekhompyutheni yakho. {{ui:Londoloza}} idawunilodela "<name> (marked up).pdf" futhi okokuqala kuhlala kunjalo. Ngesikhathi esilandelayo, vula ikhophi "(marked up)", futhi uhlanze amakhophi amadala ngokwakho.
2. **I-PDF ilondolozwa uma ulondoloza.** Kuze kube yilapho uyilondoloza, ikhopi yokubuyisela kulesi sisheshi igcina izimakiwe zakho lapho kwenzeka ukuphahlazeka noma ithebhu evaliwe, futhi i-MarkupCraft iyakuphakamisela kulesi esilandelayo. Iwukuvikela okuphephile: londoloza i-PDF ukuze ugcine ukubuyekezwa.
3. **Iphrofayili yakho ihlala kusisheshi.** Igama lakho lombhali, izilungiselelo zamathuluzi nechibi lamathuluzi kugcinwa kulesi sisheshi kule komputha. Isisheshi esihlukile noma ikhompyutha ehlukile iqala kusha, futhi ukusula idatha yesayithi kuyasisusa. Thumela ichibi lamathuluzi njengesipele. Bheka [Okuhlala kulesi sisheshi](topic:browser-storage).
4. **Azikho izikhathi ezabelwanwe okwamanje.** Akukho sikhathi sokubuyekeza esabelwana ngaso ngesikhathi sangempela, futhi akukho iphrojekthi yefu. Ukubuyekezwa kuhamba ku-PDF: londoloza, thumela ikhophi, umuntu olandelayo uphendula futhi alondoloze.
5. **Isikali sisekelwa ngeshidi, futhi ngeshidi kuphela.** Azikho ama-viewport ngaphakathi kweshidi futhi azikho "faka lesi sikali kuzo zonke izinkasa". Linganisa ishidi ngalinye obalinganisa kulo.

## Okhiye abahlukile

Okhiye abaningi bohlamvu abodwa bavumelana nokulindele, kodwa hlola lokhu ngaphambi kokuthi iminwe yakho ikwenze ngawe:

- {{key:K}} ingu-{{ui:Isichazi}}, futhi {{key:Q}} ingu-{{ui:Indawo}}. Uma ujwayele ukusebenzisa Q ezichazini, lapha iqala indawo.
- {{key:C}} ingu-{{ui:Ifu}} lebhokisi; {{key:Shift+C}} ingu-{{ui:Ifu elikhululekile}}.
- {{key:M}} ingu-{{ui:Ubude}}, {{key:U}} ingu-{{ui:Bala}}, {{key:Y}} ingu-{{ui:Iphola-layini}}, {{key:G}} ingu-{{ui:Ipholigoni}}, {{key:I}} ingu-{{ui:Qaqambisa}}.
- {{key:Mod+D}} ikopisha okukhethiwe (esikhundleni sesimaki sesisheshi).
- {{key:Mod+Plus}} no {{key:Mod+Minus}} zisondeza ishidi, hhayi ikhasi lesisheshi.
- Noma iyiphi inkinobho ingashintshwa: bheka ngezansi.

:::tip
Uma iminwe yakho ijwayele i-Bluebeam Revu, khetha isitayela semenyu {{ui:Ukubuyekezwa kwe-PDF}} ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:MarkupCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}: ibha yemenyu (Ifayela, Hlela, Bona, Idokhumenti, Ukumaka, Linganisa, Iwindi) ngaphezu komugqa wamathuluzi okubuyekeza, {{key:Q}} ye-callout, {{key:H}} ye-highlighter, {{key:N}} ye-polygon, nezilinganiso ku-{{key:Alt+Shift}} nohlamvu. Noma iyiphi inkinobho ingashintshwa lapho futhi. Bona [Imenyu, okhiye nemouse](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse).
:::

Uhlu olugcwele lutholakala ku-[Ikhibhodi nemouse](topic:keyboard).

## Izinto ezisebenza ngendlela ehlukile kunalokho ongalindele

- **Amathuluzi ahlala evuliwe.** Ngemva kwe-cloud, usadweba amafu. {{key:Esc}} kanye isula ukukhetha, kabili ibuyela ku-{{ui:Khetha}}.
- **Umbhalo namachazi kudinga umbhalo.** Qeda ungathayiphi lutho futhi akukho makiwe ongeziwe. Amanothi agcinwa engenalutho.
- **Izilinganiso zidinga isikali kuqala.** {{ui:Ubude}} no {{ui:Indawo}} zishintshela ku-{{ui:Linganisa}} eshidini elingalinganiswanga bese liyabuya ngemva.
- **Amazwana avela kwezinye izinhlelo.** Amanxande, ama-ellipse, imigqa, ama-polyline, ama-polygon, ipeni, amabhokisi ombhalo nezinothi ezincane ezenziwe kwenye indawo ziba yizimakiwe ze-MarkupCraft ezihleleka, futhi ziphinde zibhalwe ngendlela ye-MarkupCraft lapho ulondoloza (ukubukeka kwazo kungashintsha kancane). Ukugqamisa umbhalo, izitembu zezinye izinhlelo nezinye izinhlobo ezikhethekile zihlala zingafundwa kuphela: ungaphendula kuzo, kodwa awukwazi ukuzishintsha.
- **Izinhlamvu ngaphandle kwezinhlamvu zaseNtshonalanga Yurophu** zikhipha "?" kwi-PDF elondoloziwe. Bheka [Londoloza i-PDF emakiwe](topic:save-the-marked-up-pdf).
- **Izinsuku zesitembu** zisebenzisa i-UTC, ngakho isitembu esenziwe kusihlwa kakhulu e-Americas siyakhombisa usuku lwangomuso.
- **Izilinganiso ziyadlulisa** njengamazwana ajwayelekile wokulinganisa, futhi isheshi ngalinye elilinganisiwe ligcina isikali salo, ngakho uhlelo oluyifunda ilinganiso le-PDF lujwayele ukukwazi ukubonisa. Ukuziphatha ngokuqondile kuncike kulolo hlelo.

## Ukusebenzisa zombili

Ungaya phambili nasemuva. Amazwana enziwe kwenye indawo avula ku-MarkupCraft, futhi i-PDF "(marked up)" ivula kwezinye izinhlelo ze-PDF nazo zonke izimakiwe, izimpendulo nezimo zibonakala. Okufundwa yi-MarkupCraft kuphela (umuntu ophethe nosuku lokuphela kwento yohlu lokulungisa, isimo sento) kugcinwa efayeleni le-MarkupCraft.
