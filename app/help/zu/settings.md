Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ngegiya elisebhange elingaphezulu, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+,}}. Ithebhu yokuqala, {{ui:MarkupCraft}}, ibamba izilungiselelo zalolu hlelo kuphela; {{ui:Jikelele}} ibamba ezabelwa wonke amahlelo e-CadCraft (itimu, ibha yamathuluzi, amapaneli). Izilungiselelo ze-MarkupCraft zikhona kuphela kuhlelo: uzishintsha lapha, futhi lesi siphequluli siyazikhumbula (bheka [Okugcinwa kulesi siphequluli](topic:browser-storage)). Ushintsho lusebenza ngokushesha; akukho okudingeka ukugcinwa.

## Igama lakho

{{ui:Igama lokumaka}} yigama elizovela kuyo yonke imimaka nempendulo entsha, nalokho amaPDF alondoloziwe akubonisa njengombhali wezwana. Liyigama elifanayo nenkinobho esekugcineni kwesokudla sebha yesimo; shintsha noma kuphi. Amamaka asevele adwetshiwe agcina igama ayenziwa ngalo. Bheka [Setha igama lakho lombhali](topic:set-your-name).

## Amamaka amasha

- **{{ui:Umbala wokumaka}}** ngumbala amapeni amasha, imigqa, izinkomba, izindandatho, ama-ellipse, ama-polyline, ama-polygon, amafu, izimakhalekhukhwini nombhalo aqala ngawo (obomvu ekuqaleni). Izibhalo zokukhanyisa, amanothi, izitembu, ubude, izindawo nezinani zigcina imibala yazo, ngoba imibala yazo ichaza ukuthi zinjani.
- **{{ui:Imigqa emincane}}, {{ui:Ejwayelekile}}, {{ui:Imigqa emikhulu}}** zenza imigqa yalawa mamaka ibe mincane ngesigamu, njengoba ijwayelekile, noma ibe mikhulu ngesigamu esengeziwe.

Umbala noma ububanzi bomugqa okukhetha ngethuluzi elilodwa ku-{{ui:Izici}} (lapho ithuluzi livulekile futhi kungekho okukhethiwe) buyanqoba lezi. Amathuluzi asuka ku-[ibhokisi lamathuluzi](topic:tool-chest) agcina isitayela sawo. Amamaka asevele adwetshiwe awashintshi: wakhethe bese uwashintsha ku-{{ui:Izici}}.

## Namathisela

Lapho {{key:Mod+V}} ibeka khona okukopishiwe:

- **{{ui:Esikhombini}}** (okuqala): isikhombi sisesikrinini, ikhophi ifika ibekwe maphakathi nalapho sikhomba khona. Uma isikhombi sisendaweni yonye, ifika eduze kwesokuqala.
- **{{ui:Eduze nekhophi}}**: njalo incane ngaphansi nangakwesokudla kwesokuqala, inyathela kancane ngakunye ukunamathisela.

Inkinobho {{ui:Namathisela}} ne-{{key:Mod+Shift+V}} ({{ui:Namathisela endaweni}}) zisebenza ngendlela efanayo nakukhethwa lolu khetho. Bheka [Kopisha, namathisela nenze ikhophi](topic:select-and-edit#copy-paste-and-duplicate).

## Izilinganiso

- **{{ui:Bonisa ubude nge-}}** yiyunithi ibhokisi lesikali seshidi elinikeza kuqala uma ukalibeka ngamaphoyinti amabili: izinyawo namairashi, izinyawo ezinezinhlamvu zedesimali, amairashi, amamitha noma amamilimitha. Amashidi owawakalibe ngaphambili agcina iyunithi yawo. Bheka [Kalibrela ishidi bese ulinganisa](topic:calibrate-and-measure).
- **{{ui:Indawo eshidini}}** ikhetha ama-intshi noma amamilimitha wesikhundla somlotha ongaphansi kwesokunxele sesikrini. Kuyisikhundla ephepheni, hhayi ibanga langempela.

## Setha kabusha

{{ui:Setha kabusha izilungiselelo ze-MarkupCraft}} ngaphansi kwebhokisi lengxoxo ibuyisela lezi zilungiselelo, nezilungiselelo zamapaneli nebha yamathuluzi ku-{{ui:Jikelele}}, ezindaweni zazo zokuqala. Igcina igama lakho, ngoba lichaza wena hhayi indlela othanda ngayo ukusebenza, futhi igcina imibala okhethe ngayo ephaneli yethuluzi ngalinye.
