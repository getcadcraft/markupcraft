:::mac
**Ku-trackpad:** ukuskrola ngeminwe emibili kusondeza. Ukucindezela nakho kusondeza ku-Chrome, Edge ne-Firefox. Shelelisa ngokubamba {{key:Space}} uhudule, noma ngethuluzi {{ui:Pan}}. Ukuchofoza ngeminwe emibili kuyinkinobho yesokudla futhi kuqeda iphola-layini. Ukuchofoza nge-Control lapha kungasebenza njengokuchofoza okujwayelekile esikhundleni sokuchofoza ngakwesokudla, ngakho sebenzisa ukuchofoza ngeminwe emibili.
:::

## Izinqamuleli esiphequluli sizigcinayo

- {{key:Mod+W}}, {{key:Mod+T}}, {{key:Mod+N}} no-{{key:Mod+Q}} bungokwesiphequluli. Ukuvala ithebhu noma iwindi ngokukhumbula okungalondolozwanga kulahlekelwa yikho, ngemva kombuzo "Shiya isayithi?" wesiphequluli kwikhompyutha.
- Iziphequluli eziningi zigcina {{key:Mod+N}} ewindini elisha, ngakho ethebhini lesiphequluli sebenzisa {{ui:New}} kwibha yamathuluzi; i-app yasedeskithophu ne-Studio idlulisela lo khiye ku-MarkupCraft.
- I-MarkupCraft ithatha {{key:Mod+O}}, {{key:Mod+S}}, {{key:Mod+D}}, {{key:Mod+A}}, {{key:Mod+V}}, {{key:Mod+Shift+V}}, {{key:Mod+0}}, {{key:Mod+Plus}} no-{{key:Mod+Minus}} kusiphequluli uma i-app inekhibhodi, kanye no-{{key:Mod+C}} ne-{{key:Mod+X}} uma kukhethiwe ukumaka. {{key:Mod+,}} ivula {{ui:Settings}}. Ukuze usondeze ikhasi lonke lesiphequluli, sebenzisa imenyu yesiphequluli.
- {{key:Mod+9}} kwezinye iziphequluli ifika kuthebhu yokugcina yesiphequluli ngaphambi kokuthi i-MarkupCraft iyibone. Uma kwenzeka lokho, sebenzisa {{ui:Fit width}} ngaphansi kweshidi.
- {{key:Esc}} esikrinini esigcwele nayo iphuma esikrinini esigcwele.

## Uma ukhiye ungenzi lutho

- **Usabhala ebhokisini.** Chofoza ephepheni kuqala. (Ukuchofoza ngethuluzi lokudweba kudweba; cindezela {{key:Esc}} ngemva kwalokho noma sebenzisa {{key:Mod+Z}}.)
- **Ingxoxo ivuliwe.** Okhiye baya engxoxweni kuze kuvale.
- **Usanda kuchofoza inkinobho yebha yamathuluzi,** futhi okhiye bemitolo noma {{key:Space}} basebenza kuleyo nkinobho esikhundleni salo. Hambisa isikhombi phezu kwephepha usebenzise ivili noma inkinobho ephakathi, noma cindezela inhlamvu yethuluzi esikhundleni sokuchofoza izinkinobho.

## Lapho kuhlala khona ukuhambisa

Alikho ikhomanda yokuhambisa: khetha ukumaka uwuhudule ({{key:Shift}} igcina izinyathelo ze-45°). Ipini lihanjiswa ngomyalo {{ui:Move pin}}, nekhasi ngomyalo {{ui:Move page earlier}} noma {{ui:Move page later}}.
