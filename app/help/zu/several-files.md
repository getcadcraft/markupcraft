Isethi yamapulani ivame ukufika njenge-PDF eyodwa ngesifunda ngasinye, noma i-PDF eyodwa ngeshidi ngalinye. I-MarkupCraft ibona konke oyivulayo njengesethi **eyodwa**.

## Vula ndawonye

Khetha wonke ama-PDF kwisikhethi sefayela ngesikhathi esisodwa, noma ulahle wonke endaweni yamashidi. Ukungeza amanye kamuva, vula ngendlela efanayo; ajoyina ekugcineni kohlu lwe-{{ui:Amashidi}} ngolandelelano owavula ngalo.

Umphumela: "Ivule amafayela angu-6 (amashidi angu-84 esethini)." Wonke amakhasi ngamashidi. Amashidi anenombolo yeshidi abonisa "Ishidi A-101" ngaphansi kwegama lawo; amanye abonisa ifayela nekhasi.

Akukho ifayela "lesethi" elilondoloziwe: ngesikhathi esilandelayo, vula ama-PDF afanayo futhi (amakhophi "(marked up)", uma ulondoloze).

## Kuso sonke isethi

- **Thola ishidi:** {{ui:Sesha amashidi}} ifuna ezinombolweni zamashidi, emagameni amashidi nasemagameni wamafayela kuwo wonke amafayela. {{key:PageUp}} ne-{{key:PageDown}} zihamba kuwo wonke amashidi wawo wonke amafayela ngolandelelano.
- **Uhlu lwamamaka:** ithebhu ye-{{ui:Okumakiwe}} ibala amamaka kuwo wonke amafayela. Hlunga nge-{{ui:Ishidi}} ukuze uwunciphise. I-CSV inekholomu ethi "File".
- **Uhlu lwezinto zesijeziso:** ukunumbeka kunye kwesethi yonke.
- **Qhathanisa:** ishidi elidala nelisha lingavela kumafayela ahlukene.
- **Kopisha nenamathisele:** {{key:Mod+C}} kweshidi elilodwa bese {{key:Mod+V}} kwelinye, kunoma yiliphi ifayela. Ikhophi lifika endaweni efanayo ekhasini, lishintshwe kancane.

## Londoloza

Khetha {{ui:Londoloza}}. Uma kuvulwe amafayela amaningi, i-MarkupCraft iyabuza:

- {{ui:Lelifayela}}: ikhophi elimakiwe lefayela leshidi osebenza kulo.
- {{ui:Wonke amafayela ashintshiwe}}: ikhophi lefayela ngalinye elinoshintsho olungalondolozwanga. Ngalinye lilandelwa ngokwahlukana njengo "<file name> (marked up).pdf".
- {{ui:Khansela}}

:::chromium
Ukulondoloza kokuqala kwamafayela amaningi ngesikhathi esisodwa, i-Chrome ne-Edge zingabuza ukuthi lesi sayithi singalanda amafayela amaningi yini. Khetha {{ui:Vumela}}; ngaphandle kwalokho kufika ikhophi lokuqala kuphela.
:::

:::safari
I-Safari ingabuza kanye ngefayela ngalinye. Ku-iPad ikhophi ngalinye liya ku-app ethi Files.
:::

Ifayela lapho amamaka alondoloziwe liyeka ukubalwa njengelishintshiwe; amanye asalokhu eshintshiwe.

Ukukhishwa kwe-CSV, BCF nohlu lwezinto zesijeziso kubizwa ngegama lokuqala ofake kuvulwe ngalo.

## Vala

{{ui:Vala}} ivala isethi yonke; akukho ndlela yokuvala ifayela elilodwa okwamanje. Uma kukhona okungalondolozwanga, kubuza:

- {{ui:Khansela}}: akukho okwenzekayo.
- {{ui:Vala ingalondolozwanga}}: amamaka angalondolozwanga ayalahleka.
- {{ui:Londoloza amakhophi kuqala}}: ilondoloza wonke amafayela ashintshiwe, bese iyavala.

Ukuvala kuphinde kususe umlando wokuhlehlisa.

## Amasethi amakhulu

- Umbhalo wawo wonke amakhasi ufundwa ekuvulweni ukuze kutholakale izinombolo zamashidi, ngakho isethi yamashidi angu-100 ithatha isikhathi. Ibha yesimo ibala amashidi njengoba iqhubeka.
- Izithombe zamashidi kuhlu zidwetshwa njengoba uskrola kuzo.
- I-PDF ngayinye evulekile igcinwa enkumbulweni yethebhu yesiphequluli. Uma ithebhu ihamba kancane, vala amanye amathebhu anzima, noma buyekeza idiphathimenti elilodwa ngesikhathi.
- Ukuvula ifayela elifanayo kabili kukunika amakhophi amabili amashidi awo. Uma kwenzeka lokho, khetha {{ui:Vala}} bese uvula isethi kabusha.
