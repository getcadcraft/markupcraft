Into yohlu lokulungisa iyimaki enenombolo, umuntu obelwe umsebenzi, usuku lokuphela nesimo sento. Inombolo idwetshwe ekhasini eduze kwemaki, ngakho ishidi elishicilelwe nohlu kuvumelana.

## Engeza izinto

1. Phawula izinkinga ezisheethini: ifu, isichazi, inothi, noma yini.
2. Khetha ezisodwa noma eziningi (bona [Khetha, hambisa nishintshe amamaki](topic:select-and-edit)).
3. Vula ithebhu ye-{{ui:Uhlu lokulungisa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza ezikhethiwe kuhlu lokulungisa}}, noma khetha {{ui:Engeza ohlwini lwe-punch}} e-{{ui:Izici}}.

Umphumela: imaki ngayinye ithola inombolo elandelayo (1, 2, 3…), ngokuhleleka kwamashidi bese ngokulandelana ngako okwadwetswa ngakho. Ibheji enenombolo ivela ekhasini, futhi ibha yesimo ithi "Kungeziwe izinto ezi-3 kuhlu lokulungisa." Amamaki anenombolo ngaphambili, namakhomenti afundeka kuphela, ayadlulwa.

## Gcwalisa imininingwane

Ku-ithebhu ye-{{ui:Uhlu lokulungisa}}, into ngayinye ibonisa inombolo yayo, isihloko, umbhalo, ishidi nesimo, nezinkundla ezintathu ngaphansi kwayo. Lezo zinkundla zisendaweni efanayo ye-{{ui:Izici}} ngaphansi kwe-"{{ui:Uhlu lokulungisa}}" uma imaki ikhethiwe:

- {{ui:Umuntu obelwe umsebenzi}}: igama noma umsebenzi, njengombhalo wamahhala.
- {{ui:Isikhathi sokuphela}}: usuku, kusuka kukhethi losuku.
- {{ui:Isimo sento}}: Ivulekile, Iqhubeka, Ilungele ukubuyekezwa noma Ivaliwe.

Chofoza ikhanda lento ukuze uye kuyo eshidini layo.

Isimo sento sihlukile kwisimo sokubuyekezwa semaki (Ivulekile, Yamukelwe, Yenqatshiwe, Iqedile). Sebenzisa isimo sento womsebenzi, nesimo sokubuyekezwa wekhomenti.

## Susa into

Khetha imaki bese ukhetha {{ui:Susa ohlwini lwe-punch}} e-{{ui:Izici}}. Imaki ihlala; inombolo kuphela iyasuka. Isethi ivulekile, izinombolo azisetshenziswa kabusha: into elandelayo ithola inombolo entsha elandelayo, ngakho ukuphuka kwenombolo kusho ukuthi into isusiwe. Uma ifayela livulwa futhi, ukunombolwa kuqhubeka ngemva kwenombolo ephezulu kuwo.

## Thumela uhlu

Khetha {{ui:Thumela i-CSV}} ku-ithebhu ye-{{ui:Uhlu lokulungisa}}. Uthola "<name> punch list.csv" namakholomu No., Sheet, File, Page, Item, Comment, Assignee, Due, Status, Markup status, Author and Created, ohlwini lwenombolo. Vula kunoma yiluphi uhlelo lwamashidi.

Izinombolo zohlu lokulungisa, abantu ababelwe imisebenzi, izinsuku zokuphela nezimo zento nazo zigcinwa ngaphakathi kwe-PDF enamamaki, ngakho ziyabuya uma uyivula futhi. Abanye ababoni be-PDF babonisa ibheji yenombolo njengengxenye yemaki. Ukuthunyelwa kwe-BCF kohlu lwamamaki nakho kuthwala umuntu obelwe umsebenzi nosuku lokuphela (bona [Izisekelo ze-BCF](topic:bcf-basics)).

## Esizeni ngethebhulethi

Vula isethi ku-iPad, uhambe umsebenzi, bese wehlisa {{ui:Inothi}} noma udwebe {{ui:Ifu}} endaweni ngayinye enkingeni. Yengeza kuhlu lokulungisa njengoba uhamba, bese ulondoloza ngaphambi kokuhamba. Bona [Bheka ku-iPad noma kusikrini esithintwayo](topic:review-on-a-tablet).

Uhlu lokulungisa njengombiko we-PDF noma wamashidi olungiselelwe, nezithombe ezinamathiselwe ezintweni, akukakhiwa okwamanje.
