Amanothi akho okusebenza akudingi ukuba abe edrowingini noma afinyelele kumakhasimende. Kukhona izinhlobo ezintathu zosizo lapha, ungazisebenzisa ndawonye.

## Ibhodi lokusebenza

Isikhala esizungeze ishidi siyibhodi lokusebenza. Noma yini odwebayo noma unamathisela lapho ibalwa njengenothi, ngakho ayiphrintwa.

1. Vula {{ui:Amanothi}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Bonisa ibhodi lokusebenza}} ukuze usondele ngaphandle unesikhala esizungeze ishidi.
2. Dweba, thayipha noma namathisela eceleni kweshidi nganoma yiliphi ithuluzi.
3. Ukuze ugcine ingxenye yedrowingi ibonakale, khetha {{ui:Thatha isithombe sibe ebhodini lokusebenza}}, hudula phezu kwendawo, bese ukhetha {{ui:Ebhodini lokusebenza}}. Isithombe sifika eceleni kweshidi ngosayizi waso ephepheni.

Izithombe ziyizithombe: azishintshi uma ishidi lishintsha.

## Amanothi owenza ukuthi abonakale futhi afihleke

Inothi yimaki efiha futhi ibonakale ngeswishi eyodwa.

- Ukwenza amamaki amasha abe amanothi, setha {{ui:Izimaki ezintsha ziba}} e-{{ui:Amanothi}}.
- Ukushintsha imaki eyodwa, yikhethe bese usebenzisa {{ui:Lesi simaki siba}} e-{{ui:Izici}}.
- Ukufihla noma ukubonisa onke amanothi, cindezela **Shift+H** noma usebenzise {{ui:Amanothi}} ebhalweni ngaphansi kweshidi.

Amanothi awaphrintwa, ayabekwa ngaphandle kwamakhophi aqinisiwe, kuhlu, kumafingqo nakokuthunyelwa, futhi abanye abafundi be-PDF awawabonisi. I-PDF yakho egciniwe iwagcina ukuze uwathole ngesikhathi esilandelayo uyivula. Noma ubani ovula leli fayela ku-MarkupCraft angabona amanothi alo, ngakho sebenzisa inothi elingasese kunoma yini okufanele wena kuphela ukuyifunda.

## Amanothi angasese

Inothi elingasese libethelwe ngephasiwedi owaziyo wena kuphela. Akekho omunye ongalifunda kunoma yiyiphi indlela yokubuka.

1. E-{{ui:Amanothi}}, khetha {{ui:Setha iphasiwedi}}.
2. Setha {{ui:Izimaki ezintsha ziba}} ku-{{ui:Inothi elingasese}}, noma shintsha imaki e-{{ui:Izici}}.

Kwamanye amadivayisi akho, vula ifayela bese uvula ngephasiwedi efanayo. Abasebenzi bakho bangavula bese belondoloza ifayela ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa amanothi akho, futhi akukho okubatshela ukuthi kukhona inothi elingasese.

**Akukho ndlela yokubuyisa iphasiwedi elikhohliwe.** Akekho, kuhlanganise nathi, ongavula amanothi akho angasese ngaphandle kwayo.

Amanothi angasese awathunyelwa kubantu oku-seshini ephilayo noma kubasizi, futhi awakwazi ukukopishelwa kubhodi lokukopisha. Ekhompyutheni eyabiwe, shiya {{ui:Hlala uvuliwe kule divayisi}} ingasetshenzisiwe, bese usebenzisa {{ui:Vala}} uma usuhamba. Lapho ivuliwe, noma ubani osebenzisa ikhompyutha yakho angababona.

Ikhophi yangaphambili yefayela olilondoloze ngaphambi kokwenza imaki ibe yangasese isengaba nalowo maki kuwo. Thumela ikhophi entsha, hhayi leyo endala.

## Ukuthumela ifayela komunye umuntu

Khetha {{ui:Londoloza ikhophi ehlanzekile}} e-{{ui:Amanothi}}, noma {{ui:Ifayela}}, bese {{ui:Amakhasi}}. Ikhophi igcina amamaki akho ajwayelekile futhi ayinawo amanothi, awanaso amanothi angasese, futhi ayinalutho oluvela kubhodi lokusebenza. Ukuphrinta namakhophi aqinisiwe nawo ayawabeka ngaphandle.
