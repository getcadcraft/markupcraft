Iningi lezinkinga lichazwa ebhaleni lesimo elingaphansi kwesikrini. Liqale ulifunde.

## Ukuvula amafayela

**"I-MarkupCraft ivula amafayela e-PDF. "x" akuyona i-PDF."**
Ukhethe noma wehlisa enye into. Kuphela ama-PDF avulwa. Khipha imidwebo kuhlelo lwakho lwe-CAD njenge-PDF kuqala.

**'"igama" lidinga iphasiwedi ukuze livulwe. Vula ikhophi engavikelekile.'**
I-PDF ivikelwe ngephasiwedi. Cela okuthumele ikhophi engavikelekile, noma uyivule kuhlelo lwe-PDF lapho uyazi iphasiwedi bese ulondoloza ikhophi ngaphandle kwephasiwedi.

**'Akukwazanga ukufunda "igama" njenge-PDF (…)'**
Ifayela lonakele noma akusiyo i-PDF ngempela (ukulanda okungaphelele, i-imeyili enamathiselwe eqhekekile). Liphinde ulilande. Uma ezinye izinhlelo ze-PDF zingalivula, sebenzisa i-"save as" yazo ukwenza ikhophi ehlanzekile bese uvula leyo.

**"Amanye amafayela awavulekanga"**
Uma uvula amaningi ngesikhathi esisodwa, lo bhokisi ulukhombisa amafayela ahlulekile nesizathu. Amanye avuliwe.

**"Umfundi we-PDF akakwazanga ukulayisha: …"**
Injini ye-PDF ayikwazanga ukulandwa. Ku-inthanethi: layisha kabusha ikhasi bese uzama futhi; hlola ukuthi i-firewall noma isivimbi somkhiqizo asivimbi lesi sayithi. Ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi: i-MarkupCraft ayizange igcinwe ukuze isebenze ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kulesi siphequluli (ibha ephezulu ayithi "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi"). Thola i-inthanethi, khetha {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}, bese ulinda "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi". Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

**"Amanye amakhomenti angavela kabili esikrinini" noma "Imininingwane eengeziwe yekhomenti ayikwazanga ukufundwa"**
Amakhomenti asefayelini anokuthile okungavamile. Avulwa noma kunjalo ngendlela engcono kakhulu i-MarkupCraft engayenza; wahlole ngaphambi kokuthembela kuwo. Ukulondoloza kusasebenza.

**Ishidi linegama elingalungile, noma "ikhasi 3"**
Bona [Indlela amashidi athola ngayo amagama](topic:open-a-plan-set#how-sheets-get-their-names). Amashidi askenwe akanamagama okufunda; sebenzisa {{ui:Qamba kabusha ishidi}}.

**Amashidi afanayo avela kabili**
Ifayela elifanayo livuliwe kabili. Chofoza {{ui:Vala}} (londoloza kuqala uma kudingeka) bese uvula isethi kabusha.

## Ukudweba nokukhetha

**Akukho okwenzekayo uma ngichofoza ishidi**
Hlola ithuluzi elichofoziwe ethulini, bese ufunda umugqa wesimo. Ngamathuluzi {{ui:Unxande}}, {{ui:Iyelipsi}} kanye {{ui:Ifu}}, ukuchofoza ngaphandle kokudonsa akudweba lutho: donsa. Ngamathuluzi {{ui:Iphola-layini}}, {{ui:Ipholigoni}}, {{ui:Ifu elikhululekile}} kanye {{ui:Indawo}}, amaphoyinti ayangezwa: qeda ngokuchofoza kabili noma ngo-{{key:Enter}}.

**"Ipholigoni idinga okungenani amaphoyinti ama-3." (noma ama-2 kwephola-layini)**
Chofoza amaphoyinti amaningi ngaphambi kokuqeda.

**"Akukho okuthayiphiwe, ngakho akukho mbhalo ongeziwe."**
Amabhokisiombhalo namakholi agcinwa kuphela uma enombhalo. Dweba futhi ubhale ngaphambi kokuchofoza kwenye indawo.

**"Amaphoyinti amabili afana. Chofoza amaphoyinti amabili ahlukile."**
Kwemigqa, imicibisholo, ubude nokulinganisa, izinhlangothi ezimbili kumele zihlukane. Donsa, noma chofoza kabili ezindaweni ezihlukile.

**Angikwazi ukuhambisa noma ukusula okumakiwe; kunebhokisi elimpunga elinomugqa oqhekeziwe**
Iyikhomenti efundwayo kuphela efike nefayela (isibonelo ukugqamisa umbhalo). I-MarkupCraft iyigcina njengoba kunjalo: ungayiphendula kuphela. Umugqa wesimo uthi "okumakiwe okufundwayo kuphela kugcinwa njengasefayelini".

**Okhiye bami babhala izinhlamvu ebhokisini esikhundleni sokukhetha amathuluzi**
Ikhesa lisebhokisi lombhalo (impendulo, insimu ku-{{ui:Izici}}, ukusesha kwesheshi). Chofoza endaweni engenalutho yesheshi kuqala, noma cindezela {{key:Esc}} uma uhlela umbhalo wokumaka.

**Isikhala asishelelisi, noma izinkinobho zomcibisholo azenzi lutho**
Usanda kuchofoza inkinobho yethuluzi, ngakho isasebenzisa ikhibhodi. Donsa ngenkinobho ephakathi yembewu esikhundleni salokho, noma chofoza ishidi kanye ngethuluzi {{ui:Khetha}} livulekile, bese uzama futhi. Bona nokuthi [Ikhibhodi nembewu](topic:keyboard#when-a-key-does-nothing).

**Ukucindezela ngeminwe emibili esikrinini sokuthinta kushiye isimaka**
Ithuluzi lokudonsa (ipeni, ifu, unxande) laqala ngaphambi kokuthi umunwe wesibili ufike, laqeda ngaphambi kokuthi likhanselwe. Cindezela {{ui:Hlehlisa}}. Beka iminwe emibili ngasikhathi sinye uma ufuna ukusondeza.

## Ukulinganisa

**"Linganisa leli shidi kuqala: khetha amaphoyinti amabili ebanga elaziwayo, noma khetha isikali salo. Bese ulinganisa."**
Ishidi ngalinye lidinga isikali salo ngaphambi kwe-{{ui:Ubude}} noma {{ui:Indawo}}. I-MarkupCraft isishintshele ku-{{ui:Linganisa}}: chofoza izinhlangothi ezimbili zesilinganiso esaziwayo bese uthayipha ubude baso, noma chofoza inkinobho yesikali ebhareni lesimo bese ukhetha isikali. Bona [Linganisa ishidi bese ulinganisa](topic:calibrate-and-measure).

**'Lokho akusona ubude i-MarkupCraft eliqondayo.'**
Thayipha njengo `24'-0"`, `12' 6 1/2"`, `7.2 m` noma `900 mm`. Bona [Amayunithi, ubude namasikali](topic:units-and-scales).

**Izilinganiso zingalungile kancane, noma zingalungile kakhulu**
- Isikali esimiswe kusengaphambili ku-PDF engakalingani ngobukhulu obugcwele silinganisa ngephutha. Linganisa kusuka esilinganisweni eside esaziwayo.
- Uphawu lwezinkulungwane lufundwe njengophawu lwedesimali: `1,200 mm` kusho 1.2 mm. Thayipha `1200 mm`.
- Sondeza uma uchofoza izinhlangothi.
- Umdwebo mhlawumbe awulingene nesikali ("NTS").

**"Khetha iqembu lokubala kuqala, bese uchofoza {{key:Shift}} ukuze ususe uphawu."**
Khetha {{ui:Khetha}}, chofoza iqembu lokubala, bese ukhetha {{ui:Bala}} futhi uchofoze {{key:Shift}} ngokudlula kuphawu.

## Ukulondoloza

**"Le PDF ivikelekile (ifakwe ikhodi), ngakho ikhophi emakiwe ayikwazi ukubhalwa."**
Ifayela livimbela izinguquko. Cela ikhophi engavikelekile. Ngalesi sikhathi ungasathumela uhlu lokumaka njenge-CSV noma i-BCF.

**"Umbhali we-PDF akakwazanga ukulayisha: …"**
Ngokuvamile awusoku-inthanethi futhi uhlelo alugcinwanga ukuze lusebenze ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi (akukho "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi" ebhareni elingaphezulu). Okumakiwe akusalahlekile ethebhini: ligcine livulekile, fika ku-inthanethi, bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza}} futhi. Bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} ukuze kusebenze ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngesikhathi esilandelayo; ikhophi elondoloziwe lifaka umbhali we-PDF.

**"Akukwazanga ukulondoloza <ifayela>"**
Ibhokisi likhombisa isizathu. Okumakiwe akusalahlekile ethebhini. Zama futhi; uma kuphindeka, thumela i-CSV ukuze amakhomenti angalahleki, bese ubika umyalezo.

**Ngikhethe Londoloza kodwa ifayela elilodwa kuphela lifike**
Isiphequluli sakho sivimbele okulandwayo okwengeziwe. Bheka isaziso ebhareni lekheli bese uvumela le sayithi ukulanda amafayela amaningi, bese ulondoloza futhi.

**Angiyikuthola ifayela elilondoliwe**
Lingene kuhla lokulanda lesiphequluli. Vula uhlu lokulanda lwesiphequluli (ku-Chrome ne-Edge, {{pick:mac=Option-Command-L|windows linux=Ctrl+J}}; ku-Firefox ne-Safari, inkinobho yokulanda ebhareni lamathuluzi) ukuze ubone lapho likhona. Bona [Iziphequluli namakhompyutha](common:browsers).

**Okunye kombhalo kubonisa "?" kwezinye izinhlelo ze-PDF**
Izinhlamvu ezingaphandle kwesethi yaseNtshonalanga Yurophu zikhombisa "?" ekhophini. Umlayezo wokulondoloza uzibalile. Bona [Londoloza i-PDF ephawulwe](topic:save-the-marked-up-pdf).

**Isitembu sibonisa usuku lwakusasa (noma lwasolanga)**
Amadethi ezitembu angu-UTC. Bona [Izitembu namanothi okunamathela](topic:stamps-and-notes).

## Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi

**Inkinobho ye-offline ithi "Akukwazanga ukulondoloza"**
Amanye amafayela ohlelo awalandanga. Hlola uxhumo bese uchofoza inkinobho futhi; ibala "Saving N of M" kuze kube "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi".

**Inkinobho ye-offline ithi "Offline not available"**
Iwindi lalesi siphequluli alikwazi ukugcina ikhophi ye-offline: iwindi eliyimfihlo, noma ikhompyutha enomgomo ovimbela lokho. Vula i-MarkupCraft ewindini elijwayelekile bese uzama futhi.

**Ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, uhlelo luyinguqulo endala**
Ikhophi ye-offline iyinguqulo oyilondoloze. Uma ku-inthanethi, chofoza "Ilungele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi" bese ukhetha "Londoloza futhi (thola inguqulo emisha)". Ukukhulula isikhala, khetha "Susa ikhophi ye-offline". Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

**Usizo alivuli**
Cindezela {{key:F1}} (ku-Mac, nge-**fn**), noma khetha {{ui:Usizo}} ebhareni eliphezulu. Lisebenza nangaphandle kwe-inthanethi.

## Izinto ezilahlekile

**Okumakiwe kwami kunyamalele ngemva kokuvala noma ukulayisha kabusha ithebhu**
Vula i-MarkupCraft kabusha kusiphequluli esifanayo: iyakunikeza umsebenzi ongalondolozwanga nge-"Buyisa umsebenzi wakho?". Khetha {{ui:Buyisela}} bese ulondoloza i-PDF. Amakhophi agcinwa kufika kwezinsuku ezingama-30; iwindi eliyimfihlo, noma ukucisha idatha yesayithi, akusasho lutho. Bona [Buyisela umsebenzi wakho](common:recover).

**Igama lami, izilungiselelo zamathuluzi noma umgxobo wamathuluzi kunyamalele**
Zigcinwa kulesi siphequluli kuphela futhi zisulwe (idatha yesayithi isulwe, iwindi eliyimfihlo, esinye isiphequluli noma ikhompyutha, noma ukuhlanzwa kwe-Safari kwezayithi ezingavakashelwanga isikhathi). Setha igama futhi ungenise umgxobo wakho owuthumelile. Bona [Okuhlala kulesi sisheshi](topic:browser-storage).

**"Umgxobo wamathuluzi awukwazanga ukugcinwa kulesi siphequluli (ukugcinwa kuvaliwe noma kugcwele). Uwuthumele ngaphandle ukuze uwugcine."**
Isiphequluli sala ukugcina idatha yale sayithi (imodi yangasese, ukugcinwa kuvinjwe yisilungiselelo noma inqubomgomo, noma kugcwele). Thumela umgxobo ngemva kokuwushintsha, bese uwungenisa ngakusasa.

## Ukubonakala nesivinini

**Ikhasi libonakala libhuqekile isikhashana**
I-MarkupCraft ibonisa ukubonakala okusheshayo, okungacacile uma usondeza noma ushelelisa, bese iyibuyisela ibukhali isikhathi esincane ngemva kokuyeka.

**Konke kuhamba kancane nesethi enkulu**
Vala amathebhu amanye anzima, vula amafayela ambalwa ngesikhathi sinye, futhi ugweme ukusondeza kuze kufike ekugcineni emashidini amakhulu. Bona [Sebenza namafayela amaningana ngesikhathi sinye](topic:several-files#big-sets).

**Ishidi limnyama linemigqa ekhanyayo**
Usemtatsheni we-{{ui:Imodeli}}. Khetha {{ui:Iphepha}} ebhareni eliphezulu ukuze uthole imigqa emnyama esimhlophe. Okumakiwe akho nePDF elondoloziwe akuthintwa.
