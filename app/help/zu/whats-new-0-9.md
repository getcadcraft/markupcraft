Lokhu kushintshe kuMarkupCraft 0.9, uma kuqhathaniswa no-0.8.1. Umqondo oyinhloko ukuthi amanothi akho okusebenza ahlala ngaphandle kwekhasi umkliyenti alithola, nesethi yonke yemizobo ingaxhunyanwa ngokudlula kanye.

## Okusha

### Amanothi, ibhodi lokusebenza namanothi asese

- **Ibhodi lokusebenza.** Indawo ezungeze ishidi ngalinye iyidesika: noma yini edwetshwe, ebhalwe noma enamathelwe eceleni kweshidi iyinothi futhi ayiphrintwa. Khetha {{ui:Bonisa ibhodi lokusebenza}} ukuze usondeze kude ukuthola indawo, noma {{ui:Thatha isithombe sibe ebhodini lokusebenza}}, hudula ibhokisi, bese {{ui:Ebhodini lokusebenza}} ukubeka isithombe sengxenye yomzobo eceleni kweshidi. Isithombe siyisithombe futhi asishintshi uma ishidi lishintsha. Bona [Amanothi, ibhodi lokusebenza namanothi asese](topic:notes-and-pasteboard).
- **Bonisa nofihle amanothi.** Noma yimuphi umaka ungaba yinothi: setha {{ui:Izimaki ezintsha ziba}} ethebhini le-{{ui:Amanothi}}, noma {{ui:Lesi simaki siba}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}. {{key:Shift+H}} noma iswishi ye-{{ui:Amanothi}} ngaphansi kweshidi lifihla futhi libonise wonke amanothi ngesikhathi esisodwa. Amanothi ashiywa ekuphrintweni, ezikopisweni ezilinganisiwe, ohlwini nasekuthunyelweni, futhi abanye abafundi be-PDF abawabonisi. I-PDF oyigcinile iwagcina ukuze uwabone ngesikhathi esilandelayo oyivula ku-MarkupCraft.
- **Amanothi asese.** Khetha {{ui:Setha iphasiwedi}}, bese wenza umaka ube {{ui:Inothi elingasese}}. Ufakwa ekhodini kufayela ngephasiwedi enezinhlamvu ezingu-10 noma ngaphezulu, futhi akukho ndlela yokubuyisa: akekho, nathi ngaphakathi, ongayivula ngaphandle kwephasiwedi. {{ui:Vala}} ibeka amanothi asese eceleni; {{ui:Hlala uvuliwe kule divayisi}} ayivulwa ngokuzenzakalelayo.
- **Gcina ikhophi ehlanzekile.** {{ui:Londoloza ikhophi ehlanzekile}} isusa wonke amanothi, amanothi asese kanye nakho konke okusebhodini lokusebenza. Yisebenzise ifayela eliya kukliyenti.

### Xhuma isethi yonke

- **Amaxhumanisi ngebhetshi.** Khetha {{ui:Amakhasi}} › {{ui:Amaxhumanisi ngebhetshi…}}, noma {{ui:Xhuma izimakhalekhukhwini zeshidi…}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Thola umbhalo}} epanelini le-{{ui:Amashidi}}. Isinyathelo 1 sibala inombolo negama leshidi ngalinye njengoba lifundwa ebhlokeni lesihloko; lungisa lawo angalungile. Isinyathelo 2 sibala izimakhalekhukhwini ezitholakele, njengo `3/A-501` noma `DETAIL 2 ON A-501`. {{ui:Izixhumanisi ezizoxhunywa}} ibonisa ishidi nombono wangalowo ngamunye, {{ui:Akuxhunyiwe}} ibonisa izinombolo ezingekho emashidini avulekile, futhi {{ui:Dala izixhumanisi}} zenza zonke ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Yenza futhi ngemva kwe-addendum noma ukubalwa kabusha kwezinombolo; amaxhumanisi owenze ngesandla awakaze ashintshwe. Ixhumanisi elinombono lilondolozwa njenge-PDF link, ukuze abanye abafundi bavule nemininingwane. Bona [Amaxhumanisi wamashidi](topic:sheet-links).

### Umzobo

- **Arc, izindlela ezilishumi.** Ithuluzi {{ui:Arc}} ({{key:O}}) manje linebhokisi {{ui:Indlela yomnsalo}} phezu kwekhasi, enezindlela eziyishumi zokudweba ijika; i-engele, ubude, iradiyasi nendlela kungachofozwa noma kubhalwe. Bona [Dweba ijika ngezindlela eziyishumi](topic:draw-an-arc).

### Ukusebenza ndawonye

- **Landela umbuyekezi.** Ku-iseshini ye-Live khetha {{ui:Landela}} eduze kwegama lomuntu, bese ukubuka kwakho kuhamba nokwakhe; ukuhambisa noma ukusondeza wena kuyayima. Umnikazi angakhetha {{ui:Cela wonke umuntu andilandele}}. Uhlu lwabantu lubonisa umbala negama lomuntu ngamunye, ishidi abakulo nesikhathi sokuhlela kwabo kokugcina. Bona [Landela omunye umbuyekezi ku-iseshini ye-Live](topic:follow-in-live).

## Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- Amathiphu esiqondiso ngu-Bootstrap, ithiphu yokuqala ingavalwa, amabhande wamathebhu ayaskrola uma egcwala, futhi amagama amathebhu esiphequluli ayi `<document> — MarkupCraft | CadCraft`.
- Ipanele enezigaba eziningi ivula eyodwa iyakhe ezinye (ukhiye ongaseduze kwesikhombi uyigcina ivuliwe); iswishi ye-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} iyakucima lokhu.
- Uhlelo ngalunye luqala kuqoqo lwezinto zomtapo ezizenzakalelayo.
- Izinhlamvu zesisindo nezinga lokushisa ziyatholakala ezilungiselelweni zamayunithi.

## Okulungisiwe

- **Izinto ezisusiwe azisasali zifundeka ngaphakathi kwe-PDF elondoloziwe.** Ukugcina kwakuke kususe ukumaka okususiwe noma okubhalwe kabusha ekhasini lakho kodwa kushiye into, nombhalo wayo, kufayela. Manje ukugcina kususa lezo zinto. Amafayela agcinwe yizakhiwo zangaphambili asabamba kuze kube yilapho uwagcina futhi.

## Imikhawulo eyiqiniso

- Amaxhumanisi ngebhetshi ahlolwe kumasethi ethu wokuhlola. Amasethi angempela adweba izimpandla ngezindlela eziningi, ngakho kwisethi yeklayenti ezinye izinkomba zingase zingatholakali. Amashidi askeniwe adinga {{ui:Funda umbhalo (OCR)…}} kuqala.
- Amanothi, amanothi asese nebhodi lokusebenza kuhlolwe ku-browser engenakhanda nakumafayela ethu, hhayi nesethi enkulu yangempela ye-vector, futhi hhayi ku-iseshini yangempela ye-Live yabantu ababili. Ukushintsha iphasiwedi akwakhiwe. Izakhiwo ezingaphambi kwe-0.9 azibonisi amanothi.
- Ukubukwa kwekilasi kuwuhlu lwabantu; akuzange kuhlolwe nothisha nabafundi bangempela.
- Izilimi ezingeyona isiNgisi ziyizinhlobo zomshini kuze kufundwe umuntu wasendaweni.
- Abafakeli be-Windows abasayinwanga ngekhodi (ngu-{{ui:Ulwazi olwengeziwe}}, bese {{ui:Qhuba noma kunjalo}}); isithombe-disk se-macOS asihlolwanga yi-Apple (chofoza ngesokudla, bese {{ui:Vula}}).

## Ngaphambili: 0.8 ne-0.8.1

Lokhu kushintshe kuMarkupCraft 0.8 ne-0.8.1, uma kuqhathaniswa ne-0.7. Okukhulu okusha ukuthi ukumaka kungaba umsebenzi umuntu, noma umsizi wakho ozowenza, futhi uzivalele ngokwawo lapho umzobo ushintsha ngempela.

### Okusha

**Ukumaka okwenza okuthile**

- **Yenza ukumaka kube umsebenzi.** Khetha okumakiwe okukodwa noma ngaphezulu, vula ithebhu ethi {{ui:Imisebenzi}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Yenza okukhethiwe kube umsebenzi}}. Umsebenzi unesihloko, umuntu noma umsizi wakho oxhunyiwe owuzoyenza, usuku lokufika uma ufuna, nesimo; uhlala endaweni yawo ishidini nokuxoxisana kwawo. Bona [Yenza ukumaka kube umsebenzi](topic:markups-that-act).
- **Imisebenzi evala yona.** Umsebenzi ungaqapha umzobo ongaphakathi kokumaka kwawo. Vula uguqulo olusha lweshidi bese ukhetha {{ui:Hlola manje}}: uma imigqa esendaweni leyo ishintshe ngaphezu kwencane, umsebenzi umakwa njengoKwenziwe, ukumaka kwawo kuba {{ui:Iqedile}} futhi inothi ethreadini lichaza okushintshile. Isitembu sosuku esisha, noma imigqa ehambe ngaphansi kwe-1.5 amaphoyinti, akubalwa. Umsebenzi ungaqapha futhi into yemodeli: umsizi wakho ubika into nge-`tasks_check`, futhi umbiko osehlukile, noma into esusiwe, uvala umsebenzi. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa siwuvulela futhi.
- **Thola umsebenzi.** Hlunga uhlu {{ui:Imisebenzi}} ngesimo nangomuntu wona, kufaka phakathi {{ui:Ephuzile}}; khetha {{ui:Obekwe kuye}}, bese {{ui:Umsizi}} ukuze ubone okulindele umsizi wakho. Umsebenzi oqede usuku lokufika lwawo ubonakala njengesexwayiso ephaneli le-{{ui:Izinkinga}}. {{ui:CSV}} ithumela uhlu. Imisebenzi igcinwa ku-PDF nokumaka futhi ihamba nge-{{ui:Live}} njengezinye izici zokumaka.

**Okushintshile, njengohlu olulula**

- **Bhala izinguquko.** Vula ithebhu ethi {{ui:Qhathanisa}}, bese {{ui:Qhathanisa isethi yonke}}, khetha ifayela langaphambili nelisha bese ukhetha {{ui:Bhala uhlu lwezinguquko}}. Uhlu luqala ngenani, bese lugubha ishidi ngalinye eshintshile, ushintsho olukhulu kuqala: ikuphi endaweni yeshidi, bukhulu kangakanani (ngamayunithi wangempela nakumainshi ephepha kweshidi elilinganisiwe), ukuthi imigqa yengezwe noma yasuswa, izinkomba eziningi ezisendaweni leyo, namagama avela noma anyamalala. {{ui:Kopisha uhlu}} libeka ohlwini lokukopisha, futhi {{ui:CSV}} ibhala umugqa owodwa ngoshintsho ngalunye. Bona [Uhlu lwezinto ezishintshile phakathi kwezinguquko](topic:what-changed-in-words).

**Amanothi ezwi nezinqumo**

- **Amanothi ezwi.** Ethebhini le-{{ui:Amanothi}} khetha {{ui:Rekhoda inothi lezwi}}, bese {{ui:Yeka ukurekhoda}}; ukurekhoda kuyeka kwodwa emizuzwini emibili. Inothi liphinwa phakathi kweshidi, uyalihudula lapho okufanele likhona, ubhale isihloko esifushane ku-{{ui:Izici}}, futhi {{ui:Dlala}} ohlwini liyalidlala. Ukurekhoda kugcinwa ngaphakathi kwe-PDF.
- **Izinqumo.** {{ui:Phina isinqumo}} ibhala umusho noma amabili ushidini. Uma sithatha indawo yesinqumo sangaphambili, khetha esidala ku-{{ui:Sithatha indawo ye}}: esidala sihlala, sibhalwe njengoSithathiwe, futhi sikhomba esisha. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa sikususa. Bona [Phina amanothi ezwi nezinqumo ashidini](topic:voice-notes-and-decisions).

**Redline ukuya ekubuyekezweni**

- **Thumela futhi uphendule.** Ethebhini le-{{ui:Imisebenzi}} vula {{ui:Redline ukuya ekubuyekezweni}}, bhala uguqulo okumakiwe okuqhathaniswa nalo bese ukhetha {{ui:Thumela ifayela le-redline}}. Uthola ifayela le-`.redline.json` linento eyodwa ngokumaka okuvulekile ngakunye: ishidi, indawo (njengengxenye yeshidi, ukuze ukuphrinta kabusha ngosayizi ohlukile kusahambisana), amagama, umbhali, ithreadi nomsebenzi wayo. Lowo odweba izinguquko uphendula ngoYamukelwe, uYenqatshiwe noma uKwenziwe kufayela le-`.redline-answers.json`; {{ui:Layisha ifayela lezimpendulo…}} unika ukumaka ngakunye lesimo sawo kanye nenothi lomdwebi, njengesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Bona [Thumela okumakiwe kumdwebi njenge-redline](topic:redline-to-revision).

**Abasizi be-AI**

- **Amathuluzi amaningi.** Umsizi oxhunywe nge-CadCraft Studio ungadwebela uhlu lwemisebenzi (`tasks_list`), enze noma ashintshe enye (`task_set`), ayisuse (`task_clear`), ahlole enye ngokuchazwa okusha (`tasks_check`) nokufunda uhlu olulula lwezinguquko phakathi kwamasethi amabili avulekile (`revisions_changes`). Uthola kuphela amathuluzi owathikile ngaphansi kweswishi yabasizi, futhi ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Bona [Sebenzisa i-MarkupCraft nomsizi we-AI](topic:use-with-ai-assistants).

### Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- **Izilimi.** CadCraft Studio manje inezilimi ezingu-54. Eziyishumi ezintsha: isiLithuania, isiLatvia, isiBosnia, isiMarathi, isiTelugu, isi-Albania, isi-Afrikaans, isiFilipino, isiSwahili kanye nesi-Uzbek. Zonke ezingezona ezesiNgisi ziyizinhlobo zomshini kuze kufundwe umuntu wasendaweni.
- **Amadivayisi okufaka.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Jikelele}} › {{ui:Amadivayisi okufaka}} kusetha ukuthi i-trackpad, izinkinobho ezingaseceleni kwemouse yakho, ipeni, ukuthinta ngomunwe, imouse ye-3D, i-game controller noma i-MIDI controller yenza ini, kuzo zonke izinhlelo noma kuphela kule. Kuze kube yilapho ushintsha okuthile, zonke izinto zisebenza njengakuqala. Bona [Sebenzisa i-trackpad, imouse ye-3D noma i-controller](common:input-devices).
- **Imigqa yebha yamathuluzi.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Imigqa yebha yamathuluzi}} inezinketho {{ui:Phindisela emugqeni olandelayo}} (okuzenzakalelayo), {{ui:Imigqa emi-2}} kanye ne-{{ui:Umugqa owodwa}}, ukuze umzobo uthole indawo engaphezulu. Bona [Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels).
- **Ipanele yezakhiwo.** Izinkambu zesitayela sokumaka zisebenzisa igridi evamile, nezigaba ezingasongeka namakholomu wegama nenani.
- **Ipanele yezinkinga.** Izexwayiso ezivela ku-app, njengomsebenzi ophuzile, ziqoqelwa kwipanele elilodwa lezinkinga.
- **Live nama-add-on.** Iziseshini ze-Live zichazwe ku-[Sebenza ndawonye ngokuqondile](common:work-together-live); uhlu lwama-add-on olulandela i-akhawunti yakho lusencazelweni ku-[Ama-add-on nohlu lwe-akhawunti yakho](common:addons-basics).
- **Xhuma i-AI yakho.** CadCraft Studio yisisekelo somsizi wakho siqu: xhuma kanye wonke ama-app ayisishiyagalombili. Ama-app awanayo i-AI yawo.

### Okulungisiwe ku-0.8.1

- **I-Studio ingashintsha ulimi ngesikhathi isasebenza.** Ngaphambili, i-app yedeskithophu nezinguqulo ezipakishiwe zazihlala ngesiNgisi. Lokhu akukahlolwa kuzo zonke izinkundla: sitshele uma ulimi lungashintshi.
- Ukufakwa kwe-Studio 0.8 kunikezwa i-0.8.1 ngu-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Izibuyekezo}}.

### Okungakakhiwe kule nguqulo

- **Akusayinwanga ngekhodi.** Abafakeli be-Windows kanye ne-disk image ye-macOS abanalo uphawu lokusayina okwamanje. Ku-Windows, uma i-SmartScreen imisa umfakeli, khetha {{ui:Ulwazi olwengeziwe}}, bese {{ui:Qhuba noma kunjalo}}. Ku-Mac, chofoza ngesokudla i-CadCraft Studio bese ukhetha {{ui:Vula}}. Izinguqulo ze-store zibekelwe eceleni.
- **Ukuxhuma i-AI yakho akuphelele ngezinye izindawo.** Ukuxhuma ngokuchofoza kanye ngaphakathi ku-Studio, isixhumi esiphethwe sabasizi be-browser kanye nokhiye wakho we-API ngaphakathi ku-Studio akuqinisekiswa kulokhu kukhishwa. Umjikelezo womsebenzi uhlolwe, kodwa awukahlolwa nomsizi wangempela.
- **Ukuhlola kwemisebenzi kudinga usayizi wekhasi ofanayo.** Ishidi elilinganiswe kabusha ngosayizi ohlukile ngeke lihambisane, ngakho imisebenzi yalo ayivali. Amashidi ahlanganiswa ngenombolo yeshidi, noma ngendawo yekhasi.
- **Amanothi ezwi ayizirekhodo kuphela.** Akukho umbhalo wokubhala, futhi umsizi ubona ubude benothi, umbhali nesihloko, hhayi umsindo. Ukurekhoda kuhlolwe ngomakrofoni wokuzenzisa ku-browser engenakhanda; umakrofoni wangempela ku-foni, ithabulethi noma ilaptop usekhona okufanele uhlolwe.
- **Amashidi wokubala nezizathu ku-PDF** agcinwa ngaphakathi kwengxenye eyimfihlo yefayela. I-MarkupCraft endala iyiyeka, futhi iyisusa lapho igcina ifayela kabusha, ngakho vula futhi ugcine lelo fayela nge-0.8 noma ngaphezulu.
- **Uhlangothi lomdwebi lwamaredlayini.** Ukufunda kwe-CadCraft ifayela le-redline nokulipheendula akukakhiwa.
- **Ezinye izilimi.** I-app bar yesiTamil iphuma nge-528 px.

Konke okunye kukuthi [Okungakakhiwe](topic:not-built-yet). Ngokukhishwa okwandulela lokhu, bona [Okusha ku-0.7](topic:whats-new-0-7).
