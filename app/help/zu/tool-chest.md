Ithebhu {{ui:Amathuluzi}} ibamba **umgxobo wakho wamathuluzi**: okumakiwe okulondoloziwe ngesimo sawo, kulungele ukudwetshwa futhi. Ithuluzi ligcina uhlobo lokumakiwe, umbala, ububanzi womugqa, ukugcwaliswa, ukuqina kombala, usayizi wombhalo nesihloko, kanye nombhalo wesitembu, igama leqembu lokubala, noma umbhalo wokuqala webhokisi lombhalo noma ikholi.

## Amathuluzi oqala nawo

Umgxobo omusha unawo ayisithupha: "Ifu lokuguqulwa" (obomvu), "Ifu lombuzo" (oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka), "Isiqaqambisi esiphuzi", "Ikholi ye-RFI" (oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, luqala ngo-"RFI: "), "Hlola esiteshini" (ikholi ewolintshi ethi "Hlola esiteshini.") kanye "Imvumelekile njengoba kuphawuliwe" (isitembu).

## Dweba ngethuluzi

1. Vula ithebhu {{ui:Amathuluzi}}.
2. Chofoza ithuluzi. Liyabonakala lichofoziwe, bese ithuluzi elifanayo ethulini lezinsimbi liyasebenza.
3. Dweba njengejwayelekile. {{ui:Izici}} ithi 'Udweba nge-"<ithuluzi>" esuka emgxobeni wamathuluzi.'

Ngenkathi ithuluzi lomgxobo lisebenza, izinguquko oziyenzayo kuphaneli {{ui:Izici}} ngaphambi kokudweba zisebenzela lelo thuluzi okwamanje, ngaphandle kokushintsha ithuluzi elilondoloziwe noma izilungiselelo zakho zejwayelekile. Ukukhetha inkinobho ethulini noma ukucindezela ukhiye welithuluzi kubuyisela ithuluzi elijwayelekile.

## Londoloza owakho

1. Dweba okumakiwe bese uwenza ngendlela ofuna ngayo: umbala, umugqa, ukugcwaliswa, isihloko, umbhalo wesitembu.
2. Khetha kona.
3. Khetha {{ui:Londoloza okukhethiwe njengethuluzi}} ethebhini {{ui:Amathuluzi}}, noma {{ui:Londoloza njengethuluzi}} kuphaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}}.
4. Bhala {{ui:Igama lethuluzi}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza ithuluzi}}.

Okuphumayo: ithuluzi lengezwa ekugcineni komgxobo, bese ithebhu {{ui:Amathuluzi}} ivuleka ilikhombisa.

## Izitembu ezenziwe ngokukhethiwe

Khetha okumakiwe amaningi (noma okuqukethwe ikhasi ngethuluzi {{ui:Okuqukethwe}}) bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza ukukhethwa njengesitembu…}} kumenyu ethi {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} noma emenyini yokuchofoza ngesokudla. Umgxobo uthola isitembu esisodwa esibamba zonke ("Isitembu: okumakiwe ama-3 ndawonye"). Chofoza kuso, bese uchofoza noma yiliphi ishidi: okumakiwe kufika ndawonye, kumaphakathi lapho uchofoza khona, njengokumakiwe amasha ngegama lakho; okuqukethwe kwekhasi kudwetshwa khona lapho. Lezi zitembu zigcinwa futhi zabelwane nomgxobo njengawo wonke amanye amathuluzi.

## Susa ithuluzi

Chofoza u-× ekugcineni komugqa wethuluzi, bese {{ui:Susa}}. Okumakiwe asevele edwetshwe ngalo ahlala njengoba enjalo.

## Lapho umgxobo ugcinwa khona

Umgxobo ugcinwa **kulesi sisheshi, kulesi sikhompyutha**. Esinye isiphequluli noma ikhompyutha inomgxobo wayo (oqala ngamathuluzi ayisithupha ajwayelekile), iwindi eliyimfihlo liyawukhohlwa uma livaliwe, futhi ukucisha idatha yale sayithi emasethingini esiphequluli kuyawusula.

Uma isiphequluli singawugcini (ukugcinwa kuvaliwe, noma kugcwele), umugqa wesimo uthi: "Umgxobo wamathuluzi awukwazanga ukugcinwa kulesi siphequluli (ukugcinwa kuvaliwe noma kugcwele). Uwuthumele ngaphandle ukuze uwugcine."

:::safari
Safari ingasula idatha egcinwe yiwebhusayithi uma ungayisebenzisi isikhathi esithile. Thumela umgxobo wakho njalo ngezikhathi ukuze ukwazi ukuwungenisa kabusha.
:::

**Thumela umgxobo wakho njengekhophi elondoloziwe**, ikakhulukazi emva kokwakha amathuluzi othembele kuwo.

## Yabelana ngomgxobo nehhovisi lakho

1. Lungiselela amathuluzi kanye.
2. Khetha {{ui:Thumela}}. Uthola "MarkupCraft tool chest.json".
3. Yibeke lapho ihhovisiligcina khona izindinganiso zalo, noma uyithumele.
4. Abanye bonke bakhetha {{ui:Ngenisa}} ethebhini labo {{ui:Amathuluzi}} bese bekhetha leli fayela.

Okuphumayo: "Ngenisiwe amathuluzi ayi-12 kusuka ku-"MarkupCraft tool chest.json"." Amathuluzi avela emgxobeni ofanayo ashintsha amakhophi awo amadala, ngakho ukungenisa umgxobo wehhovisi obuyekeziwe kubuyekeza lawo mathuluzi kunokuphindaphinda; amathuluzi amasha ayangezwa. Umgxobo ungabamba kufika kumathuluzi angu-500.

I-MarkupCraft ihlola inani ngalinye kufayela elingeniswayo futhi iyeqa lokho okungasetshenziswa: "Ayi-2 ayiqedwanga (awasebenziseki)". Amafayela angewona umgxobo wamathuluzi ayenqatshwa: "Lolu fayela alisiyo i-MarkupCraft tool chest." noma "Lolu fayela alisiyo umgxobo wamathuluzi (alisiyo JSON)."

Amafayela omgxobo wamathuluzi avela kwezinye izinhlelo zokumaka awukwazi ukungeniswa.
