Zombili ziyinothi eshidini, ngakho zibonakala ohlwini lwe-{{ui:Okumakiwe}}, emugqeni wamaphendulo nakwezinye izinhlelo ze-PDF njengamanothi okunamathela.

## Inothi lezwi

1. Bonisa ishidi bese uvula ithebhu {{ui:Amanothi}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:Rekhoda inothi lezwi}}. Isiphequluli sibuza ukusebenzisa imakrofoni okokuqala.
3. Khetha {{ui:Yeka ukurekhoda}}. Ukurekhoda kuyeka ngokwakho kumaminithi amabili.

Inothi liphinwe ohlelweni lweshidi: lihudule lapho lifanele, bese ubhala isihloko ku-{{ui:Izici}}. {{ui:Dlala}} ohlwini liyalidlala. Ukurekhoda kulondolozwa ngaphakathi kwe-PDF.

Lokhu kurekhoda kuphela. I-MarkupCraft ayiguqulisi inkulumo ibe ngumbhalo, futhi nomsizi oxhunywe ngamathuluzi akakwenzi lokho: ukwazi ukuthi inothi lezwi likhona, ubude balo, ubani owenzile nesihloko salo, kodwa awukwazi ukulizwa. Thayipha amagama abalulekile esihlokweni.

## Isinqumo

Khetha {{ui:Phina isinqumo}}, bhala esihlokweni sizinhlanga ezimbili noma ezintathu, bese ukhetha {{ui:Phina}}. Uma silungisa isinqumo esandulele, khetha lowo ku-{{ui:Sithatha indawo ye}}: esidala sihlala eshidini, simakwe ngokuthi Sishintshiwe, futhi sikhomba esisha. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa siyasususa.
