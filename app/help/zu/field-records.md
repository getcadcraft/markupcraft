Ithebhu ethi {{ui:Inkundla}} igcina izinhlobo ezintathu zamarekhodi ecaleni kokumaka kwakho. Ngayinye ixhunywe eshidini; i-RFI noma isinikezo nakho kunephinikhodi endaweni eshidini okuyo.

- **i-RFI**: umbuzo wethimba lokwakha. Izimo: Ivulekile, Iphendulwe, Ivaliwe.
- **Isinikezo**: into okufanele ibhekwe ngaphambi kokuthi umsebenzi uqhubeke. Izimo: Ivulekile, Ukubhekwa, Igunyaziwe, Igunyaziwe njengoba kuphawuliwe, Guqula uphinde uthumele, Ivaliwe.
- **Incwadi yansuku zonke**: isimo sezulu sosuku, inani labantu esayithini nomsebenzi owenziwe. Izimo: Uhlaka, Ekugcineni.

Irekhodi ngalinye linenombolo yohlobo lalo (RFI-0001, SUB-0001, LOG-0001), isihloko, umbhalo, umuntu okunikezwe kuye, usuku lokuphela, uchungechunge lwezimpendulo kanye nohlu lwamagama ezinamathiselo.

## Engeza elilodwa

1. Vula ithebhu ethi {{ui:Inkundla}} bese ukhetha {{ui:RFI}} noma {{ui:Isinikezo}}.
2. Thepha noma chofoza eshidini lapho kufanele khona. Kuvela iphinikhodi bese irekhodi livula ethebhini.
3. Gcwalisa isihloko, umbuzo noma incazelo, ukuthi ukunikezwe ubani nokuthi kufanele kuqedwe nini.

Incwadi yansuku zonke yengezwa eshidini elivuliwe ngaphandle kwephinikhodi. {{ui:Beka ipini}} imbeka kamuva; {{ui:Hambisa ipini}} ihambisa noma iyiphi iphinikhodi. Ukuthepha iphinikhodi eshidini kuvula irekhodi layo.

## Phendula futhi landela

Shintsha {{ui:Isimo}} bese lokho kushintsha kuyarekhodwa engxoxweni. Bhala impendulo bese ukhetha {{ui:Thumela impendulo}} (Ctrl+Enter nayo iyathumela). Okunamathiselwe amagama kuphela: thayipha igama noma khetha amafayela, bese uhlu lutshela ithimba ukuthi yiziphi izinto ezihambisana nerekhodi. Amafayela ngokwawo asala lapho ekhona.

Irekhodi elisuku lokuphela selidlule futhi elisalindile (i-RFI engakaphendulwa noma engakavaliwa, isinikezo engakanqunywa), limakwa **Ephuzile**, ngombala obomvu, ohlwini nasephinikhodini yalo.

## Thola okubalulekile

Izihlungi zisezingeni lohlu: uhlobo, isimo, usuku lokuphela (eziphuzile, ezifika ezinsukwini ezi-7, enalo noma engenalo usuku lokuphela), leli shidi noma wonke amashidi, ukusesha ngombhalo, kanye ne-{{ui:Ekunikezwe mina}}, eqhathanisa umuntu okunikezwe kuye negama i-MarkupCraft likwazi ngalo wena (bona [Setha igama lakho](topic:set-your-name)). Umugqa ongaphansi kwezihlungi ubala okubonisiwe nokuphuzile.

## Khipha

{{ui:CSV}} ibhala amarekhodi abonisiwe njengesipredishithi. {{ui:BCF}} ibhala njengezihloko ze-BCF, ngayinye nesithombe sendawo yayo eshidini, ukuze ezinye izinsiza ze-BCF zikwazi ukuwabala. Kokubili kulandela izihlungi.

## Londoloza, ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nokuhlanganisa amakhophi

Amarekhodi agcinwa ngaphakathi kwe-PDF lapho uyilondoloza, futhi aphinde abuyele lapho uyivula. Akudingi inethiwekhi: engeza, hlela, phendula bese ulondoloza ethabhulethini esitezini esingaphansi komhlaba, bese ubuyisa i-PDF. Ama-PDF amadala nama-PDF avela ezinye izinhlelo avulwa njengakuqala.

Ukuze uhlanganise amakhophi amabili, vula elinye bese ukhetha {{ui:Hlanganisa}}, bese ukhetha elinye ikhophi ye-PDF efanayo. Amarekhodi ahlanganiswa ngama-id awo:

- irekhodi elilodwa kuphela ikhophi elinalo lingeniswa;
- irekhodi ekhophi zombili lithatha izinkambu zalo (isihloko, umbhalo, isimo, umuntu okunikezwe kuye, usuku lokuphela) kusuka ekhophini eshintshwe kamuva, njengento ephelele, ngakho uma abantu ababili beshintshe izinkambu ezihlukile zerekhodi elifanayo, inguqulo yekhophi elisakhiwe kamuva iyanqoba; izingxoxo nemigqa yamagama ezinamathiselo kusuka kumakhophi womabili ayahlanganiswa;
- uma amakhophi womabili enombolise irekhodi elisha ngokufanayo, elifika lithola inombolo engakabi sebenziswa elandelayo;
- irekhodi elisuswe kwelinye ikhophi lisala lisusiwe, ngaphandle uma lahlelwa ngemva kwalokho.

Ukuhlanganisa kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

## Imikhawulo

- Amarekhodi awabelwani yet ngu-Live TeamWork; sebenzisa Hlanganisa.
- Akukho amaqembu, izaziso noma amaphrojekthi e-cloud amarekhodi kulolu hlobo.
- Amakhasi asusiwe ku-PDF athatha namarekhodi awo (isiqinisekiso sekhasi sibala wona).
