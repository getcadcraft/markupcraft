## Faka isitembu eshidini

1. Khetha {{ui:Isitembu}} ({{key:S}}).
2. Kuphaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}} ngakwesokudla, khetha isitembu ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Isitembu}}:
   - Imvumelekile
   - Imvumelekile njengoba kuphawuliwe
   - Buyekeza bese uthumela futhi
   - Yenqabiwe
   - Ibuyekeziwe
   - Ayisebenzi
   - Umbhalo wezifiso… (bhala kuya kuzinhlamvu ezingu-60 ebhokisini elivelayo, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} noma uchofoze enye indawo)
3. Chofoza esheshini lapho isitembu kufanele sihlale khona. Noma hudula ibhokisi ukukhetha usayizi walo.

Okuphumayo: isitembu esiboshwe ngohlaka, sibhalwe ngamagama amakhulu, nomugqa wesibili othi "Ngu-<igama lakho>, <usuku>". Isitembu esichofoziwe sisayizwa esikrinini ngokuya ngokusondeza kwakho, siphakathi kwamayintshi ayisigamu namayintshi ayi-1 1/4 ukuphakama eshidini eliphrintiwe.

Isitembu okukhethe sona sihlala siyikhethwe okulandelayo, nasemva kokuvula kabusha i-MarkupCraft kulesi sisheshi.

**Imibala ilandela incazelo:** izitembu ze-Imvumelekile zinhlaza, Buyekeza bese uthumela futhi zingewolintshi, Yenqabiwe ne-Ayisebenzi zibomvu, Ibuyekeziwe zioluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, nezitembu zezifiso zivenkile. Uma ukhethe omunye umbala ku-{{ui:Izici}}, lowo mbala uyagcinwa.

### Shintsha isitembu kamuva

Khetha lona bese ukhetha esinye isitembu kuphaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}}. Uma sasisasebenzisa umbala waso ozenzakalelayo, umbala uyashintsha uhambisane nencazelo.

Igama nosuku esitembeni yingoyiphi umbhali wesitembu nosuku esenziwe ngalo. Azishintshi uma usihlela, futhi umbhali wesitembu akakwazi ukushintshwa. Ukuze ufake isitembu ngegama lomunye umuntu, yena uzosetha igama lakhe bese eyisitembisa ngokwakhe.

:::note
Usuku oluseSitembeni lusuku lwe-Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Kusihlwa ezindaweni zase-Americas, noma ekuseni kakhulu e-Asia nase-Australia, lungabonisa usuku olulandelayo noma olwedlule uma luqhathaniswa newashi lakho. Isikhathi sokudalwa esiku {{ui:Izici}} sisesikhathini sakho sendawo.
:::

### Izitembu ozisebenzisa kaningi

Umgxobo wamathuluzi unethuluzi lesitembu elithi "Imvumelekile njengoba kuphawuliwe", futhi ungalondoloza noma yisiphi isitembu (umbhalo waso nombala waso) njengethuluzi lapho. Bona [Umgxobo wamathuluzi](topic:tool-chest).

Izitembu ezinezindawo ezigcwaliswayo (idethi okwamukelwe ngayo, inombolo yephrojekthi) azikakhiwa.

## Faka isithombe noma isignesha yakho

Uhlu lwezitembu ku-{{ui:Izici}} luphela ngezinketho ezimbili ezengeziwe:

- {{ui:Isithombe…}} icela ifayela le-PNG, JPEG noma SVG kulesi sikhompyutha, noma ikhasi le-PDF, njengesitembu sekampani noma ilogo. Izithombe ezinkulu zincishiswa zibe ngu-1600 pixels ohlangothini olude; i-SVG noma ikhasi le-PDF liba isithombe salobo busayizi.
- {{ui:Isignesha yami…}} ivula ibhokisi lesignesha: sayina ngembewu, ngepeni noma ngomunwe, bese ukhetha {{ui:Sebenzisa le signesha}}. {{ui:Sula}} iqala kabusha. {{ui:Sebenzisa isithombe sesignesha…}} ithatha isikeni noma isithombe sesignesha yakho: i-MarkupCraft yenza iphepha elimhlophe libe nokukhanya, bese inqamula isithombe ngokuya kuinki (ye-PNG ibuza kuqala).

Bese uchofoza ishidi ukuze ulibeke (isithombe sibanzi amayintshi ama-3, isignesha amayintshi ama-2), noma hudula ibhokisi ukuze lingene ngaphakathi kwalo; izilinganiso ziyagcinwa. Lishintshe ngamanqamu alo njengakunoma yimuphi okumakiwe ({{key:Shift}} igcina izilinganiso).

Isithombe sokugcina sigcinwa kulesi sisheshi, ukuze silungele ngakusasa; {{ui:Khetha esinye isithombe…}} siyishintsha. Yonke isignesha oyidwebayo noma oyilayishayo igcinwa ku-{{ui:Amasigneshaakho}} (kufika kwezi-8, eyamanje phezulu): chofoza eyodwa ukuze uyisebenzise, noma × ukuze uyisuse. Bona [Okuhlala kulesi sisheshi](topic:browser-storage).

KwePDF elondoloziwe isithombe siba yikhomenti yesitembu esibonisa umfanekiso, ngakho noma yiliphi ifunda le-PDF liyawubona futhi liyawuphrinta. Isignesha edwetshwe lapha ingumfanekiso wesignesha yakho, hhayi isignesha yedijithali eqinisekisiwe.

## Namathisela isithombe ebhodini lokukopisha

Kopisha isithombe kwenye inhlelo, noma thatha isithombe-skrini ebhodini lokukopisha, bese ucindezela {{key:Mod+V}} phezu kwesheshi. Lifika lapho inkomba ikhona, kube ngamayintshi ama-3 ngaphezulu ububanzi noma ubude, izilinganiso zigcinwa, njengokumakiwa kwesithombe ongakuhambisa futhi ukulinganise. KwePDF elondoloziwe kuyisitembu esibonisa umfanekiso, njengezithombe ezingenhla.

{{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Namathisela isithombe}} ngaphansi kweqembu {{ui:Hlela}} yenza okufanayo kusuka kumenyu; isiphequluli singabuza kuqala. Uma ibhodi lokukopisha lingenalo isithombe, {{key:Mod+V}} namathisela okumakiwe okukopishiwe njengakuqala.

## Faka isithombe kufayela

Khetha {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Faka isithombe kufayela…}} eqenjini {{ui:Hlela}} (noma {{ui:Faka isithombe lapha…}} kumenyu yokuchofoza ngesokudla), bese ukhetha ifayela le-PNG, JPEG noma SVG noma i-PDF (i-PDF enamakhasi amaningi ibuza ukuthi yiliphi). Ungaphinda uhudule ifayela le-PNG, JPEG noma SVG uliphonse eshidini: liyafika lapho uliphonsa khona. Isithombe siyisimaki, esingaba ngamayintshi ama-3 ububanzi noma ubude: hambisa usilinganise njengakunoma yimuphi omunye.

Ukuze isithombe sibe yingxenye yekhasi, faka itiki ku-{{ui:Cindezela ibe yikhasi lapho silondolozwa}} kuphaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}}. Bona [Londoloza ikhophi elicindezelwe](topic:flatten).

## Yenza isitembu kokukhethiwe

Khetha okumakiwe (noma okuqukethwe ikhasi ngethuluzi {{ui:Okuqukethwe}}) bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza ukukhethwa njengesitembu…}} kumenyu ethi {{ui:Okwengeziwe}}, noma kumenyu yokuchofoza ngesokudla, noma {{ui:Londoloza njengesitembu…}} kuphaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}}. Nika igama. Liya emgxobeni wamathuluzi: uchofoze lapho, bese uchofoza noma yiliphi ishidi ukuze ubeke konke okukhethiwe kuhlanganiswe, kumaphakathi lapho uchofoza khona. Bona [Umgxobo wamathuluzi](topic:tool-chest).

## Amanothi okunamathela

1. Khetha {{ui:Inothi}} ({{key:N}}).
2. Chofoza lapho inothi kufanele libe khona. Isithonjana esincane senothi sivela sinebhokisi eseceleni kwaso.
3. Bhala inothi. Chofoza ngaphandle noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Enter}} ukuze ulondoloze; {{key:Esc}} iliphonsa.

Ngokungafani nombhalo namakholi, inothi ligcinwa noma ungabhali lutho, ngakho ungabeka isithonjana manje bese ubhala kamuva.

- Amagama enothi yiwona {{ui:Inothi}} kuphaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}}. Chofoza kabili isithonjana (noma ukhethe bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}) ukuze uwahlele esheshini.
- Esheshini ubona isithonjana kuphela. Ezinye izibukeli ze-PDF zibonisa inothi elijwayelekile elivula umbhalo lapho lichofozwa.
- Shintsha umbala wesithonjana ngokukhetha {{ui:Umbala}} kuphaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}}.

Ukuxoxisana okuqhubekayo, sebenzisa umugqa wamaphendulo kunoma yimuphi okumakiwe esikhundleni salokho: bona [Izimo namaphendulo](topic:statuses-and-replies).
