{{ui:Thola umbhalo}}, phezulu kwephaneli ethi {{ui:Amashidi}}, sesha amagama adwetshwe emashidini ngokwawo. {{ui:Sesha amashidi}} ngaphansi kwayo isesha amagama amashidi kuphela.

## Thola

1. Cindezela {{key:Mod+F}}, noma vula {{ui:Thola umbhalo}}.
2. Thayipha amagama, njengo `hood` noma `verify in field`.
3. Khetha {{ui:Wonke amashidi}} noma {{ui:Leli shidi}}, bese uthikitha {{ui:Linganisa ukukhulu nokuncane}} noma {{ui:Amagama aphelele}} uma udinga.
4. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} noma khetha {{ui:Thola}}.

Umphumela: wonke umphumela uhlwini ngenombolo yeshidi wawo namagama aseduze, futhi umakwa ngophuzi eshidini lawo. Khetha umphumela ukuze uvule ishidi lawo usondele kuwo.

Ibinzana litholakala ngisho noma umdwebo wayihlukanisa phakathi kwezingcezu ezimbili zombhalo noma imigqa emibili.

## Faka ifu konke

Khetha {{ui:Faka ifu konke}}. Ifu lokuguqulwa lihamba lizungeze wonke umphumela ohlwini, kuwo wonke amashidi, nekhomenti ethi "Kutholakele: <amagama akho>". Ukucindezela {{key:Mod+Z}} kukhipha konke.

## Funda umbhalo esikeniwe (OCR)

Ishidi eskeniwe liwumfanekiso nje: uze amagama alo afundwe, akukho okungatholakala. Uma inani lithi amashidi awanambhalo, khetha {{ui:Funda umbhalo (OCR)…}} ngaphansi kwawo, noma {{ui:Amakhasi}} › {{ui:Funda umbhalo (OCR)…}}.

1. Khetha amakhasi: leli shidi, wonke amakhasi efayela, noma wonke amakhasi wawo wonke amafayela avuliwe.
2. Khetha ulimi lombhalo. {{ui:Futhi isiNgisi}} ifunda namagama esiNgisi futhi.
3. Khetha {{ui:Funda umbhalo}}. Ibha ibonisa ikhasi elifundwayo; {{ui:Misa}} iyeka ukusebenza ibe igcina amakhasi asefundiwe.

Umphumela: amagama angatholakala, akhethwe, akopishwe njengombhalo wanoma yimuphi omunye. Konke kusebenza ekhompyutheni yakho: akukho okuthunyelwa noma kuphi. Kusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhumeka ku-Studio, nasebhrawuzeni uma uhlelo selulondolozelwe ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi (isiNgisi nolimi lwakho lwesixhumi) noma ulimi selusetshenziswe kanye. Amakhasi anombhalo asala njengoba ekhona.

Lapho ulondoloza, amagama angena ku-PDF njengombhalo ongabonakali ngemuva komfanekiso, ngaphandle kwezimaki zakho, ukuze nezinye izinhlelo ze-PDF zikwazi ukuwasesha nokuwakopisha. Uze ulondoloze, ifayela libonakala njengelingalondolozwanga.

## Khetha futhi kopisha umbhalo

Khetha {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Khetha umbhalo ukuze ukopishe}}. Hudula kusukela kwelinye igama uye kwelinye ukuze ukhethe amagama aphakathi kwawo, hudula kusukela endaweni engenalutho ephepheni ukuze ukhethe amagama abekwe kubhokisi, noma chofoza kabili igama elilodwa. {{key:Mod+A}} ikhetha wonke amagama eshidini. {{key:Mod+C}} iwakopisha, igcine imigqa. Noma yiliphi elinye ithuluzi liyiphelisa.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Imidwebo lapho izinhlamvu zidwetshwe njengemigqa nayo ayinambhalo; ukuyifunda njengomfanekiso kusebenza kahle kakhulu kumbhalo ophrintiwe njengencwadi eskeniwe noma inkulumo-mbhalo.
- Umfundi ngu-Tesseract, ofakwe ku-app ngedatha yolimi lwesiNgisi nezilimi ze-app. Ukusetshenziswa kokuqala kuyayilayisha (cishe ngama-MB ayi-6, ngaphezulu uma kukhona ulimi lwesibili).
- Ukuthi ufunda kahle kangakanani kuncike eskenini: amakhasi ahlanzekile ku-300 dpi afunda cishe wonke amagama; izikena ezindala, ezibukeka kancane noma ezinokuqhathanisa okuphansi zishiya amagama amaningi.
- Uhlu lubonisa imiphumela yokuqala engu-500; Faka ifu konke lufaka ifu kuze kufike kwezingu-2,000.
- Imiphumela iyasulwa uma uvala isethi, nakufayela elilodwa uma ushintsha amakhasawo.
