## Vula isethi

1. Khetha {{ui:Vula}} kubha yamathuluzi, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}.
2. Esikhethini sefayela, khetha wonke ama-PDF esethi ({{pick:mac=bamba ⌘ bese uchofoza ifayela ngalinye|windows linux=bamba Ctrl bese uchofoza ifayela ngalinye}}, noma khetha uhla nge-Shift), bese uqinisekisa.
   Noma hudula amafayela avela ku-{{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=umphathi wakho wamafayela}} aye endaweni yeshidi. "Lahla ama-PDF ukuze uwavule" ivela lapho uhudula.
3. Linda ngenkathi ibha yesimo ithi "Iyavula <file>…". Ezifayeleni ezinkulu iyabala: "(10 kwangu-60 amashidi)".

Umphumela: uhlu lwe-{{ui:Amashidi}} lubonisa wonke amakhasi wawo wonke amafayela, ngokulandelana owavula ngakho, futhi ibha yesimo ithi okufana no "Kuvulwe amafayela angu-3 (amashidi angu-42 kwesethi)." Uma amafayela ayenamakhomenti ngaphambili, ibala nokuthi angaki afundiwe.

Ukuze wengeze amanye amafayela kamuva, vula ngendlela efanayo. Ayengezwa ekugcineni kohlu.

## Qala ngamakhasi amhlophe

Ukuze udweba noma ubhale amanothi ngaphandle kwedrowingi, yenza amakhasi amhlophe esikhundleni sokuvula i-PDF:

1. Khetha {{ui:Okusha}} kubha yamathuluzi, noma {{ui:Qala ngekhasi elimhlophe}} esikrinini sokuqala, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+N}} uma isiphequluli sakho sivumela ikhasi ukusebenzisa lokho (uhlelo lwedeskithophu ne-Studio zivumela njalo).
2. Khetha {{ui:Usayizi wephepha}}: Letter, Tabloid noma ANSI B, ARCH D, ARCH E, A4, A3 noma A1, bese {{ui:Okumile}} noma {{ui:Okulele}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:Igridi}}: {{ui:Akukho}}, {{ui:Amachashazi}} noma {{ui:Izikwele}}. Igridi iba yi-1/4 in ngalinye, nomugqa omkhulu njalo nge-in ngayinye ephepheni le-inch, futhi njalo nge-5 mm, nomugqa omkhulu njalo nge-10 mm ephepheni le-metric. Iphrintwa ekhasini.
4. Thayipha inani {{ui:Amakhasi}} ofuna, kusukela ku-1 kuya ku-20, bese ukhetha {{ui:Dala}}.

Umphumela: i-PDF entsha egcinwe njenge-"Untitled-1.pdf" (bese "Untitled-2.pdf", njalo) ivulwa njengamanye amafayela, yenziwe lapha kulo mshini. Zonke amathuluzi asebenza kuyo, futhi {{ui:Londoloza}} ilanda "Untitled-1 (marked up).pdf". MarkupCraft ikhumbula okukhethile kwakho ngesikhathi esilandelayo.

Amakhasi awanaso isikali: ibha yesimo ithi "Akulinganiswanga". Igridi akuyona isikali. Ukuze ulinganise, linganisa ishidi noma khetha isikali kuqala; bona [Linganisa nokulinganisa](topic:calibrate-and-measure).

Ukuze wengeze ikhasi esethini emhlophe, vula elinye lamashidi alo bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza ikhasi elimhlophe}} ngaphansi kohlu lwe-{{ui:Amashidi}}. Ikhasi elisha linephepha negridi efanayo, futhi liya ekugcineni. Njengakho konke ukushintsha kwamakhasi, alikwazi ukuhlehliswa; bona [Phrinta nokushintsha amakhasi](topic:pages-and-printing).

## Ukuqanjwa kwamashidi

MarkupCraft iqamba ishidi ngalinye ngokuthi "<inombolo yeshidi> — <isihloko>", isibonelo "A-101 — Ipulani lomgangatho", ngokusekelwe kokokuqala okulandelayo okutholakala:

1. Igama olithayiphe nge-{{ui:Qamba kabusha ishidi}} (ngezansi).
2. Amalebula amakhasi e-PDF, uma umuntu owenza i-PDF ewasetha (isibonelo "A-101").
3. Ibhlokhi yesihloko: MarkupCraft ifunda umbhalo wekhasi bese ifuna into efana nenombolo yeshidi (A-101, A1.01, M-201A, KA1-1), ikhethe ikhoneli elingezansi kwesokudla, ustrip wesokudla nobhande langezansi, bese ithatha umbhalo omkhulu eduze kwayo njengesihloko.
4. Uma kungenjalo, igama lefayela nekhasi: "Amapulani — ikhasi 3".

Ukubona ukuthi yisiphi esasetshenziswa, cindezela {{key:Esc}} kuze kungakhethwa lutho futhi {{ui:Khetha}} kuvulekile, bese ubheka e-{{ui:Izici}}: "Kufundwe kusuka ku" ithi "Ilebula yekhasi", "Ibhlokhi yesihloko", "Igama lefayela" noma "Kuthayiphwe ngesandla".

:::note
Amashidi askeniwe, namapulani e-PDF lapho imibhalo ehlanjululwe ibe imigqa, awanalo umbhalo wokufunda. Aqanjwa ngefayela nekhasi kuze kube yilapho uwaqamba kabusha. MarkupCraft ayikwazi ukufunda umbhalo kwizithombe okwamanje.
:::

## Hamba phakathi kwamashidi

- Chofoza ishidi ku-{{ui:Amashidi}}.
- Cindezela {{key:PageDown}} eshidini elilandelayo no-{{key:PageUp}} eliphambili, noma sebenzisa izimpini ebhalweni ngaphansi kweshidi.
- Thayipha ku-{{ui:Sesha amashidi}}: ingxenye yenombolo ("A-2"), igama lesihloko ("uphahla"), noma igama lefayela. Uhlu luyancipha uma uthayipha ("4 kwangu-42"). Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uvule okokuqala okutholakele. Sula ibhokisi ukuze ubone wonke amashidi futhi.

Ishidi ngalinye ligcina ukusondeza kwalo nendawo yalo kuze kube yilapho uvala isethi.

## Sondeza nokushelelisa

:::mouse
- **Sondeza:** phendula iwhili phezu kweshidi. Lisondeza ngokuzungeze isikhombi. Noma cindezela {{key:Plus}} no-{{key:Minus}} (nakuma {{key:Mod}}), noma sebenzisa izinkinobho zokusondeza. Thayipha iphesenti elithile ebhokisini lokusondeza.
- **Lwanela:** {{ui:Lwanela ikhasi}} ({{key:Mod+0}}) ibonisa ishidi lonke; {{ui:Lwanela ububanzi}} ({{key:Mod+9}}) igcwalisa ububanzi.
- **Shelelisa:** hudula ngenkinobho ephakathi (iwhili), noma hudula ngenkinobho yesokudla, noma bamba {{key:Space}} uhudule, noma khetha {{ui:Shelelisa}} ({{key:H}}) uhudule. Lapho kungakhethiwe lutho, izinkinobho zomcibisholo nazo zihambisa ishidi.
:::

:::mac mouse
Ku-trackpad ye-Mac, ukuskrola ngeminwe emibili kusondeza (akuskroli ishidi). Ukushelelisa, bamba {{key:Space}} uhudule ngomunwe owodwa, noma shintsha ku-{{ui:Shelelisa}} nge-{{key:H}}. Ukuhlanganisa ngeminwe kusondeza ku-Chrome, Edge ne-Firefox.
:::

:::touch
- **Sondeza nokushelelisa:** beka iminwe emibili eshidini. Yandisa noma uhlanganise ukuze usondeze ngokuzungeze indawo phakathi kwazo, bese uyihambisa ndawonye ukushelelisa. Kokubili kwenzeka ngesikhathi esisodwa.
- **Shelelisa ngomunwe owodwa:** khetha {{ui:Shelelisa}} kuqala. Nganoma yiliphi elinye ithuluzi, umunwe owodwa udweba noma ukhethe.
- {{ui:Lwanela ikhasi}} no-{{ui:Lwanela ububanzi}} sebebhalweni ngaphansi kweshidi.
:::

Ungasondeza ngaphandle ngaphezu kweshidi lonke futhi ungene ngaphakathi kuze kube ngu-6400%. Ishidi lenziwa kabusha lisobala umzuzwana ngemva kokuthi uyeke ukuhambisa.

## Izimpawu

{{ui:Izimpawu}}, phezulu kwepaneli ye-{{ui:Amashidi}}, ibonisa izimpawu (isakhiwo) se-PDF lapho kuvulwe khona ishidi elivulekile. Khetha enye ukuze uye ekhasini layo. Ipensela liqamba kabusha isimpawu bese isiphambano siyasisusa. {{ui:Yakha kumashidi}} yenza isimpawu esisodwa ngeshidi ngalinye, siqanjwe njengoba uhlu lweshidi lukubonisa (inombolo yeshidi nesihloko), futhi {{ui:Engeza kuleshidi}} yengeza eyodwa yeshidi osebenza kulo. Izinguquko ziyingxenye yomsebenzi ongakagcinwa wefayela: {{ui:Londoloza}} uzibhala kukhophi, lapho zonke izibukisi ze-PDF zizibonisa khona.

## Phendula umbono

Izinkinobho ezimbili zokuphendula ngakwesokudla kwebha ngaphansi kweshidi zizungezisa umbono ngekota ngakunoma iyiphi indlela, ngeshidi elibekwe ngaseceleni. Kuphendula umbono kuphela: i-PDF namamaki ahlala njengoba enjalo, futhi ishidi elilandelayo livula ngendlela i-PDF yalo ivulwa ngayo (ishidi ngalinye likhumbula ukuphendula kwalo ngenkathi isethi ivulekile). Umbhalo owengeze embonweni ophenduliwe ufunda uqondile njengoba ubona. Ukuphendula ikhasi ngokwalo ku-PDF elondoloziwe, sebenzisa {{ui:Amakhasi}}, bese {{ui:Zungezisa ikhasi ngakwesokudla}}; bona [Phrinta nokushintsha amakhasi](topic:pages-and-printing).

## Qamba kabusha ishidi

Uma igama leshidi lingalungile noma lingekho:

1. Bonisa ishidi.
2. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} kuze kungakhethwa lutho futhi ithuluzi {{ui:Khetha}} livuliwe. Manje {{ui:Izici}} zibonisa ishidi.
3. Khetha {{ui:Qamba kabusha ishidi}}.
4. Thayipha {{ui:Inombolo yeshidi}} ne-{{ui:Isihloko}}, bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza}}.

Umphumela: uhlu lwamashidi, ibha ngaphansi kweshidi nohlu lwamamaki konke kusebenzisa igama elisha, futhi "Kufundwe kusuka ku" ithi "Kuthayiphwe ngesandla". Ukuqamba kabusha kungahlehliswa nge-{{key:Mod+Z}}.

Ukuze ubuyele kulokho ifayela elikushoyo, vula futhi {{ui:Qamba kabusha ishidi}}, sula amabhokisi amabili, bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza}}.

Uma ulondoloza, inombolo yeshidi othayiphile iba yilebula yekhasi ku-PDF, ngakho nabanye ababoni be-PDF babonisa "A-101" ebhokisini labo lekhasi nabo. Uma unikeza noma iliphi ikhasi lefayela inombolo, wonke amakhasi alelo fayela athola ilebula ekhophini (inombolo oyithayiphile, uma kungenjalo ilebula yakudala, uma kungenjalo inombolo yekhasi). Isihloko othayiphile siyagcinwa kwi-MarkupCraft kodwa abanye ababoni abasibonisi.

## Thatha i-PDF ku-TakeoffCraft, noma uyilethe lapha

Uma i-TakeoffCraft ivuliwe endaweni efanayo (ilebhu, Try noma ama-app ku-inthanethi), ibha ngaphansi kweshidi inento ethi {{ui:Vula ku-TakeoffCraft}}. Ivula i-PDF yeshidi ku-TakeoffCraft ethebhini entsha, njengoba ivulwe lapha, ukuze ulinganise izinto kuyo. Amamaki akho ahlala lapha; wagcine njengoba ujwayele.

Inkinobho ye-{{ui:MarkupCraft}} ye-TakeoffCraft yenza okufanayo ngendlela enye: i-PDF ivula lapha njengokuthi uyikhethe {{ui:Vula}}. Uma isiphequluli sivimba ithebhu entsha, vumela ama-pop-up kule sayithi bese ukhetha inkinobho futhi.
