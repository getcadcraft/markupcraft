Amathuluzi okuqhathanisa abonisa izinguquko ebhodini. Lona lubhala zona phansi.

1. Vula zombili izinguqulo (bona [Vula isethi yamapulani](topic:open-a-plan-set)).
2. Vula ithebhu {{ui:Qhathanisa}}, bese {{ui:Qhathanisa isethi yonke}}.
3. Khetha ifayela langaphambili nelangemva, bese ukhetha {{ui:Bhala uhlu lwezinguquko}}.

Uhlu luqala ngenani (amashidi ayi-2 kwayi-14 ahlanganisiwe ashintshile; 1 entsha; 1 esusiwe) bese luhlanganisa ishidi ngalinye elishintshile, eliguquke kakhulu kuqala: indawo esheetini (phezulu kwesobunxele, phakathi…), ubukhulu bayo, ukuthi imigqa yengezwe, yasuswa noma kokubili, okungaki okumakiwe okukuwo, nokuthi yimaphi amagama avela noma anyamalala. Ezishidini ezilinganisiwe usayizi ubonakala ngamayunithi angempela kanye namainchi asephepheni.

Uhlu lubonisa okusephepheni; alusho ukuthi kusho ini. Umugqa odwetshiwe owushintshile ungaba udonga noma umugqa wokuxhuma. Umsizi wakho oxhunyiwe ungafunda uhlu ngethuluzi `revisions_changes` aluchaze ngamagama, nawe ungabheka ishidi ukuze uthole okunye. {{ui:Kopisha uhlu}} ilibeka ebhodini lokukopisha, ne-{{ui:CSV}} ibhala umugqa oyedwa ngokushintsha ngakunye.

Amashidi ahlanganiswa ngenombolo yeshidi, njengakuthi [Faka ishidi lokubuyekeza](topic:slip-sheet-a-revised-set). Izinguquko ezincane kunamaphoyinti ambalwa ziyashaywa, nombhalo ongxenye yesithombe (isikena) awunawo amagama okuqhathanisa.
