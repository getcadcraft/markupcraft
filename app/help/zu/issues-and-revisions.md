## Okuzoyenza

Sebenza ngomlando ofanayo wezinkinga njengethimba lonke. Bona ukuthi yimaphi amashidi aphumela ngoguqulo luni, futhi ugcine okwenzeka esayithini ngokumelene nenkinga okuyeyayo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula **Izinkinga nokuguqulwa**.
2. Khetha **Funda umlando wezinkinga** bese ukhetha ifayela `.issues.json` elilondolozwe ku-BimCraft noma kwenye i-app. Izinkinga namarekhodi ayo zingezwa kwawakho. Akukho okushintshwayo.
3. Khetha **Engeza irekhodi** lemiyalo yesayithi, umdwebo wokwakha, isethi yamaki, imodeli exhunyiwe noma inothi. Khetha inkinga ehambisana nayo nesimo sayo. Ifayela oliKhethayo lirekhodwa ngegama lalo, usayizi walo ne-SHA-256; ifayela lisala lapho likhona.
4. **Incwadi yoshintsho lwemidwebo** ibonisa umugqa owodwa ngeshidi ngalinye nekholomu eyodwa ngenkinga ngayinye. Ukuguqulwa okusemaphakathi kwamabakala kukhishwe futhi kungashintshiwe.
5. **Landa ushintsho (CSV)** iyalondoloza izinkinga, ithebula lamashidi namarekhodi. **Londoloza umlando wezinkinga** ibhala ifayela okufundwa ngalo ezinye izinhlelo.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- **Rekhoda izimaki ezikhethiwe** ibeka izimaki ozikhethile ngokumelene nenkinga yakamuva. Ifu lokuguqulwa lirekhodwa njengefu lokuguqulwa ngoguqulo lwaleyo nkinga. Khetha **Bonisa** eceleni kwerekhodi ukuze uyofika kulo.
- **Rekhoda inkinga** ikhipha amashidi akho anezimaki. **Okushintshiwe kusukela** bese kutshela ukuthi yimaphi amashidi ahlelwa ngemva kwalowo mkhankaso.
- Umlando ulondolozwa ngaphakathi kwe-PDF enezimaki. Awuyingxenye yokuhlehlisa.
- Ukufunda ifayela elifanayo kabili akungeziwe kabili. Uma amakhophi amabili enerekhodi elifanayo, elisha kakhulu liyagcinwa.
