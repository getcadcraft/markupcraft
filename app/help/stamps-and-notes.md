## Stamp a sheet

1. Choose {{ui:Stamp}} ({{key:S}}).
2. In {{ui:Properties}} on the right, pick the stamp under {{ui:Stamp}}:
   - Approved
   - Approved as noted
   - Revise and resubmit
   - Rejected
   - Reviewed
   - Void
   - Custom text… (type up to 60 characters in the box that appears, then press {{key:Enter}} or click elsewhere)
3. Click on the sheet where the stamp goes. Or drag a box to choose its size.

Result: a framed stamp in capitals with a second line "By <your name>, <date>". A clicked stamp is sized for the screen at your zoom, kept between half an inch and an inch and a quarter tall on the printed sheet.

The stamp you picked stays chosen for the next one, also after you reopen MarkupCraft in this browser.

**Colors follow the meaning:** the Approved stamps are green, Revise and resubmit orange, Rejected and Void red, Reviewed blue, and custom stamps purple. If you choose another color in {{ui:Properties}}, that color is kept.

### Change a stamp later

Select it and pick another stamp in {{ui:Properties}}. If it still had its automatic color, the color changes with the meaning.

The name and date on the stamp are the stamp's author and the day it was made. They do not change when you edit it, and a stamp's author cannot be changed. To stamp in someone else's name, they set their name and stamp it themselves.

:::note
The date on a stamp is the date in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Late in the evening in the Americas, or early in the morning in Asia and Australia, it can show the next or the previous day compared with your clock. The created time in {{ui:Properties}} is in your own time zone.
:::

### Stamps you use often

The tool chest comes with an "Approved as noted" stamp tool, and you can save any stamp (its wording and color) as a tool of its own. See [The tool chest](topic:tool-chest).

Image stamps, signature stamps and stamps with filled-in fields (date received, project number) are not built yet.

## Stamp a picture or your signature

The stamp list in {{ui:Properties}} ends with two more choices:

- {{ui:Picture…}} asks for a PNG or JPEG on this computer, such as a company seal or a logo. Large pictures are scaled down to 1600 pixels on their longer side.
- {{ui:My signature…}} opens a signature box: sign with a mouse, a pen or a finger, and choose {{ui:Use this signature}}. {{ui:Clear}} starts again.

Then click the sheet to place it (a picture 3 in wide, a signature 2 in wide), or drag a box to fit it inside; the proportions are kept. Resize it with its handles like any markup ({{key:Shift}} keeps the proportions).

The last picture and your signature are kept in this browser, so they are ready next time; {{ui:Choose another picture…}} and {{ui:Sign again…}} replace them. See [What stays in this browser](topic:browser-storage).

In the saved PDF a picture is a stamp comment showing the image, so any PDF viewer shows and prints it. A signature drawn here is a picture of your signature, not a certified digital signature.

## Paste a picture from the clipboard

Copy a picture in another program, or take a screenshot to the clipboard, then press {{key:Mod+V}} over the sheet. It lands where the pointer is, at most 3 in wide or tall with its proportions kept, as a picture markup you can move and resize. In the saved PDF it is a stamp comment showing the image, like the pictures above.

{{ui:More}} › {{ui:Paste a picture}} in the {{ui:Edit}} group does the same from the menu; the browser may ask first. When the clipboard holds no picture, {{key:Mod+V}} pastes copied markups as before.

## Sticky notes

1. Choose {{ui:Note}} ({{key:N}}).
2. Click where the note goes. A small note icon appears with a box beside it.
3. Type the note. Click outside or press {{key:Mod+Enter}} to keep it; {{key:Esc}} throws it away.

Unlike text and callouts, a note is kept even if you type nothing, so you can place the icon now and write later.

- The note's words are its {{ui:Note}} field in {{ui:Properties}}. Double-click the icon (or select it and press {{key:Enter}}) to edit them on the sheet.
- On the sheet you see only the icon. Other PDF viewers show a standard sticky note that opens to the text when clicked.
- Change the icon's color with {{ui:Color}} in {{ui:Properties}}.
