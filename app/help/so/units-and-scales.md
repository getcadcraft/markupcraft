## Qorista dherer

Markaad hagaajinayso laba dhibcood, waxaad qortaa masaafada dhabta ah. MarkupCraft wuxuu akhrinayaa dhammaan kuwan:

| Waxaad qortaa | Macnaheedu waa |
|---|---|
| `24'-0"` | 24 fut |
| `12' 6 1/2"` ama `12'-6 1/2"` | 12 fut iyo 6½ inji |
| `12' 6` | 12 fut iyo 6 inji (calaamadda inta la inji ka reebay) |
| `6"` ama `1/2"` | inji |
| `12.5'` ama `12.5 ft` | 12½ fut |
| `3 ft 6 in` | 3 fut iyo 6 inji (sidoo kale feet, foot, inch, inches) |
| `3.5 m`, `90 cm`, `900 mm` | metrik (sidoo kale meter, metre, millimeter, centimetre…) |
| `24` | tiro bannaan, cutubka lagu doortay {{ui:Muuji dherarka oo ku jira}} |

Qoraallada gobolka (’ ”) iyo laba apostrophe ('') oo inji ah ayaa sidoo kale shaqeeya. Dherer eber iyo kuwa taban waa la diidaa.

:::warning
Calaamadda comma waxaa loo akhriyaa sida dhibic tobaan: `1,5 m` waa 1.5 mitir. Ha qorin kala-soocidda kumaankiiska: `1,200 mm` waxaa loo akhriyaa 1.2 millimitir. Ku qor `1200 mm`.
:::

## Sida dherarka iyo aagga loo muujiyo

| {{ui:Muuji dherarka oo ku jira}} | Dherer wuxuu u muuqdaa sida | Aag wuxuu u muuqdaa sida |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Fut iyo inji}} | `12'-6 1/2"`, wareegsan 1/16" | fut laba-jibbaaran, labo meelood oo tobaan |
| {{ui:Fiit (tobaan)}} | `12.54'`, labo meelood oo tobaan | fut laba-jibbaaran, labo meelood oo tobaan |
| {{ui:Inji}} | `150.5"`, ilaa labo meelood oo tobaan | inji laba-jibbaaran, hal meel oo tobaan |
| {{ui:Mitir}} | `3.820 m`, saddex meel oo tobaan | mitir laba-jibbaaran, saddex meel oo tobaan |
| {{ui:Millimitir}} | `3820 mm`, millimitir oo dhan | millimitir laba-jibbaaran, oo dhan |

Wareegga aagga wuxuu isticmaalaa qaabka dhererka.

## Miisaanada la dejiyay

Dejintu waxay malaynaysaa in PDF-ku yahay cabirkiisa buuxa ee la daabacay: 72 PDF point inji kasta, sida barnaamijka CAD uu u sawiro.

| Kooxda | Miisaanada | Dherarka lagu muujiyo |
|---|---|---|
| Dhismaha | 1/32" = 1'-0", 1/16", 3/32", 1/8", 3/16", 1/4", 3/8", 1/2", 3/4", 1", 1 1/2", 3" = 1'-0" | Fut iyo inji |
| Injineernimo | 1" = 10', 20', 30', 40', 50', 60', 100' | Fiit (tobaan) |
| Metrik | 1:1, 1:5, 1:10, 1:20, 1:25, 1:50 | Millimitir |
| Metrik | 1:100, 1:200, 1:250, 1:500, 1:1000 | Mitir |

Si aad u isticmaasho miisaan la dejiyay laakiin dherarka aad ugu aragto cutub kale, ku hagaaji laba dhibcood halkii, oo dooro cutubka halkaas.

## Miisaanada barnaamijyada kale kaydiyay

Markii PDF horay u sidday miisaan bog kasta oo barnaamij kale ah (miisaan cabbir oo lagu kaydiyay bogga), MarkupCraft wuu isticmaalaa haddii ay ku jirto fut, inji, mitir, sentimitir ama millimitir. Calaamadeeda waxaa lagu muujiyaa sida faylku sheegayo.

## Booska warqadda

Bar xaaladdu wuxuu muujiyaa halka taabiyuhu ku yaal warqadda, inji ka fog geeska bidix ee sare ee xaashida, miisaan kasta oo xaashidu leedahay. Compare's nudge steps waa point (72 point inji kasta).

## Saxnaanta

Cabbirku wuxuu ka wanaagsan yahay hagaajinta iyo guuskaaga. Hagaaji dhererka ugu dheer ee aad heli karto, zoom-garee si aad gees-yadiisa u guuto, kadibna hubi mid kale oo cabbir ah. PDF-ka la miisaamay markii la sameeyay (plot "fit to page" ama set nus-cabbir ah) weli waa sax markaad ka hagaajiso cabbir, laakiin ma aha miisaan la dejiyay.
