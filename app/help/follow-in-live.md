In a Live session you can make your view follow a colleague's, for example a teacher walking a class through a sheet. This page is about following; starting and joining the session is done in CadCraft Studio, see [Review one plan set together with Live TeamWork](topic:work-live).

## What you'll do

Follow one person, stop following, and (as host) ask everyone to follow you.

## Steps

1. In a Live session, open the list of people. It shows each person's colour and name.
2. Choose {{ui:Follow}} beside the name of the person to follow. The status line reads {{ui:Following}} and their name, and your view moves with theirs.
3. To stop, choose {{ui:Stop following}}, or just pan or zoom your own view: following stops by itself.
4. As host, choose {{ui:Ask everyone to follow me}} to ask the whole group to follow you. Choose {{ui:Stop asking everyone to follow me}} to end it.

## Good to know

- Each person shows the sheet they are on and when their last edit was, so a teacher can see who has made progress.
- If the person you follow is on a view that cannot be followed, or on another sheet, the status line says where they are instead.
- Following changes only your view. Nothing is saved in the file.
- The class view has not yet been tried with a real teacher and students.
